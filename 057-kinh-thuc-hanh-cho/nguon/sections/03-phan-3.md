## mn57:13.5
- **PLI**: Labheyyāhaṁ, bhante, bhagavato santike pabbajjaṁ, labheyyaṁ upasampadan”ti.
- **EN**: Sir, may I receive the going forth, the ordination in the Buddha’s presence?”
- **NOTE**: In the early period, there was no distinction between “going forth” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>pabbajjā</i>) and “ordination” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>upasampadā</i>). They refer to two sides of the same coin: leaving the home life and entering the ascetic life. Ordination was originally granted by the Buddha with the simple call, “Come mendicant!” (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-kd1/en/sujato#6.32.1'>Kd 1:6.32.1</a>). As the community grew, the Buddha authorized the Saṅgha to perform the “going forth and ordination” (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-kd1/en/sujato#12.1.1'>Kd 1:12.1.1</a>). Later still, the novice (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>sāmaṇera</i>) platform was introduced for teenagers and became known as “going forth” (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-kd1/en/sujato#54.3.2'>Kd 1:54.3.2</a>). Finally, the novice ordination was standardized for all ordinands, even if it was a mere preliminary for the “full ordination”.

## mn57:14.1  `nya14`
- **PLI**: “Yo kho, seniya, aññatitthiyapubbo imasmiṁ dhammavinaye ākaṅkhati pabbajjaṁ, ākaṅkhati upasampadaṁ so cattāro māse parivasati. Catunnaṁ māsānaṁ accayena āraddhacittā bhikkhū pabbājenti, upasampādenti bhikkhubhāvāya.
- **EN**: “Seniya, if someone formerly ordained in another sect wishes to take the going forth, the ordination in this teaching and training, they must spend four months on probation. When four months have passed, if the mendicants are satisfied, they’ll give the going forth, the ordination into monkhood.
- **NOTE**: This probation is laid down in the Vinaya at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-kd1/en/sujato#38.1.5'>Kd 1:38.1.5</a>. The candidate shaves, dons the robes, takes refuge, and asks for probation. They must show good conduct and restraint, diligence in duties, and enthusiasm for the Buddha’s teachings and practice.

## mn57:14.2
- **PLI**: Api ca mettha puggalavemattatā viditā”ti.
- **EN**: However, I recognize individual differences in this matter.”
- **NOTE**: In addition to individual exceptions, there are general exceptions for dreadlocked fire-worshipping ascetics, since they believe in kamma, and for the Buddha’s relatives (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-kd1/en/sujato#38.11.3'>Kd 1:38.11.3</a>).

## mn57:14.3  `cck13.90, ms10M_221`
- **PLI**: “Sace, bhante, aññatitthiyapubbā imasmiṁ dhammavinaye ākaṅkhantā pabbajjaṁ ākaṅkhantā upasampadaṁ te cattāro māse parivasanti catunnaṁ māsānaṁ accayena āraddhacittā bhikkhū pabbājenti upasampādenti bhikkhubhāvāya, ahaṁ cattāri vassāni parivasissāmi catunnaṁ vassānaṁ accayena āraddhacittā bhikkhū pabbājentu, upasampādentu bhikkhubhāvāyā”ti.
- **EN**: “Sir, if four months probation are required in such a case, I’ll spend four years on probation. When four years have passed, if the mendicants are satisfied, let them give me the going forth, the ordination into monkhood.”

## mn57:15.2
- **PLI**: Acirūpasampanno kho panāyasmā seniyo eko vūpakaṭṭho appamatto ātāpī pahitatto viharanto nacirasseva—yassatthāya kulaputtā sammadeva agārasmā anagāriyaṁ pabbajanti, Tadanuttaraṁ—brahmacariyapariyosānaṁ diṭṭheva dhamme sayaṁ abhiññā sacchikatvā upasampajja vihāsi.
- **EN**: Not long after his ordination, Venerable Seniya, living alone, withdrawn, diligent, keen, and resolute, soon realized the supreme culmination of the spiritual path in this very life. He lived having achieved with his own insight the goal for which gentlemen rightly go forth from the lay life to homelessness.
