## mn57:7.5
- **PLI**: atthi, puṇṇa, kammaṁ kaṇhasukkaṁ kaṇhasukkavipākaṁ;
- **EN**: dark and bright deeds with dark and bright results; and

## mn57:7.6

- **EN**: neither dark nor bright deeds with neither dark nor bright results, which lead to the ending of deeds.
- **NOTE**: These are the intentions associated with the noble eightfold path.
