## mn1:75.1  `bj10.14, csp1ed9.6, csp2ed9.6, ms9M_34, msdiv9, ndp9.8, nya75, pts-vp-pli1.5`
- **PLI**: Yopi so, bhikkhave, bhikkhu arahaṁ khīṇāsavo vusitavā katakaraṇīyo ohitabhāro anuppattasadattho parikkhīṇabhavasaṁyojano sammadaññā vimutto, sopi pathaviṁ pathavito abhijānāti;
- **EN**: A mendicant who is perfected—with defilements ended, who has completed the spiritual journey, done what had to be done, laid down the burden, achieved their heart’s goal, utterly ended the fetter of continued existence, and is rightly freed through enlightenment—directly knows earth as earth.

## mn1:75.2

- **EN**: Having directly known earth as earth, they do not conceive it to be earth, they do not conceive it in earth, they do not conceive it as earth, they do not conceive that ‘earth is mine’, they do not approve earth.

## mn1:75.4
- **PLI**: Khayā rāgassa, vītarāgattā.
- **EN**: Because they’re free of greed due to the ending of greed.
- **NOTE**: The text repeats the passage on the perfected one three times, emphasizing the ending of greed, hate, and delusion respectively.
