## mn1:147.1  `dr12.9, ms9M_43, msdiv12, nya147, pts-vp-pli1.6`
- **PLI**: Tathāgatopi, bhikkhave, arahaṁ sammāsambuddho pathaviṁ pathavito abhijānāti;
- **EN**: The Realized One, the perfected one, the fully awakened Buddha directly knows earth as earth.
- **NOTE**: The Buddha is an <i lang='pi' translate='no'>arahant</i>, and he shares his fundamental understanding with other arahants. Yet the suttas elevate his understanding as the one who discovered the path.

## mn1:147.2
- **PLI**: pathaviṁ pathavito abhiññāya pathaviṁ na maññati, pathaviyā na maññati, pathavito na maññati, pathaviṁ meti na maññati, pathaviṁ nābhinandati.
- **EN**: Having directly known earth as earth, he does not conceive it to be earth, he does not conceive it in earth, he does not conceive it as earth, he does not conceive that ‘earth is mine’, he does not approve earth.

## mn1:147.3
- **PLI**: Taṁ kissa hetu?
- **EN**: Why is that?

## mn1:147.4
- **PLI**: ‘Pariññātantaṁ tathāgatassā’ti vadāmi.
- **EN**: Because the Realized One has completely understood it to the end, I say.
- **NOTE**: To “completely understood to the end” is a phrase unique to this sutta. It implies that, while other arahants understand phenomena to the extent necessary for release, the Buddha understands all phenomena without exception.

## mn1:171.1  `csp1ed9.8, csp2ed9.8, ms9M_46, msdiv13, ndp9.10, nya171`
- **PLI**: Tathāgatopi, bhikkhave, arahaṁ sammāsambuddho pathaviṁ pathavito abhijānāti;
- **EN**: The Realized One, the perfected one, the fully awakened Buddha directly knows earth as earth.

## mn1:171.2
- **PLI**: pathaviṁ pathavito abhiññāya pathaviṁ na maññati, pathaviyā na maññati, pathavito na maññati, pathaviṁ meti na maññati, pathaviṁ nābhinandati.
- **EN**: Having directly known earth as earth, he does not conceive it to be earth, he does not conceive it in earth, he does not conceive it as earth, he does not conceive that ‘earth is mine’, he does not approve earth.

## mn1:171.3
- **PLI**: Taṁ kissa hetu?
- **EN**: Why is that?

## mn1:171.4
- **PLI**: ‘Nandī dukkhassa mūlan’ti—
- **EN**: Because he has understood that approval is the root of suffering,
- **NOTE**: This clarifies an ambiguity in the phrase “approve” (or “delights”, “relishes”, <i lang='pi' translate='no'>(abhi)-nandati</i>). This can have a positive sense, as the audience normally “approves” the Buddha’s teachings (but see the end of this sutta). Here, however, the Buddha clarifies that he is using “approve” in the sense of craving, as it is found in the standard definition of the second noble truth (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn56.11/en/sujato#4.4'>SN 56.11:4.4</a>).

## mn1:171.5
- **PLI**: iti viditvā ‘bhavā jāti bhūtassa jarāmaraṇan’ti.
- **EN**: and that rebirth comes from continued existence; whoever has come to be gets old and dies.
- **NOTE**: With these lines the Buddha connects the teachings of this sutta with dependent origination. He employs a similar strategy at the end of the Brahmajālasutta.

## mn1:171.6
- **PLI**: Tasmātiha, bhikkhave, ‘tathāgato sabbaso taṇhānaṁ khayā virāgā nirodhā cāgā paṭinissaggā anuttaraṁ sammāsambodhiṁ abhisambuddho’ti vadāmi.
- **EN**: That’s why the Realized One—with the ending, fading away, cessation, giving up, and letting go of all cravings—has awakened to the supreme perfect awakening, I say.
