# Phần 3  (8 đoạn)

## mn37:15.13
- **PLI**: paṭinissaggānupassī viharanto na kiñci loke upādiyati,
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)

## mn37:15.14
- **PLI**: anupādiyaṁ na paritassati, aparitassaṁ paccattaññeva parinibbāyati:
- **EN**: Not grasping, they’re not anxious. Not being anxious, they personally become extinguished.  (= mn37:13.9)

## mn37:15.15
- **PLI**: “khīṇā jāti, vusitaṁ brahmacariyaṁ, kataṁ karaṇīyaṁ, nāparaṁ itthattāyā”ti pajānāti.
- **EN**: They understand: “Rebirth is ended, the spiritual journey has been completed, what had to be done has been done, there is nothing further for this place.”  (= mn37:13.10)

## mn37:15.16
- **PLI**: Ettāvatā kho, devānaminda, bhikkhu saṅkhittena taṇhāsaṅkhayavimutto hoti accantaniṭṭho accantayogakkhemī accantabrahmacārī accantapariyosāno seṭṭho devamanussānan’ti.
- **EN**: That’s how I briefly define a mendicant who is freed through the ending of craving, who has reached the ultimate end, the ultimate sanctuary from the yoke, the ultimate spiritual life, the ultimate goal, and is best among gods and humans.’  (= mn37:13.11)

## mn37:15.17
- **PLI**: Evaṁ kho ahaṁ, moggallāna, abhijānāmi sakkassa devānamindassa saṅkhittena taṇhāsaṅkhayavimuttiṁ bhāsitā”ti.
- **EN**: “That’s how I recall briefly explaining freedom through the ending of craving to Sakka, lord of gods.”

## mn37:15.18  `ms9M_1280`
- **PLI**: Idamavoca bhagavā.
- **EN**: That is what the Buddha said.

## mn37:15.19
- **PLI**: Attamano āyasmā mahāmoggallāno bhagavato bhāsitaṁ abhinandīti.
- **EN**: Satisfied, Venerable Mahāmoggallāna approved what the Buddha said.

## mn37:15.20
- **PLI**: Cūḷataṇhāsaṅkhayasuttaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ sattamaṁ.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)
