## mn37:10.4
- **PLI**: “idampi, mārisa moggallāna, passa vejayantassa pāsādassa rāmaṇeyyakaṁ;

- **PLI**: idampi, mārisa moggallāna, passa vejayantassa pāsādassa rāmaṇeyyakan”ti.
- **EN**: And that lovely thing!”

## mn37:10.6
- **PLI**: “Sobhati idaṁ āyasmato kosiyassa, yathā taṁ pubbe katapuññassa.
- **EN**: “That looks beautiful for the venerable Kosiya, as befits one who has made merit in the past.
- **NOTE**: After Sakka’s disappointing response, Moggallāna is trying to say something nice. At the same time, he subtly implies the difference between his past merit and present negligence.

## mn37:10.8

- **EN**: ‘It looks beautiful enough for the gods of the thirty-three!’

## mn37:10.9
- **PLI**: Tayidaṁ āyasmato kosiyassa sobhati, yathā taṁ pubbe katapuññassā”ti.
- **EN**: That looks beautiful for the venerable Kosiya, as befits one who has made merit in the past.”
