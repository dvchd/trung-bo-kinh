## mn24:14.4
- **PLI**: Tassa antarā ca sāvatthiṁ antarā ca sāketaṁ satta rathavinītāni upaṭṭhapeyyuṁ.
- **EN**: Now, between Sāvatthī and Sāketa seven chariots were stationed at the ready for him.
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Rathavinīta</i>, which lends the sutta its title, is explained by the commentary as “seven chariots with horses at the ready”, where <i lang='pi' translate='no'>vinīta</i> has its usual sense of “trained, readied”. While the series of chariots forms a relay, the word <i lang='pi' translate='no'>vinīta</i> does not mean “relay”. The term and the simile are unique to this sutta.

## mn24:14.11

- **EN**: The seventh chariot at the ready would bring him to the gate of the royal compound of Sāketa.
- **NOTE**: Sāketa was one of the great cities (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn17/en/sujato#1.2.4'>DN 17:1.2.4</a>), a former capital of Kosala, and a regular stop on the road near Sāvatthī (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn44.1/en/sujato#1.2'>SN 44.1:1.2</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/snp5.1/en/sujato#37.1'>Snp 5.1:37.1</a>). Situated on the banks of the Sarabhū (modern Sarayu), it had several forests nearby where ascetics would stay. It is identified with the modern city of Ayodhya, about 95 km south of Sāvatthī. See also note on <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn22.95/en/sujato#1.1'>SN 22.95:1.1</a>. | If the distance traveled by each chariot was one “yoking” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>yojana</i>, “league”), this gives us a league of approximately 14 km.
