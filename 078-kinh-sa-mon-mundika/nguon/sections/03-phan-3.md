# Phần 3  (50 đoạn)

## mn78:11.6
- **PLI**: ‘Cittasamuṭṭhānā’tissa vacanīyaṁ.
- **EN**: You should say that they stem from the mind.

## mn78:11.7
- **PLI**: Katamaṁ cittaṁ?
- **EN**: What mind?

## mn78:11.8
- **PLI**: Cittampi hi bahuṁ anekavidhaṁ nānappakārakaṁ.
- **EN**: The mind takes many and diverse forms.

## mn78:11.9
- **PLI**: Yaṁ cittaṁ vītarāgaṁ vītadosaṁ vītamohaṁ, itosamuṭṭhānā kusalā sīlā.
- **EN**: But skillful behaviors stem from a mind that is free from greed, hate, and delusion.

## mn78:11.10  `ms10M_740`
- **PLI**: Ime ca, thapati, kusalā sīlā kuhiṁ aparisesā nirujjhanti?
- **EN**: And where do these skillful behaviors cease without remainder?

## mn78:11.11
- **PLI**: Nirodhopi nesaṁ vutto.
- **EN**: Their cessation has also been stated.

## mn78:11.12
- **PLI**: Idha, thapati, bhikkhu sīlavā hoti no ca sīlamayo,
- **EN**: It’s when a mendicant behaves ethically, but they are not determined by ethical behavior.
- **NOTE**: Taking the suffix <i lang='pi' translate='no'>-maya</i> in the same sense as <i lang='pi' translate='no'>tammaya</i>; see my note on <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn47/en/sujato#13.4'>MN 47:13.4</a>. The point is that they are not formed by and hence limited by the scope of ethics.

## mn78:11.13
- **PLI**: tañca cetovimuttiṁ paññāvimuttiṁ yathābhūtaṁ pajānāti; yatthassa te kusalā sīlā aparisesā nirujjhanti.
- **EN**: And they truly understand the freedom of heart and freedom by wisdom where these skillful behaviors cease without remainder.

## mn78:11.14  `csp1ed10.219, csp2ed10.219, dr13.321, ms10M_741, ndp10.252, sya13.349`
- **PLI**: Kathaṁ paṭipanno ca, thapati, kusalānaṁ sīlānaṁ nirodhāya paṭipanno hoti?
- **EN**: And how is someone practicing for the cessation of skillful behaviors?

## mn78:11.15
- **PLI**: Idha, thapati, bhikkhu anuppannānaṁ pāpakānaṁ akusalānaṁ dhammānaṁ anuppādāya chandaṁ janeti vāyamati vīriyaṁ ārabhati cittaṁ paggaṇhāti padahati;
- **EN**: It’s when a mendicant generates enthusiasm, tries, makes an effort, exerts the mind, and strives so that bad, unskillful qualities don’t arise …

## mn78:11.16
- **PLI**: uppannānaṁ pāpakānaṁ akusalānaṁ dhammānaṁ pahānāya …pe…
- **EN**: so that unskillful qualities are given up …

## mn78:11.17
- **PLI**: anuppannānaṁ kusalānaṁ dhammānaṁ uppādāya …pe…
- **EN**: so that skillful qualities arise …

## mn78:11.18
- **PLI**: uppannānaṁ kusalānaṁ dhammānaṁ ṭhitiyā asammosāya bhiyyobhāvāya vepullāya bhāvanāya pāripūriyā chandaṁ janeti vāyamati vīriyaṁ ārabhati cittaṁ paggaṇhāti padahati.
- **EN**: so that skillful qualities that have arisen remain, are not lost, but increase, mature, and are fully developed.

## mn78:11.19
- **PLI**: Evaṁ paṭipanno kho, thapati, kusalānaṁ sīlānaṁ nirodhāya paṭipanno hoti.
- **EN**: Someone practicing like this is practicing for the cessation of skillful behaviors.
