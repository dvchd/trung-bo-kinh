# Phần 4  (10 đoạn)

## mn78:13.17
- **PLI**: uppannānaṁ pāpakānaṁ akusalānaṁ dhammānaṁ pahānāya …pe…
- **EN**: so that unskillful qualities are given up …

## mn78:13.18
- **PLI**: anuppannānaṁ kusalānaṁ dhammānaṁ uppādāya …pe…
- **EN**: so that skillful qualities arise …

## mn78:13.19
- **PLI**: uppannānaṁ kusalānaṁ dhammānaṁ ṭhitiyā asammosāya bhiyyobhāvāya vepullāya bhāvanāya pāripūriyā chandaṁ janeti vāyamati vīriyaṁ ārabhati cittaṁ paggaṇhāti padahati.
- **EN**: so that skillful qualities that have arisen remain, are not lost, but increase, mature, and are fully developed.

## mn78:13.20
- **PLI**: Evaṁ paṭipanno kho, thapati, kusalānaṁ saṅkappānaṁ nirodhāya paṭipanno hoti.
- **EN**: Someone practicing like this is practicing for the cessation of skillful thoughts.

## mn78:14.1  `cck13.350, ms10M_750, msdiv268, ndp10.254, nya14, pts-vp-pli2.29`
- **PLI**: Katamehi cāhaṁ, thapati, dasahi dhammehi samannāgataṁ purisapuggalaṁ paññapemi sampannakusalaṁ paramakusalaṁ uttamapattipattaṁ samaṇaṁ ayojjhaṁ?
- **EN**: Chamberlain, when an individual person has what ten qualities do I describe them as an invincible ascetic—accomplished in the skillful, excelling in the skillful, attained to the highest attainment?

## mn78:14.2
- **PLI**: Idha, thapati, bhikkhu asekhāya sammādiṭṭhiyā samannāgato hoti, asekhena sammāsaṅkappena samannāgato hoti, asekhāya sammāvācāya samannāgato hoti, asekhena sammākammantena samannāgato hoti, asekhena sammāājīvena samannāgato hoti, asekhena sammāvāyāmena samannāgato hoti, asekhāya sammāsatiyā samannāgato hoti, asekhena sammāsamādhinā samannāgato hoti, asekhena sammāñāṇena samannāgato hoti, asekhāya sammāvimuttiyā samannāgato hoti—
- **EN**: It’s when a mendicant has an adept’s right view, right purpose, right speech, right action, right livelihood, right effort, right mindfulness, right immersion, right knowledge, and right freedom.

## mn78:14.3
- **PLI**: imehi kho ahaṁ, thapati, dasahi dhammehi samannāgataṁ purisapuggalaṁ paññapemi sampannakusalaṁ paramakusalaṁ uttamapattipattaṁ samaṇaṁ ayojjhan”ti.
- **EN**: When an individual possesses these ten qualities, I describe them as an invincible ascetic—accomplished in the skillful, excelling in the skillful, attained to the highest attainment.”

## mn78:14.4  `ms10M_751`
- **PLI**: Idamavoca bhagavā.
- **EN**: That is what the Buddha said.

## mn78:14.5

- **EN**: Satisfied, Pañcakaṅga the chamberlain approved what the Buddha said.
