## mn81:18.7

## mn81:18.9

- **EN**: He is free of doubt regarding suffering, its origin, its cessation, and the practice that leads to its cessation.
- **NOTE**: This shows him to be one of the noble ones.

## mn81:18.10
- **PLI**: Ghaṭikāro kho, mahārāja, kumbhakāro ekabhattiko brahmacārī sīlavā kalyāṇadhammo.
- **EN**: He eats in one part of the day; he’s chaste, ethical, and of good character.
- **NOTE**: This is equivalent to the eight precepts.

## mn81:18.11
- **PLI**: Ghaṭikāro kho, mahārāja, kumbhakāro nikkhittamaṇisuvaṇṇo apetajātarūparajato.
- **EN**: He has set aside gems and gold, and quit using gold and currency.
- **NOTE**: The same is said of Buddhist mendicants at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn42.10/en/sujato#2.4'>SN 42.10:2.4</a>. This parallels the ten precepts of the <i lang='pi' translate='no'>sāmaṇera</i>, which forbid using money (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-kd1/en/sujato#56.1.14'>Kd 1:56.1.14</a>).

## mn81:18.12

- **EN**: He has put down the shovel and doesn’t dig the earth with his own hands.
- **NOTE**: Also said of ancient Brahmanical hermits at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn27/en/sujato#22.6'>DN 27:22.6</a>. Digging the earth is an offense for mendicants (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-bu-vb-pc10/en/sujato'>Bu Pc 10</a>).

## mn81:18.15

- **EN**: He provided for his blind old parents.

- **PLI**: Ghaṭikāro kho, mahārāja, kumbhakāro pañcannaṁ orambhāgiyānaṁ saṁyojanānaṁ parikkhayā opapātiko tattha parinibbāyī anāvattidhammo tasmā lokā.
- **EN**: And since he has ended the five lower fetters, Ghaṭīkāra will be reborn spontaneously and will become extinguished there, not liable to return from that world.
- **NOTE**: He is a non-returner.

## mn81:19.2

- **EN**: “Excuse me, where has Bhaggava gone?”
- **NOTE**: The Buddha uses Ghaṭīkāra’s clan name, as he does at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn1.50/en/sujato#11.2'>SN 1.50:11.2</a>.

## mn81:19.4
- **PLI**: “Nikkhanto kho te, bhante, upaṭṭhāko antokumbhiyā odanaṁ gahetvā pariyogā sūpaṁ gahetvā paribhuñjā”ti.
- **EN**: “Your supporter has gone out, sir. But take rice from the pot and sauce from the pan and eat.”

## mn81:19.5
- **PLI**: Atha khvāhaṁ, mahārāja, kumbhiyā odanaṁ gahetvā pariyogā sūpaṁ gahetvā paribhuñjitvā uṭṭhāyāsanā pakkamiṁ.
- **EN**: So that’s what I did. And after eating I rose from my seat and left.
- **NOTE**: This appears to technically violate <a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-bu-vb-pc40/en/sujato'>Bu Pc 40</a>, which requires that food be given with the hands, not simply by speech. However, not all Buddhas lay down the exact same Vinaya rules. Several parallels explain this by saying that Kassapa was following the customs of the mystical land of Uttarakuru (MA 63 at T i 502a19; T i 502b8; Saṅghabhedavastu 17:391 at Gnoli 1978a:27,14 and D (1) <em>’dul ba</em>, <em>ga</em> 8a3), where no one had any possessions (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn32/en/sujato#7.4'>DN 32:7.4</a>).

- **EN**: Then joy and happiness did not leave him for a fortnight, or his parents for a week.
