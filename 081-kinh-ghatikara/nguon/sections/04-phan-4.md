## mn81:20.4
- **PLI**: “Nikkhanto kho te, bhante, upaṭṭhāko anto kaḷopiyā kummāsaṁ gahetvā pariyogā sūpaṁ gahetvā paribhuñjā”ti.
- **EN**: “Your supporter has gone out, sir. But take porridge from the pot and sauce from the pan and eat.”

## mn81:20.5
- **PLI**: Atha khvāhaṁ, mahārāja, kaḷopiyā kummāsaṁ gahetvā pariyogā sūpaṁ gahetvā paribhuñjitvā uṭṭhāyāsanā pakkamiṁ.
- **EN**: So that’s what I did. And after eating I rose from my seat and left.

## mn81:20.6
- **PLI**: Atha kho, mahārāja, ghaṭikāro kumbhakāro yena mātāpitaro tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā mātāpitaro etadavoca:
- **EN**: Then Ghaṭīkāra went up to his parents and said,

## mn81:20.7
- **PLI**: “ko kaḷopiyā kummāsaṁ gahetvā pariyogā sūpaṁ gahetvā paribhuñjitvā uṭṭhāyāsanā pakkanto”ti?
- **EN**: “Who took porridge from the pot and sauce from the pan, ate it, and left?”

## mn81:20.8
- **PLI**: “Kassapo, tāta, bhagavā arahaṁ sammāsambuddho kaḷopiyā kummāsaṁ gahetvā pariyogā sūpaṁ gahetvā paribhuñjitvā uṭṭhāyāsanā pakkanto”ti.
- **EN**: “It was the Buddha Kassapa, my dear.”

## mn81:20.9
- **PLI**: Atha kho, mahārāja, ghaṭikārassa kumbhakārassa etadahosi:
- **EN**: Then Ghaṭīkāra thought,

## mn81:20.10
- **PLI**: “lābhā vata me, suladdhaṁ vata me,
- **EN**: “I’m so fortunate, so very fortunate,

## mn81:20.11
- **PLI**: yassa me kassapo bhagavā arahaṁ sammāsambuddho evaṁ abhivissattho”ti.
- **EN**: to be trusted so much by the Buddha Kassapa!”

## mn81:20.12
- **PLI**: Atha kho, mahārāja, ghaṭikāraṁ kumbhakāraṁ aḍḍhamāsaṁ pītisukhaṁ na vijahati, sattāhaṁ mātāpitūnaṁ.
- **EN**: Then joy and happiness did not leave him for a fortnight, or his parents for a week.

## mn81:22.1  `bj11.418, dr13.358, ms10M_834, msdiv292, nya22, sya13.387`
- **PLI**: Atha kho, ānanda, kikī kāsirājā ghaṭikārassa kumbhakārassa pañcamattāni taṇḍulavāhasatāni pāhesi paṇḍupuṭakassa sālino tadupiyañca sūpeyyaṁ.
- **EN**: Then King Kikī sent around five hundred loads of rice, soft saffron rice, and suitable sauce to Ghaṭīkāra.
- **NOTE**: “Suitable sauce” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>tadupiyañca sūpeyyaṁ</i>) is also at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn17/en/sujato#2.15.10'>DN 17:2.15.10</a> and <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn22.96/en/sujato#4.10'>SN 22.96:4.10</a>, where it describes a similarly luxurious meal. | A secondary theme of this parable is to highlight the different attitudes to offering. Kikī is generous, but his offering is extravagant and indulgent, and ultimately serves his desires, whereas Ghaṭīkāra is concerned only to help with what is needed at the time.
