# Phần 3  (21 đoạn)

## mn19:25.6
- **PLI**: Tasseva kho pana, bhikkhave, mahato migasaṅghassa kocideva puriso uppajjeyya atthakāmo hitakāmo yogakkhemakāmo.
- **EN**: Then along comes a person who wants to help keep the herd of deer safe.

## mn19:25.7
- **PLI**: So yvāssa maggo khemo sovatthiko pītigamanīyo taṁ maggaṁ vivareyya, pidaheyya kummaggaṁ, ūhaneyya okacaraṁ, nāseyya okacārikaṁ.
- **EN**: They open up the safe, secure path that leads to happiness, and close off the wrong path. They get rid of the decoys

## mn19:25.8
- **PLI**: Evañhi so, bhikkhave, mahāmigasaṅgho aparena samayena vuddhiṁ virūḷhiṁ vepullaṁ āpajjeyya.
- **EN**: so that, in due course, that herd of deer would grow, increase, and mature.

- **EN**: ‘A large herd of deer’ is a term for sentient beings.

## mn19:26.5
- **PLI**: Puriso anatthakāmo ahitakāmo ayogakkhemakāmoti kho, bhikkhave, mārassetaṁ pāpimato adhivacanaṁ.
- **EN**: ‘A person who wants to harm, injure, and threaten them’ is a term for Māra the Wicked.

## mn19:26.6
- **PLI**: Kummaggoti kho, bhikkhave, aṭṭhaṅgikassetaṁ micchāmaggassa adhivacanaṁ, seyyathidaṁ—
- **EN**: ‘The wrong path’ is a term for the wrong eightfold path, that is,

## mn19:26.7
- **PLI**: micchādiṭṭhiyā micchāsaṅkappassa micchāvācāya micchākammantassa micchāājīvassa micchāvāyāmassa micchāsatiyā micchāsamādhissa.
- **EN**: wrong view, wrong purpose, wrong speech, wrong action, wrong livelihood, wrong effort, wrong mindfulness, and wrong immersion.

## mn19:26.8
- **PLI**: Okacaroti kho, bhikkhave, nandīrāgassetaṁ adhivacanaṁ.
- **EN**: ‘A domesticated male deer’ is a term for greed and relishing.

## mn19:26.9
- **PLI**: Okacārikāti kho, bhikkhave, avijjāyetaṁ adhivacanaṁ.
- **EN**: ‘A domesticated female deer’ is a term for ignorance.

## mn19:26.10
- **PLI**: Puriso atthakāmo hitakāmo yogakkhemakāmoti kho, bhikkhave, tathāgatassetaṁ adhivacanaṁ arahato sammāsambuddhassa.
- **EN**: ‘A person who wants to help keep the herd of deer safe’ is a term for the Realized One, the perfected one, the fully awakened Buddha.

## mn19:26.11
- **PLI**: Khemo maggo sovatthiko pītigamanīyoti kho, bhikkhave, ariyassetaṁ aṭṭhaṅgikassa maggassa adhivacanaṁ, seyyathidaṁ—
- **EN**: ‘The safe, secure path that leads to happiness’ is a term for the noble eightfold path, that is:

## mn19:26.12
- **PLI**: sammādiṭṭhiyā sammāsaṅkappassa sammāvācāya sammākammantassa sammāājīvassa sammāvāyāmassa sammāsatiyā sammāsamādhissa.
- **EN**: right view, right purpose, right speech, right action, right livelihood, right effort, right mindfulness, and right immersion.
