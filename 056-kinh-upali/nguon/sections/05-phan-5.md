## mn56:19.3
- **PLI**: Sace koci nigaṇṭho āgacchati tamenaṁ tvaṁ evaṁ vadeyyāsi:
- **EN**: If any Jain ascetics come, say this to them:

## mn56:19.4
- **PLI**: ‘tiṭṭha, bhante, mā pāvisi.
- **EN**: ‘Wait, sir, do not enter.
- **NOTE**: This is even harsher than it appears, for one of the Jains’ rules is not to wait when invited for food (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>natiṭṭhabhaddantiko</i>, eg. <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn12/en/sujato#45.1'>MN 12:45.1</a>).

## mn56:19.5
- **PLI**: Ajjatagge upāli gahapati samaṇassa gotamassa sāvakattaṁ upagato.
- **EN**: From now on the householder Upāli has become a disciple of the ascetic Gotama.

## mn56:19.6
- **PLI**: Āvaṭaṁ dvāraṁ nigaṇṭhānaṁ nigaṇṭhīnaṁ, anāvaṭaṁ dvāraṁ bhagavato bhikkhūnaṁ bhikkhunīnaṁ upāsakānaṁ upāsikānaṁ.
- **EN**: His gate is closed to Jain monks and nuns, and opened for the Buddha’s monks, nuns, laymen, and laywomen.

## mn56:19.7
- **PLI**: Sace te, bhante, piṇḍakena attho, ettheva tiṭṭha, ettheva te āharissantī’”ti.
- **EN**: If you require almsfood, wait here, they will bring it to you.’”

## mn56:19.8
- **PLI**: “Evaṁ, bhante”ti kho dovāriko upālissa gahapatissa paccassosi.
- **EN**: “Yes, sir,” replied the gatekeeper.
