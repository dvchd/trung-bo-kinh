## mn38:30.8

- **EN**: When they smell an odor with their nose …

## mn38:30.9
- **PLI**: jivhāya rasaṁ sāyitvā …pe…
- **EN**: When they taste a flavor with their tongue …

## mn38:30.10
- **PLI**: kāyena phoṭṭhabbaṁ phusitvā …pe…
- **EN**: When they feel a touch with their body …

## mn38:30.11
- **PLI**: manasā dhammaṁ viññāya piyarūpe dhamme sārajjati, appiyarūpe dhamme byāpajjati, anupaṭṭhitakāyasati ca viharati parittacetaso.
- **EN**: When they know an idea with their mind, if it’s pleasant they desire it, but if it’s unpleasant they dislike it. They live with mindfulness of the body unestablished and their heart stunted.

## mn38:30.12
- **PLI**: Tañca cetovimuttiṁ paññāvimuttiṁ yathābhūtaṁ nappajānāti yatthassa te pāpakā akusalā dhammā aparisesā nirujjhanti.
- **EN**: And they don’t truly understand the freedom of heart and freedom by wisdom where those arisen bad, unskillful qualities cease without remainder.

## mn38:30.13
- **PLI**: So evaṁ anurodhavirodhaṁ samāpanno yaṁ kiñci vedanaṁ vedeti sukhaṁ vā dukkhaṁ vā adukkhamasukhaṁ vā, so taṁ vedanaṁ abhinandati abhivadati ajjhosāya tiṭṭhati.
- **EN**: Being so full of favoring and opposing, when they experience any kind of feeling—pleasant, unpleasant, or neutral—they approve, welcome, and keep clinging to it.

## mn38:30.14
- **PLI**: Tassa taṁ vedanaṁ abhinandato abhivadato ajjhosāya tiṭṭhato uppajjati nandī.
- **EN**: This gives rise to relishing.

## mn38:30.15
- **PLI**: Yā vedanāsu nandī tadupādānaṁ, tassupādānapaccayā bhavo, bhavapaccayā jāti, jātipaccayā jarāmaraṇaṁ sokaparidevadukkhadomanassupāyāsā sambhavanti.
- **EN**: Relishing feelings is grasping. Their grasping is a requirement for continued existence. Continued existence is a requirement for rebirth. Rebirth is a requirement for old age and death, sorrow, lamentation, pain, sadness, and distress to come to be.

## mn38:30.16
- **PLI**: Evametassa kevalassa dukkhakkhandhassa samudayo hoti.
- **EN**: That is how this entire mass of suffering originates.

## mn38:31.1  `bj10.626, cck12.468, ms9M_1376, msdiv410, ndp9.329, nya31, sya12.490, vri12.339`
- **PLI**: Idha, bhikkhave, tathāgato loke uppajjati arahaṁ sammāsambuddho vijjācaraṇasampanno sugato lokavidū anuttaro purisadammasārathi satthā devamanussānaṁ buddho bhagavā.
- **EN**: But consider when a Realized One arises in the world, perfected, a fully awakened Buddha, accomplished in knowledge and conduct, holy, knower of the world, supreme guide for those fit for training, teacher of gods and humans, awakened, blessed.

- **EN**: He has realized with his own insight this world—with its gods, Māras, and divinities, this population with its ascetics and brahmins, gods and humans—and he makes it known to others.

## mn38:31.4

## mn38:32.5

- **EN**: Why don’t I shave off my hair and beard, dress in ocher robes, and go forth from lay life to homelessness?’

## mn38:32.7
- **PLI**: So aparena samayena appaṁ vā bhogakkhandhaṁ pahāya, mahantaṁ vā bhogakkhandhaṁ pahāya, appaṁ vā ñātiparivaṭṭaṁ pahāya, mahantaṁ vā ñātiparivaṭṭaṁ pahāya, kesamassuṁ ohāretvā, kāsāyāni vatthāni acchādetvā, agārasmā anagāriyaṁ pabbajati.
- **EN**: After some time they give up a large or small fortune, and a large or small family circle. They shave off hair and beard, dress in ocher robes, and go forth from the lay life to homelessness.
