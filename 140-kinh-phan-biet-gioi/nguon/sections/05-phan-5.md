## mn140:30.1  `ms11M_1039, msdiv369, nya30`
- **PLI**: ‘Yattha ṭhitaṁ maññassavā nappavattanti, maññassave kho pana nappavattamāne muni santoti vuccatī’ti—
- **EN**: ‘Where they stand, the streams of conceiving do not flow. And where the streams of conceiving do not flow, they are called a sage at peace.’
- **NOTE**: “Streams of conceiving” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>maññassavā</i>) is a unique image, allied to the notion that defilements may “stream on to” a person (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>āsavā assaveyyuṁ</i>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an4.195/en/sujato#2.2'>AN 4.195:2.2</a>).

## mn140:30.2

- **EN**: That’s what I said, but why did I say it?

## mn140:31.1  `nya31`
- **PLI**: ‘Asmī’ti, bhikkhu, maññitametaṁ, ‘ayamahamasmī’ti maññitametaṁ, ‘bhavissan’ti maññitametaṁ, ‘na bhavissan’ti maññitametaṁ, ‘rūpī bhavissan’ti maññitametaṁ, ‘arūpī bhavissan’ti maññitametaṁ, ‘saññī bhavissan’ti maññitametaṁ, ‘asaññī bhavissan’ti maññitametaṁ, ‘nevasaññīnāsaññī bhavissan’ti maññitametaṁ.
- **EN**: These are all forms of conceiving: ‘I am’, ‘I am this’, ‘I will be’, ‘I will not be’, ‘I will have form’, ‘I will be formless’, ‘I will be percipient’, ‘I will be non-percipient’, ‘I will be neither percipient nor non-percipient.’

## mn140:31.2
- **PLI**: Maññitaṁ, bhikkhu, rogo maññitaṁ gaṇḍo maññitaṁ sallaṁ.
- **EN**: Conceiving is a disease, a boil, a dart.

## mn140:31.3
- **PLI**: Sabbamaññitānaṁ tveva, bhikkhu, samatikkamā muni santoti vuccati.
- **EN**: Having gone beyond all conceiving, one is called a sage at peace.

## mn140:31.4
- **PLI**: Muni kho pana, bhikkhu, santo na jāyati, na jīyati, na mīyati, na kuppati, na piheti.
- **EN**: The sage at peace is not reborn, does not grow old, and does not die. They are not shaken, and do not yearn.

## mn140:31.5
- **PLI**: Tañhissa, bhikkhu, natthi yena jāyetha, ajāyamāno kiṁ jīyissati, ajīyamāno kiṁ mīyissati, amīyamāno kiṁ kuppissati, akuppamāno kissa pihessati?
- **EN**: For they have nothing which would cause them to be reborn. Not being reborn, how could they grow old? Not growing old, how could they die? Not dying, how could they be shaken? Not shaking, for what could they yearn?

## mn140:33.1  `bj12.510, cck14.409, csp1ed11.290, csp2ed11.290, dr13.407, ms11M_1040, msdiv370, ndp11.332, nya33, pts-vp-pli3.247, sya14.447, vri14.296`
- **PLI**: Atha kho āyasmā pukkusāti:
- **EN**: Then Venerable Pukkusāti thought,

## mn140:33.2
- **PLI**: “satthā kira me anuppatto, sugato kira me anuppatto, sammāsambuddho kira me anuppatto”ti uṭṭhāyāsanā ekaṁsaṁ cīvaraṁ katvā bhagavato pādesu sirasā nipatitvā bhagavantaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: “It seems the Teacher has come to me! The Holy One has come to me! The fully awakened Buddha has come to me!” He got up from his seat, arranged his robe over one shoulder, bowed with his head to the Buddha’s feet, and said,

## mn140:33.3
- **PLI**: “accayo maṁ, bhante, accagamā yathābālaṁ yathāmūḷhaṁ yathāakusalaṁ, yohaṁ bhagavantaṁ āvusovādena samudācaritabbaṁ amaññissaṁ.

- **EN**: “Indeed, mendicant, you made a mistake. It was foolish, stupid, and unskillful of you to act in that way.

## mn140:33.6

- **EN**: For it is growth in the training of the Noble One to recognize a mistake for what it is, deal with it properly, and commit to restraint in the future.”

## mn140:34.1  `nya34`
- **PLI**: “Labheyyāhaṁ, bhante, bhagavato santike upasampadan”ti.
- **EN**: “Sir, may I receive the going forth, the ordination in the Buddha’s presence?”

## mn140:34.2
- **PLI**: “Paripuṇṇaṁ pana te, bhikkhu, pattacīvaran”ti?
- **EN**: “But mendicant, do you have a full set of bowl and robes?”
- **NOTE**: This is one of the questions asked of an ordination candidate (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-kd1/en/sujato#76.1.14'>Kd 1:76.1.14</a>). A mendicant is expected to have a bowl—made of metal or clay, not too big or too small—and three robes—the lower robe or sabong (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>antaravāsaka</i>), the upper robe (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>uttarasaṅga</i>), and the double-layered outer cloak (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>saṅghāṭi</i>). In the suttas, the Buddha does not usually ask this question, so he must have observed that Pukkusāti, who had renounced of his own volition, lacked the proper requisites.

## mn140:34.3
- **PLI**: “Na kho me, bhante, paripuṇṇaṁ pattacīvaran”ti.
- **EN**: “No, sir, I do not.”

## mn140:34.4
- **PLI**: “Na kho, bhikkhu, tathāgatā aparipuṇṇapattacīvaraṁ upasampādentī”ti.
- **EN**: “The Realized Ones do not ordain those without a full set of bowl and robes.”

## mn140:35.1  `ms11M_1041, nya35`
- **PLI**: Atha kho āyasmā pukkusāti bhagavato bhāsitaṁ abhinanditvā anumoditvā uṭṭhāyāsanā bhagavantaṁ abhivādetvā padakkhiṇaṁ katvā pattacīvarapariyesanaṁ pakkāmi.
- **EN**: And then Venerable Pukkusāti approved and agreed with what the Buddha said. He got up from his seat, bowed and respectfully circled the Buddha, keeping him on his right, before leaving.

## mn140:35.2  `cck14.410, dr13.408, ms11M_1042, sya14.448`
- **PLI**: Atha kho āyasmantaṁ pukkusātiṁ pattacīvarapariyesanaṁ carantaṁ vibbhantā gāvī jīvitā voropesi.
- **EN**: But while he was wandering in search of a bowl and robes, a stray cow took his life.
- **NOTE**: Cows were normally regarded as gentle creatures (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/snp2.7/en/sujato#27.1'>Snp 2.7:27.1</a>). They sometimes become violent, however, especially when protecting young calves, resulting in the tragic fates of Bāhiya (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/ud1.10/en/sujato#10.2'>Ud 1.10:10.2</a>) and Suppabuddha (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/ud5.3/en/sujato#7.2'>Ud 5.3:7.2</a>). Attacks by cows still occur today in India and wherever cows are raised.

## mn140:36.1  `nya36`

## mn140:36.5

- **EN**: With the ending of the five lower fetters, he’s been reborn spontaneously and will become extinguished there, not liable to return from that world.”
- **NOTE**: Pukkusāti is one of a group of seven who attained arahantship in the Pure Abode of Aviha (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn1.50/en/sujato#3.2'>SN 1.50:3.2</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn2.24/en/sujato#4.2'>SN 2.24:4.2</a>).
