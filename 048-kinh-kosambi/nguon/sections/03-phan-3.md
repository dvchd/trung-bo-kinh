# Phần 3  (8 đoạn)

## mn48:14.5
- **PLI**: So evaṁ pajānāti:
- **EN**: They understand,

## mn48:14.6
- **PLI**: ‘yathārūpāya balatāya diṭṭhisampanno puggalo samannāgato, ahampi tathārūpāya balatāya samannāgato’ti.
- **EN**: ‘I have the same strength as an individual accomplished in view.’

## mn48:14.7
- **PLI**: Idamassa sattamaṁ ñāṇaṁ adhigataṁ hoti ariyaṁ lokuttaraṁ asādhāraṇaṁ puthujjanehi.
- **EN**: This is the seventh knowledge they have achieved that is noble and transcendent, and is distinct from that of ordinary people.

## mn48:15.1  `csp1ed9.401, csp2ed9.401, ms9M_1708, msdiv500, nya15`
- **PLI**: Evaṁ sattaṅgasamannāgatassa kho, bhikkhave, ariyasāvakassa dhammatā susamanniṭṭhā hoti sotāpattiphalasacchikiriyāya.
- **EN**: When a noble disciple has these seven factors, they have properly investigated their nature through the realization of the fruit of stream-entry.
- **NOTE**: Normally <i lang='pi' translate='no'>sotāpattiphalasacchikiriyāya</i> is dative, “<em>for</em> the realization of the fruit of stream-entry”, but that cannot be the case here as they are already a stream-enterer. The commentary reads it as instrumental.

## mn48:15.2
- **PLI**: Evaṁ sattaṅgasamannāgato kho, bhikkhave, ariyasāvako sotāpattiphalasamannāgato hotī”ti.
- **EN**: A noble disciple with these seven factors has the fruit of stream-entry.”

## mn48:15.3  `ms9M_1709`
- **PLI**: Idamavoca bhagavā.
- **EN**: That is what the Buddha said.

## mn48:15.4
- **PLI**: Attamanā te bhikkhū bhagavato bhāsitaṁ abhinandunti.
- **EN**: Satisfied, the mendicants approved what the Buddha said.

- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)
