## mn48:11.5
- **PLI**: kiñcāpi tathārūpiṁ āpattiṁ āpajjati, yathārūpāya āpattiyā vuṭṭhānaṁ paññāyati, atha kho naṁ khippameva satthari vā viññūsu vā sabrahmacārīsu deseti vivarati uttānīkaroti;
- **EN**: Though they may fall into a kind of offense for which rehabilitation is possible, they quickly explain, clarify, and disclose it to the Teacher or a sensible spiritual companion.
- **NOTE**: Vinaya offenses fall into a number of classes, all of which must be confessed by a guilty monastic to their fellow monastics. Some are resolved upon confession, others by undergoing a procedure of relinquishment or temporary suspension. However, the most serious offenses—sexual intercourse, murder, stealing, and lying about spiritual attainments—entail immediate and permanent expulsion. Offenses are further distinguished by intention, as some offenses may be transgressed without ill intent or even unknowingly (for example, eating at the wrong time). A stream-enterer cannot commit an expulsion offense, but they may commit one of the other offenses without ill intent.
