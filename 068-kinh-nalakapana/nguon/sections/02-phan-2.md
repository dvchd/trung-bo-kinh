# Phần 2  (50 đoạn)

## mn68:9.1  `dr13.188, mc2.140, ms10M_448, nya9`
- **PLI**: “Na kho, anuruddhā, tathāgato janakuhanatthaṁ na janalapanatthaṁ na lābhasakkārasilokānisaṁsatthaṁ na ‘iti maṁ jano jānātū’ti sāvake abbhatīte kālaṅkate upapattīsu byākaroti:
- **EN**: “The Realized One does not declare such things for the sake of fawning or flattering people, nor for the benefit of possessions, honor, or popularity, nor thinking, ‘So let people know about me!’

## mn68:9.2
- **PLI**: ‘asu amutra upapanno, asu amutra upapanno’ti.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)

## mn68:9.3
- **PLI**: Santi ca kho, anuruddhā, kulaputtā saddhā uḷāravedā uḷārapāmojjā.
- **EN**: Rather, there are gentlemen of faith who are full of sublime joy and gladness.

## mn68:9.4
- **PLI**: Te taṁ sutvā tadatthāya cittaṁ upasaṁharanti.
- **EN**: When they hear that, they apply their minds to that end.

## mn68:9.5
- **PLI**: Tesaṁ taṁ, anuruddhā, hoti dīgharattaṁ hitāya sukhāya.
- **EN**: That is for their lasting welfare and happiness.

## mn68:10.1  `cck13.213, ms10M_449, msdiv169, nya10`
- **PLI**: Idhānuruddhā, bhikkhu suṇāti:
- **EN**: Take a monk who hears this:

## mn68:10.2
- **PLI**: ‘itthannāmo bhikkhu kālaṅkato;
- **EN**: ‘The monk named so-and-so has passed away.

## mn68:10.3
- **PLI**: so bhagavatā byākato—
- **EN**: The Buddha has declared that

## mn68:10.4
- **PLI**: aññāya saṇṭhahī’ti.
- **EN**: he was enlightened.’

## mn68:10.5
- **PLI**: So kho panassa āyasmā sāmaṁ diṭṭho vā hoti anussavassuto vā:
- **EN**: And he’s either seen for himself, or heard from someone else, that that venerable

## mn68:10.6
- **PLI**: ‘evaṁsīlo so āyasmā ahosi itipi, evaṁdhammo so āyasmā ahosi itipi, evaṁpañño so āyasmā ahosi itipi, evaṁvihārī so āyasmā ahosi itipi, evaṁvimutto so āyasmā ahosi itipī’ti.
- **EN**: had such ethics, such qualities, such wisdom, such meditation, or such freedom.

## mn68:10.7
- **PLI**: So tassa saddhañca sīlañca sutañca cāgañca paññañca anussaranto tadatthāya cittaṁ upasaṁharati.
- **EN**: Recollecting that monk’s faith, ethics, learning, generosity, and wisdom, he applies his mind to that end.

## mn68:10.8
- **PLI**: Evampi kho, anuruddhā, bhikkhuno phāsuvihāro hoti.
- **EN**: That’s how a monk lives at ease.
