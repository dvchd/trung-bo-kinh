## mn68:21.8
- **PLI**: Evampi kho, anuruddhā, upāsikāya phāsuvihāro hoti.
- **EN**: That’s how a laywoman lives at ease.

## mn68:22.1  `bj11.226, mc2.144, ms10M_461, nya22, sya13.213`
- **PLI**: Idhānuruddhā, upāsikā suṇāti:
- **EN**: Take a laywoman who hears this:

## mn68:22.2
- **PLI**: ‘itthannāmā upāsikā kālaṅkatā;
- **EN**: ‘The laywoman named so-and-so has passed away.

## mn68:22.3
- **PLI**: sā bhagavatā byākatā—
- **EN**: The Buddha has declared that,

## mn68:22.4
- **PLI**: tiṇṇaṁ saṁyojanānaṁ parikkhayā rāgadosamohānaṁ tanuttā sakadāgāminī sakideva imaṁ lokaṁ āgantvā dukkhassantaṁ karissatī’ti.
- **EN**: with the ending of three fetters, and the weakening of greed, hate, and delusion, she’s a once-returner. She’ll come back to this world once only, then make an end of suffering.’

## mn68:22.5
- **PLI**: Sā kho panassā bhaginī sāmaṁ diṭṭhā vā hoti anussavassutā vā:
- **EN**: And she’s either seen for herself, or heard from someone else, that that sister

## mn68:22.6
- **PLI**: ‘evaṁsīlā sā bhaginī ahosi itipi, evaṁdhammā … evaṁpaññā … evaṁvihārinī … evaṁvimuttā sā bhaginī ahosi itipī’ti.
- **EN**: had such ethics, such qualities, such wisdom, such meditation, or such freedom.

## mn68:22.7
- **PLI**: Sā tassā saddhañca sīlañca sutañca cāgañca paññañca anussarantī tadatthāya cittaṁ upasaṁharati.
- **EN**: Recollecting that laywoman’s faith, ethics, learning, generosity, and wisdom, she applies her mind to that end.

## mn68:22.8
- **PLI**: Evampi kho, anuruddhā, upāsikāya phāsuvihāro hoti.
- **EN**: That too is how a laywoman lives at ease.

## mn68:24.1  `ms10M_463, nya24, vri13.142`
- **PLI**: Iti kho, anuruddhā, tathāgato na janakuhanatthaṁ na janalapanatthaṁ na lābhasakkārasilokānisaṁsatthaṁ na ‘iti maṁ jano jānātū’ti sāvake abbhatīte kālaṅkate upapattīsu byākaroti:
- **EN**: So it’s not for the sake of fawning or flattering people, nor for the benefit of possessions, honor, or popularity, nor thinking, ‘So let people know about me!’ that the Realized One declares the rebirth of his disciples who have passed away:

## mn68:24.2
- **PLI**: ‘asu amutra upapanno, asu amutra upapanno’ti.
- **EN**: ‘This one is reborn here, while that one is reborn there.’

## mn68:24.3
- **PLI**: Santi ca kho, anuruddhā, kulaputtā saddhā uḷāravedā uḷārapāmojjā.
- **EN**: Rather, there are gentlemen of faith who are full of joy and gladness.

## mn68:24.4
- **PLI**: Te taṁ sutvā tadatthāya cittaṁ upasaṁharanti.
- **EN**: When they hear that, they apply their minds to that end.

## mn68:24.5
- **PLI**: Tesaṁ taṁ, anuruddhā, hoti dīgharattaṁ hitāya sukhāyā”ti.
- **EN**: That is for their lasting welfare and happiness.”
