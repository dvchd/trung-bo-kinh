# Phần 3  (50 đoạn)

## mn68:15.6
- **PLI**: ‘evaṁsīlā sā bhaginī ahosi itipi, evaṁdhammā …pe… evaṁpaññā … evaṁvihārinī … evaṁvimuttā sā bhaginī ahosi itipī’ti.
- **EN**: had such ethics, such qualities, such wisdom, such meditation, or such freedom.

## mn68:15.7
- **PLI**: Sā tassā saddhañca sīlañca sutañca cāgañca paññañca anussarantī tadatthāya cittaṁ upasaṁharati.
- **EN**: Recollecting that nun’s faith, ethics, learning, generosity, and wisdom, she applies her mind to that end.

## mn68:15.8
- **PLI**: Evampi kho, anuruddhā, bhikkhuniyā phāsuvihāro hoti.
- **EN**: That too is how a nun lives at ease.
