# Phần 3  (50 đoạn)

## mn5:16.2
- **PLI**: ‘aho vata ahameva bhattagge bhuttāvī anumodeyyaṁ, na añño bhikkhu bhattagge bhuttāvī anumodeyyā’ti.
- **EN**: ‘I hope that I alone give the verses of appreciation after eating in the refectory, not some other mendicant.’
- **NOTE**: This is the <i lang='pi' translate='no'>anumodana</i>, which is still recited in Pali today. It was a short teaching on the benefits of giving, emphasizing the positive effects of the donor’s actions. See examples at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn16/en/sujato#1.31.2'>DN 16:1.31.2</a> = <a href='https://suttacentral.net/ud8.6/en/sujato#22.1'>Ud 8.6:22.1</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn92/en/sujato#26.1'>MN 92:26.1</a> = <a href='https://suttacentral.net/snp3.7/en/sujato#35.1'>Snp 3.7:35.1</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn55.26/en/sujato#20.1'>SN 55.26:20.1</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an5.44/en/sujato#8.1'>AN 5.44:8.1</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an54/en/sujato#8.1'>AN 54:8.1</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-kd1/en/sujato#15.14.4'>Kd 1:15.14.4</a>, and <a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-kd1/en/sujato#1.5.1'>Kd 1:1.5.1</a>.

- **EN**: But it’s possible that some other mendicant gives the verses of appreciation after eating in the refectory …
