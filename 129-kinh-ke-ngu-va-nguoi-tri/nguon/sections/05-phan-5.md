## mn129:35.7
- **PLI**: ‘pāṇo na hantabbo, adinnaṁ nādātabbaṁ, kāmesumicchā na caritabbā, musā na bhāsitabbā, majjaṁ na pātabbaṁ, yathābhuttañca bhuñjathā’ti.
- **EN**: ‘Do not kill living creatures. Do not steal. Do not commit sexual misconduct. Do not lie. Do not drink liquor. Maintain the current level of taxation.’
- **NOTE**: Read <i lang='pi' translate='no'>bhuñjati</i> at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn98/en/sujato#10.30'>MN 98:10.30</a> with <i lang='pi' translate='no'>yathābhuttañca bhuñjatha</i> at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn17/en/sujato#1.9.4'>DN 17:1.9.4</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn26/en/sujato#6.7'>DN 26:6.7</a>, and <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn129/en/sujato#35.7'>MN 129:35.7</a>. These have sometimes been rendered “eat”, “enjoy”, or “govern”. But compare the archaic English “use” meaning “the benefit or profit of lands”. Thus <i lang='pi' translate='no'>yathābhuttañca bhuñjatha</i> means “use as has been used”, i.e. “maintain the current level of taxation”.
