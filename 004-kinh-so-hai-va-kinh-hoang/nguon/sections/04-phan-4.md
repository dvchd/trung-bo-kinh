# Phần 4  (13 đoạn)

## mn4:33.1  `nya33`
- **PLI**: Ayaṁ kho me, brāhmaṇa, rattiyā pacchime yāme tatiyā vijjā adhigatā,
- **EN**: This was the third knowledge, which I achieved in the final watch of the night.

## mn4:33.2
- **PLI**: avijjā vihatā vijjā uppannā, tamo vihato āloko uppanno, yathā taṁ appamattassa ātāpino pahitattassa viharato.
- **EN**: Ignorance was banished and knowledge arose; darkness was banished and light arose, as happens for a meditator who is diligent, keen, and resolute.

## mn4:34.1  `bj10.54, ms9M_120, msdiv55, nya34`
- **PLI**: Siyā kho pana te, brāhmaṇa, evamassa:
- **EN**: Brahmin, you might think:

## mn4:34.2
- **PLI**: ‘ajjāpi nūna samaṇo gotamo avītarāgo avītadoso avītamoho, tasmā araññavanapatthāni pantāni senāsanāni paṭisevatī’ti.
- **EN**: ‘Perhaps the ascetic Gotama is not free of greed, hate, and delusion even today, and that is why he still frequents remote lodgings in the wilderness and the forest.’

## mn4:34.3
- **PLI**: Na kho panetaṁ, brāhmaṇa, evaṁ daṭṭhabbaṁ.
- **EN**: But you should not see it like this.

## mn4:34.4
- **PLI**: Dve kho ahaṁ, brāhmaṇa, atthavase sampassamāno araññavanapatthāni pantāni senāsanāni paṭisevāmi—
- **EN**: I see two reasons to frequent remote lodgings in the wilderness and the forest.

## mn4:34.5
- **PLI**: attano ca diṭṭhadhammasukhavihāraṁ sampassamāno, pacchimañca janataṁ anukampamāno”ti.
- **EN**: I see happiness for myself in this life, and I have sympathy for future generations.”
- **NOTE**: Also at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an2.30/en/sujato#1.1'>AN 2.30:1.1</a>. The Buddha confirms Jānussoṇi’s belief that he acts to set a good example for others.

## mn4:35.1  `dr12.37, ms9M_121, msdiv56, ndp9.32, nya35, pts-vp-pli1.24, vri12.30`
- **PLI**: “Anukampitarūpā vatāyaṁ bhotā gotamena pacchimā janatā, yathā taṁ arahatā sammāsambuddhena.
- **EN**: “Indeed, worthy Gotama has sympathy for future generations, since he is a perfected one, a fully awakened Buddha.

## mn4:35.2
- **PLI**: Abhikkantaṁ, bho gotama. Abhikkantaṁ, bho gotama.
- **EN**: Excellent, worthy Gotama! Excellent, worthy Gotama!

## mn4:35.3
- **PLI**: Seyyathāpi, bho gotama, nikkujjitaṁ vā ukkujjeyya, paṭicchannaṁ vā vivareyya, mūḷhassa vā maggaṁ ācikkheyya, andhakāre vā telapajjotaṁ dhāreyya: ‘cakkhumanto rūpāni dakkhantī’ti; evamevaṁ bhotā gotamena anekapariyāyena dhammo pakāsito.
- **EN**: As if he were righting the overturned, or revealing the hidden, or pointing out the path to the lost, or lighting a lamp in the dark so people with clear eyes can see what’s there, worthy Gotama has made the teaching clear in many ways.

## mn4:35.4
- **PLI**: Esāhaṁ bhavantaṁ gotamaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi dhammañca bhikkhusaṅghañca.
- **EN**: I go for refuge to the worthy Gotama, to the teaching, and to the mendicant Saṅgha.
- **NOTE**: Jānussoṇi goes for refuge so often in the suttas that it is almost a genre unto itself (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn27/en/sujato'>MN 27</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn12.47/en/sujato'>SN 12.47</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an2.17/en/sujato#3.1'>AN 2.17:3.1</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an3.55/en/sujato'>AN 3.55</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an3.59/en/sujato'>AN 3.59</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an4.184/en/sujato'>AN 4.184</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an6.52/en/sujato'>AN 6.52</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an7.47/en/sujato'>AN 7.47</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an10.119/en/sujato'>AN 10.119</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an10.167/en/sujato'>AN 10.167</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an10.177/en/sujato'>AN 10.177</a>). Such narrative accounts of conversion or enlightenment at the end of discourses are not very reliable, as they are not the Buddha’s words but were added by editors at some point.

## mn4:35.5
- **PLI**: Upāsakaṁ maṁ bhavaṁ gotamo dhāretu ajjatagge pāṇupetaṁ saraṇaṁ gatan”ti.
- **EN**: From this day forth, may the worthy Gotama remember me as a lay follower who has gone for refuge for life.”

## mn4:35.6
- **PLI**: Bhayabheravasuttaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ catutthaṁ.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)
