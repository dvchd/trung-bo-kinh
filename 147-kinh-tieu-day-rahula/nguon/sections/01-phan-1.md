# Phần 1  (50 đoạn)

## mn147:1.1  `bj12.574, cck14.457, csp1ed11.325, csp2ed11.325, dr13.459, ms11M_1185, msdiv416, ndp11.376, nya1, pts-vp-pli3.278, sya14.504, vri14.334`
- **PLI**: Evaṁ me sutaṁ—
- **EN**: So I have heard.
- **NOTE**: This discourse recurs at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn35.121/en/sujato'>SN 35.121</a>.

## mn147:1.2
- **PLI**: ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme.
- **EN**: At one time the Buddha was staying near Sāvatthī in Jeta’s Grove, Anāthapiṇḍika’s monastery.

## mn147:1.3
- **PLI**: Atha kho bhagavato rahogatassa paṭisallīnassa evaṁ cetaso parivitakko udapādi:
- **EN**: Then as he was in private retreat this thought came to his mind,

## mn147:1.4
- **PLI**: “paripakkā kho rāhulassa vimuttiparipācanīyā dhammā.
- **EN**: “The qualities that ripen in freedom have ripened in Rāhula.
- **NOTE**: Five perceptions that ripen in freedom are taught at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn33/en/sujato#2.1.139'>DN 33:2.1.139</a>, but apart from that we do not encounter this idea in the discourses. | In <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn18.1/en/sujato'>SN 18.1</a>–5, Rāhula requests a teaching so he can go on retreat. The teachings given there can be compared with the parallel, which says the Buddha first told Rāhula to teach the five aggregates, the six sense-spheres, and conditionality, and then to go on retreat and meditate on the same teachings (SA 200 at T ii 51a23). Only then did the Buddha consider him ready.

## mn147:1.5
- **PLI**: Yannūnāhaṁ rāhulaṁ uttariṁ āsavānaṁ khaye vineyyan”ti.
- **EN**: Why don’t I lead him further to the ending of defilements?”

## mn147:1.6
- **PLI**: Atha kho bhagavā pubbaṇhasamayaṁ nivāsetvā pattacīvaramādāya sāvatthiṁ piṇḍāya pāvisi.
- **EN**: Then the Buddha robed up in the morning and, taking his bowl and robe, entered Sāvatthī for alms.

## mn147:1.7
- **PLI**: Sāvatthiyaṁ piṇḍāya caritvā pacchābhattaṁ piṇḍapātapaṭikkanto āyasmantaṁ rāhulaṁ āmantesi:
- **EN**: Then, after the meal, on his return from almsround, he addressed Venerable Rāhula,

## mn147:1.8
- **PLI**: “gaṇhāhi, rāhula, nisīdanaṁ;
- **EN**: “Rāhula, get your sitting cloth.
- **NOTE**: The sitting cloth was a standard requisite of monastics (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-bu-vb-pc89/en/sujato'>Bu Pc 89</a>). Regularly featured in the texts of the Sarvāstivāda schools, it is rarely mentioned in the Pali suttas (but see <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn16/en/sujato#3.1.3'>DN 16:3.1.3</a>).

## mn147:1.9
- **PLI**: yena andhavanaṁ tenupasaṅkamissāma divāvihārāyā”ti.
- **EN**: Let’s go to the Dark Forest for the day’s meditation.”

## mn147:1.10
- **PLI**: “Evaṁ, bhante”ti kho āyasmā rāhulo bhagavato paṭissutvā nisīdanaṁ ādāya bhagavantaṁ piṭṭhito piṭṭhito anubandhi.
- **EN**: “Yes, sir,” replied Rāhula. Taking his sitting cloth he followed behind the Buddha.

## mn147:2.1  `ms11M_1186, nya2`
- **PLI**: Tena kho pana samayena anekāni devatāsahassāni bhagavantaṁ anubandhāni honti:
- **EN**: Now at that time many thousands of deities followed the Buddha, thinking,
- **NOTE**: The presence of a thousand deities, which is unique to this discourse, is not mentioned in the Chinese parallel, and is obviously a later addition.

## mn147:2.2

## mn147:2.3

- **EN**: Then the Buddha plunged deep into the Dark Forest and sat at the root of a tree on the seat spread out.

## mn147:2.4
- **PLI**: Āyasmāpi kho rāhulo bhagavantaṁ abhivādetvā ekamantaṁ nisīdi.
- **EN**: Rāhula bowed to the Buddha and sat down to one side.

## mn147:2.13

- **EN**: “No, sir.”
