# Phần 3  (13 đoạn)

## mn147:9.2
- **PLI**: Sotasmiṁ nibbindati, saddesu nibbindati …pe…
- **EN**: They grow disillusioned with the ear …

## mn147:9.3
- **PLI**: ghānasmiṁ nibbindati, gandhesu nibbindati …
- **EN**: nose …

## mn147:9.4
- **PLI**: jivhāya nibbindati, rasesu nibbindati …
- **EN**: tongue …

## mn147:9.5
- **PLI**: kāyasmiṁ nibbindati, phoṭṭhabbesu nibbindati …
- **EN**: body …

## mn147:9.6
- **PLI**: manasmiṁ nibbindati, dhammesu nibbindati, manoviññāṇe nibbindati, manosamphasse nibbindati, yamidaṁ manosamphassapaccayā uppajjati vedanāgataṁ saññāgataṁ saṅkhāragataṁ viññāṇagataṁ tasmimpi nibbindati.
- **EN**: mind, ideas, mind consciousness, and mind contact. And they grow disillusioned with anything included in feeling, perception, choices, and consciousness that arises dependent on mind contact.

## mn147:9.7
- **PLI**: Nibbindaṁ virajjati, virāgā vimuccati. Vimuttasmiṁ vimuttamiti ñāṇaṁ hoti.
- **EN**: Being disillusioned, desire fades away. When desire fades away they’re freed. When they’re freed, they know they’re freed.

## mn147:9.8
- **PLI**: ‘Khīṇā jāti, vusitaṁ brahmacariyaṁ, kataṁ karaṇīyaṁ, nāparaṁ itthattāyā’ti pajānātī”ti.
- **EN**: They understand: ‘Rebirth is ended, the spiritual journey has been completed, what had to be done has been done, there is nothing further for this place.’”

## mn147:9.9  `dr13.463, ms11M_1190, ndp11.379`
- **PLI**: Idamavoca bhagavā.
- **EN**: That is what the Buddha said.

## mn147:9.10
- **PLI**: Attamano āyasmā rāhulo bhagavato bhāsitaṁ abhinandīti.
- **EN**: Satisfied, Venerable Rāhula approved what the Buddha said.

## mn147:9.11
- **PLI**: Imasmiñca pana veyyākaraṇasmiṁ bhaññamāne āyasmato rāhulassa anupādāya āsavehi cittaṁ vimucci.
- **EN**: And while this discourse was being spoken, Rāhula’s mind was freed from defilements by not grasping.

## mn147:9.12
- **PLI**: Tāsañca anekānaṁ devatāsahassānaṁ virajaṁ vītamalaṁ dhammacakkhuṁ udapādi:
- **EN**: And the stainless, immaculate vision of the Dhamma arose in those thousands of deities:

## mn147:9.13
- **PLI**: “yaṁ kiñci samudayadhammaṁ sabbaṁ taṁ nirodhadhamman”ti.
- **EN**: “Everything that is liable to arise is liable to cease.”

## mn147:9.14
- **PLI**: Cūḷarāhulovādasuttaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ pañcamaṁ.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)
