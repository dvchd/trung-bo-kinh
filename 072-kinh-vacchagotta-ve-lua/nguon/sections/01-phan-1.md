## mn72:1.1  `bj11.256, cck13.244, dr13.219, mc2.163, ms10M_529, msdiv187, ndp10.176, nya1, pts-vp-pli1.484, sya13.240, vri13.160`
- **PLI**: Evaṁ me sutaṁ—
- **EN**: So I have heard.
- **NOTE**: This sutta depicts Vacchagotta, having gained a degree of faith as depicted in <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn71/en/sujato'>MN 71</a>, questioning the Buddha regarding the undeclared points. This is just a taste of Vacchagotta’s curiosity, as the 55 suttas of SN 33 all deal with the same topic, as do <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn44.7/en/sujato'>SN 44.7</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn44.8/en/sujato'>SN 44.8</a>, and <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn44.11/en/sujato'>SN 44.11</a>. Underlying all these questions is the assumption of a self, which Vacchagotta asks directly about at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn44.10/en/sujato'>SN 44.10</a>.

## mn72:1.2

- **EN**: At one time the Buddha was staying near Sāvatthī in Jeta’s Grove, Anāthapiṇḍika’s monastery.

## mn72:2.1  `nya2`
- **PLI**: Atha kho vacchagotto paribbājako yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavatā saddhiṁ sammodi.
- **EN**: Then the wanderer Vacchagotta went up to the Buddha and exchanged greetings with him.

## mn72:2.2
- **PLI**: Sammodanīyaṁ kathaṁ sāraṇīyaṁ vītisāretvā ekamantaṁ nisīdi. Ekamantaṁ nisinno kho vacchagotto paribbājako bhagavantaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: When the greetings and polite conversation were over, he sat down to one side and said to the Buddha:
