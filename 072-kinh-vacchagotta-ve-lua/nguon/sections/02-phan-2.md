## mn72:15.1  `ms10M_544, nya15, sya13.245`
- **PLI**: “Atthi pana bhoto gotamassa kiñci diṭṭhigatan”ti?
- **EN**: “But does the worthy Gotama have any convictions at all?”

## mn72:15.2
- **PLI**: “Diṭṭhigatanti kho, vaccha, apanītametaṁ tathāgatassa.
- **EN**: “A realized one has done away with convictions.
- **NOTE**: The Buddha, or indeed any arahant, has no “convictions” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>diṭṭhigata</i>), i.e. theories to attach to, since his “right view” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>sammādiṭṭhi</i>) is grounded in what he has “seen” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>diṭṭha</i>).

## mn72:15.3
- **PLI**: Diṭṭhañhetaṁ, vaccha, tathāgatena:
- **EN**: For a realized one has seen:

## mn72:15.4
- **PLI**: ‘iti rūpaṁ, iti rūpassa samudayo, iti rūpassa atthaṅgamo;
- **EN**: ‘Such is form, such is the origin of form, such is the disappearance of form.

## mn72:15.5
- **PLI**: iti vedanā, iti vedanāya samudayo, iti vedanāya atthaṅgamo;
- **EN**: Such is feeling, such is the origin of feeling, such is the disappearance of feeling.

## mn72:15.6
- **PLI**: iti saññā, iti saññāya samudayo, iti saññāya atthaṅgamo;
- **EN**: Such is perception, such is the origin of perception, such is the disappearance of perception.

## mn72:15.7
- **PLI**: iti saṅkhārā, iti saṅkhārānaṁ samudayo, iti saṅkhārānaṁ atthaṅgamo;
- **EN**: Such are choices, such is the origin of choices, such is the disappearance of choices.

## mn72:15.8
- **PLI**: iti viññāṇaṁ, iti viññāṇassa samudayo, iti viññāṇassa atthaṅgamo’ti.
- **EN**: Such is consciousness, such is the origin of consciousness, such is the disappearance of consciousness.’

## mn72:15.9
- **PLI**: Tasmā tathāgato sabbamaññitānaṁ sabbamathitānaṁ sabbaahaṅkāramamaṅkāramānānusayānaṁ khayā virāgā nirodhā cāgā paṭinissaggā anupādā vimuttoti vadāmī”ti.
- **EN**: That’s why a Realized One is freed by not grasping with the ending, fading away, cessation, giving up, and letting go of all conceiving, all churning, and all I-making, mine-making, or underlying tendency to conceit, I say.”
- **NOTE**: At <a href='https://suttacentral.net/thag14.2/en/sujato'>Thag 14.2</a>, <i lang='pi' translate='no'>mathita</i> means “oppressed, weighed down”, and the same sense probably applies at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/thag1.102/en/sujato'>Thag 1.102</a>. At <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dhp349/en/sujato'>Dhp 349</a>, however, the root sense of “churn” fits the context of “thoughts”, while at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn90/en/sujato#12.14'>MN 90:12.14</a> it refers to starting a fire by “churning” with a fire-drill, a common Vedic usage (Rig Veda 3.23.1a, 3.29.12a, 8.48.6a; Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa 2.1.4.8, 3.7.3.3, etc.). Its unique appearance in this context anticipates the simile of fire.
