# Phần 4  (9 đoạn)

## mn72:20.20
- **PLI**: Upapajjatīti na upeti, na upapajjatīti na upeti, upapajjati ca na ca upapajjatīti na upeti, neva upapajjati na na upapajjatīti na upetī”ti.
- **EN**: ‘They’re reborn’, ‘they’re not reborn’, ‘they’re both reborn and not reborn’, ‘they’re neither reborn nor not reborn’—none of these apply.”

## mn72:21.1  `cck13.253, dr13.228, ms10M_557, ndp10.183, nya21, pts-vp-pli1.489, sya13.249`
- **PLI**: Evaṁ vutte, vacchagotto paribbājako bhagavantaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: When he said this, the wanderer Vacchagotta said to the Buddha:

## mn72:21.2
- **PLI**: “seyyathāpi, bho gotama, gāmassa vā nigamassa vā avidūre mahāsālarukkho.
- **EN**: “Worthy Gotama, suppose there was a large sal tree not far from a town or village.

## mn72:21.3
- **PLI**: Tassa aniccatā sākhāpalāsā palujjeyyuṁ, tacapapaṭikā palujjeyyuṁ, pheggū palujjeyyuṁ;
- **EN**: And because it’s impermanent, its branches and foliage, bark and shoots, and softwood would fall off.

## mn72:21.4
- **PLI**: so aparena samayena apagatasākhāpalāso apagatatacapapaṭiko apagataphegguko suddho assa, sāre patiṭṭhito;
- **EN**: After some time it would be rid of branches and foliage, bark and shoots, and softwood, pure, and consolidated in the core.

## mn72:21.5
- **PLI**: evameva bhoto gotamassa pāvacanaṁ apagatasākhāpalāsaṁ apagatatacapapaṭikaṁ apagatapheggukaṁ suddhaṁ, sāre patiṭṭhitaṁ.
- **EN**: In the same way, worthy Gotama’s dispensation is rid of branches and foliage, bark and shoots, and softwood, pure, and consolidated in the core.

## mn72:22.1  `nya22`
- **PLI**: Abhikkantaṁ, bho gotama …pe…
- **EN**: Excellent, worthy Gotama! …

## mn72:22.2
- **PLI**: upāsakaṁ maṁ bhavaṁ gotamo dhāretu ajjatagge pāṇupetaṁ saraṇaṁ gatan”ti.
- **EN**: From this day forth, may the worthy Gotama remember me as a lay follower who has gone for refuge for life.”
- **NOTE**: Vacchagotta had already gone forth as a wanderer, so it is surprising that he calls himself a “lay follower” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>upāsaka</i>). The Chinese parallels simply say that he left without taking refuge (SA 962 at T ii 246a17 and SA2 196 at T ii 445c8). Given that the Pali tradition repeats him going for refuge in the next sutta (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn73/en/sujato#15.2'>MN 73:15.2</a>), it seems likely that this detail was added by mistake here.

## mn72:22.3
- **PLI**: Aggivacchasuttaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ dutiyaṁ.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)
