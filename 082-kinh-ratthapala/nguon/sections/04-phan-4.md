## mn82:22.2

## mn82:22.5

- **EN**: “If you’d follow my advice, householder, you’d have this heap of gold, both coined and uncoined, loaded on a cart and carried away to be dumped in the middle of the Ganges River.

## mn82:23.1  `nya23`

- **EN**: Then Raṭṭhapāla’s former wives each clasped his feet and said,

## mn82:23.2
- **PLI**: “kīdisā nāma tā, ayyaputta, accharāyo yāsaṁ tvaṁ hetu brahmacariyaṁ carasī”ti?
- **EN**: “What are they like, master, the nymphs for whom you lead the spiritual life?”

## mn82:23.3
- **PLI**: “Na kho mayaṁ, bhaginī, accharānaṁ hetu brahmacariyaṁ carāmā”ti.
- **EN**: “Sisters, I don’t lead the spiritual life for the sake of nymphs.”
- **NOTE**: Raṭṭhapāla refers to his former wives as sisters, an indication that he sees them in accordance with the advice in <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn35.127/en/sujato#1.6'>SN 35.127:1.6</a>, to regard women of one’s one age as a sister. Notably, he has already used the same respectful term when addressing the household maid, whereas his mother uses the disrespectful “wench” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>je</i>).

## mn82:23.4

- **EN**: Saying, “Our master Raṭṭhapāla addresses us as sisters!” they fainted right away.
