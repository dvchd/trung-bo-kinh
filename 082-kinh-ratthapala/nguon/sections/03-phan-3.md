## mn82:11.11

## mn82:13.1  `nya13`

- **EN**: And Raṭṭhapāla received the going forth, the ordination in the Buddha’s presence.

## mn82:13.2
- **PLI**: Atha kho bhagavā acirūpasampanne āyasmante raṭṭhapāle aḍḍhamāsupasampanne thullakoṭṭhike yathābhirantaṁ viharitvā yena sāvatthi tena cārikaṁ pakkāmi.
- **EN**: Not long after Venerable Raṭṭhapāla’s ordination, a fortnight later, the Buddha—having stayed in Thullakoṭṭhika as long as he pleased—set out for Sāvatthī.

## mn82:13.3
- **PLI**: Anupubbena cārikaṁ caramāno yena sāvatthi tadavasari.
- **EN**: Traveling stage by stage, he arrived at Sāvatthī,

## mn82:13.4
- **PLI**: Tatra sudaṁ bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme.
- **EN**: where he stayed in Jeta’s Grove, Anāthapiṇḍika’s monastery.

## mn82:14.1  `nya14`
- **PLI**: Atha kho āyasmā raṭṭhapālo eko vūpakaṭṭho appamatto ātāpī pahitatto viharanto nacirasseva—yassatthāya kulaputtā sammadeva agārasmā anagāriyaṁ pabbajanti, tadanuttaraṁ—brahmacariyapariyosānaṁ diṭṭheva dhamme sayaṁ abhiññā sacchikatvā upasampajja vihāsi.
- **EN**: Then Venerable Raṭṭhapāla, living alone, withdrawn, diligent, keen, and resolute, soon realized the supreme culmination of the spiritual path in this very life. He lived having achieved with his own insight the goal for which gentlemen rightly go forth from the lay life to homelessness.
- **NOTE**: While the conventional phrase “soon” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>nacirasseva</i>) is used, the commentary says it was twelve years, which would explain why his family did not immediately recognize him.

## mn82:14.2
- **PLI**: “Khīṇā jāti, vusitaṁ brahmacariyaṁ, kataṁ karaṇīyaṁ, nāparaṁ itthattāyā”ti abbhaññāsi.
- **EN**: He understood: “Rebirth is ended; the spiritual journey has been completed; what had to be done has been done; there is nothing further for this place.”

## mn82:14.3
- **PLI**: Aññataro kho panāyasmā raṭṭhapālo arahataṁ ahosi.
- **EN**: And Venerable Raṭṭhapāla became one of the perfected.

## mn82:16.1  `nya16`

- **EN**: And then Raṭṭhapāla rose from his seat, bowed, and respectfully circled the Buddha, keeping him on his right. Then he set his lodgings in order and, taking his bowl and robe, set out for Thullakoṭṭhika.

## mn82:16.2

- **EN**: Then Raṭṭhapāla robed up in the morning and, taking his bowl and robe, entered Thullakoṭṭhika for alms.

## mn82:17.1  `nya17`
- **PLI**: Tena kho pana samayena āyasmato raṭṭhapālassa pitā majjhimāya dvārasālāya ullikhāpeti.
- **EN**: Now at that time Raṭṭhapāla’s father was having his hair dressed in the hall of the middle gate.
- **NOTE**: This is a sign of the dandified life expected of Raṭṭhapāla. Hairstyles for upper class men of the time were quite fancy, or at least they are depicted so in the early sculptures at Sanchi.

## mn82:17.2

## mn82:18.4
