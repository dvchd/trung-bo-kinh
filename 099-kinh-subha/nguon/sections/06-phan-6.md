- **EN**: Now at that time the brahmin Jānussoṇi drove out from Sāvatthī in the middle of the day in an all-white chariot drawn by mares.

## mn99:30.2
- **PLI**: Addasā kho jāṇussoṇi brāhmaṇo subhaṁ māṇavaṁ todeyyaputtaṁ dūratova āgacchantaṁ.
- **EN**: He saw the student Subha coming off in the distance,

## mn99:30.3
- **PLI**: Disvāna subhaṁ māṇavaṁ todeyyaputtaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: and said to him,

## mn99:30.4
- **PLI**: “handa kuto nu bhavaṁ bhāradvājo āgacchati divā divassā”ti?
- **EN**: “So, worthy Bhāradvāja, where are you coming from in the middle of the day?”

## mn99:30.5
- **PLI**: “Ito hi kho ahaṁ, bho, āgacchāmi samaṇassa gotamassa santikā”ti.
- **EN**: “Just now, good fellow, I’ve come from the presence of the ascetic Gotama.”

## mn99:30.6

- **EN**: “What do you think of the ascetic Gotama’s lucidity of wisdom? Do you think he’s astute?”

## mn99:30.7
- **PLI**: “Ko cāhaṁ, bho, ko ca samaṇassa gotamassa paññāveyyattiyaṁ jānissāmi?
- **EN**: “Worthy sir, who am I to judge the ascetic Gotama’s lucidity of wisdom?

## mn99:30.8
- **PLI**: Sopi nūnassa tādisova yo samaṇassa gotamassa paññāveyyattiyaṁ jāneyyā”ti.
- **EN**: You’d really have to be on the same level to judge his lucidity of wisdom.”

## mn99:30.9
- **PLI**: “Uḷārāya khalu bhavaṁ bhāradvājo samaṇaṁ gotamaṁ pasaṁsāya pasaṁsatī”ti.
- **EN**: “Worthy Bhāradvāja praises the ascetic Gotama with high praise indeed.”

## mn99:30.10

- **EN**: “Who am I to praise the ascetic Gotama?

## mn99:30.11

- **EN**: He is praised by the praised as the best among gods and humans.
