## mn99:24.1  `cck13.641, ms10M_1507, msdiv471, ndp10.480, nya24, vri13.431`
- **PLI**: “Katamo ca, māṇava, brahmānaṁ sahabyatāya maggo?
- **EN**: “And what is a path to company with divinity?

## mn99:24.2
- **PLI**: Idha, māṇava, bhikkhu mettāsahagatena cetasā ekaṁ disaṁ pharitvā viharati, tathā dutiyaṁ, tathā tatiyaṁ, tathā catutthaṁ; iti uddhamadho tiriyaṁ sabbadhi sabbattatāya sabbāvantaṁ lokaṁ mettāsahagatena cetasā vipulena mahaggatena appamāṇena averena abyābajjhena pharitvā viharati.
- **EN**: Firstly, a mendicant meditates spreading a heart full of love to one direction, and to the second, and to the third, and to the fourth. In the same way above, below, across, everywhere, all around, they spread a heart full of love to the whole world—abundant, expansive, limitless, free of enmity and ill will.

## mn99:24.3
- **PLI**: Evaṁ bhāvitāya kho, māṇava, mettāya cetovimuttiyā yaṁ pamāṇakataṁ kammaṁ na taṁ tatrāvasissati, na taṁ tatrāvatiṭṭhati.
- **EN**: When the heart’s release by love has been developed like this, any limited deeds they’ve done don’t remain or persist there.

## mn99:24.4
- **PLI**: Seyyathāpi, māṇava, balavā saṅkhadhamo appakasireneva cātuddisā viññāpeyya;
- **EN**: Suppose there was a powerful horn blower. They’d easily make themselves heard in the four quarters.

## mn99:24.5
- **PLI**: evameva kho, māṇava …pe… evaṁ bhāvitāya kho, māṇava, mettāya cetovimuttiyā yaṁ pamāṇakataṁ kammaṁ na taṁ tatrāvasissati, na taṁ tatrāvatiṭṭhati.
- **EN**: In the same way, when the heart’s release by love has been developed like this, any limited deeds they’ve done don’t remain or persist there.
- **NOTE**: The mind in <i lang='pi' translate='no'>jhāna</i> is so powerful that it effectively overrides any limited kamma, including ordinary good or bad deeds. Unless they have committed serious crimes with a fixed kammic result such as matricide, etc., the meditator will be reborn in a Brahmā realm.

## mn99:24.6
- **PLI**: Ayampi kho, māṇava, brahmānaṁ sahabyatāya maggo.
- **EN**: This is a path to company with divinity.
