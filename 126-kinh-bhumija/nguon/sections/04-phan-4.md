# Phần 4  (29 đoạn)

## mn126:17.5
- **PLI**: nevāsaṁ nānāsañcepi karitvā dadhiṁ kalase āsiñcitvā matthena āviñcheyya, bhabbo navanītassa adhigamāya.
- **EN**: or neither did nor did not make a wish.

## mn126:17.6
- **PLI**: Taṁ kissa hetu?
- **EN**: Why is that?

## mn126:17.7
- **PLI**: Yoni hesā, bhūmija, navanītassa adhigamāya.
- **EN**: Because that’s a rational way to produce butter.

## mn126:19.1  `bj12.330, csp1ed11.184, csp2ed11.184, ms11M_611, nya19, sya14.283, vri14.184`
- **PLI**: Sace kho taṁ, bhūmija, jayasenassa rājakumārassa imā catasso upamā paṭibhāyeyyuṁ anacchariyaṁ te jayaseno rājakumāro pasīdeyya, pasanno ca te pasannākāraṁ kareyyā”ti.
- **EN**: Bhūmija, it would be no wonder if, had these four similes occurred to you, Prince Jayasena would have gained confidence in you and shown his confidence.”
- **NOTE**: Above, Jayasena expresses his confidence in the Buddha, but not in Bhūmija (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn126/en/sujato#5.1'>MN 126:5.1</a>).

## mn126:19.2
- **PLI**: “Kuto pana maṁ, bhante, jayasenassa rājakumārassa imā catasso upamā paṭibhāyissanti anacchariyā pubbe assutapubbā, seyyathāpi bhagavantan”ti?
- **EN**: “But sir, how could these four similes have occurred to me as they did to the Buddha, since they were neither supernaturally inspired, nor learned before in the past?”
- **NOTE**: As at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn125/en/sujato#11.2'>MN 125:11.2</a>.
