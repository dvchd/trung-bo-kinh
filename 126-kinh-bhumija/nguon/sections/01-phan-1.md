## mn126:1.2
- **PLI**: ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā rājagahe viharati veḷuvane kalandakanivāpe.
- **EN**: At one time the Buddha was staying near Rājagaha in the Bamboo Grove, the squirrels’ feeding ground.

## mn126:2.1  `nya2`
- **PLI**: Atha kho āyasmā bhūmijo pubbaṇhasamayaṁ nivāsetvā pattacīvaramādāya yena jayasenassa rājakumārassa nivesanaṁ tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā paññatte āsane nisīdi.
- **EN**: Then Venerable Bhūmija robed up in the morning and, taking his bowl and robe, went to the home of Prince Jayasena, where he sat down on the seat spread out.
- **NOTE**: We know Bhūmija from <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn12.25/en/sujato'>SN 12.25</a>, where he asks Sāriputta about the efficacy of deeds. | This is the second discourse featuring Jayasena (after <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn125/en/sujato'>MN 125</a>), but despite his evident interest in the Dhamma, and his support for mendicants expressed by offering the meal here, we do not hear of his conversion or spiritual development.

## mn126:3.1  `nya3`
- **PLI**: Atha kho jayaseno rājakumāro yenāyasmā bhūmijo tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā āyasmatā bhūmijena saddhiṁ sammodi.
- **EN**: Then Jayasena approached and exchanged greetings with him.

## mn126:3.2
- **PLI**: Sammodanīyaṁ kathaṁ sāraṇīyaṁ vītisāretvā ekamantaṁ nisīdi. Ekamantaṁ nisinno kho jayaseno rājakumāro āyasmantaṁ bhūmijaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: When the greetings and polite conversation were over, he sat down to one side and said to Bhūmija:

## mn126:3.3
- **PLI**: “santi, bho bhūmija, eke samaṇabrāhmaṇā evaṁvādino evaṁdiṭṭhino:
- **EN**: “Worthy Bhūmija, there are some ascetics and brahmins who have this doctrine and view:

## mn126:3.4
- **PLI**: ‘āsañcepi karitvā brahmacariyaṁ caranti, abhabbā phalassa adhigamāya;
- **EN**: ‘If you make a wish and lead the spiritual life, you can’t win the fruit.
- **NOTE**: “Wish” is <i lang='pi' translate='no'>āsa</i>.

## mn126:3.5
- **PLI**: anāsañcepi karitvā brahmacariyaṁ caranti, abhabbā phalassa adhigamāya;
- **EN**: If you don’t make a wish and lead the spiritual life, you can’t win the fruit.

## mn126:3.6
- **PLI**: āsañca anāsañcepi karitvā brahmacariyaṁ caranti, abhabbā phalassa adhigamāya;
- **EN**: If you both make a wish and don’t make a wish and lead the spiritual life, you can’t win the fruit.

## mn126:3.7
- **PLI**: nevāsaṁ nānāsañcepi karitvā brahmacariyaṁ caranti, abhabbā phalassa adhigamāyā’ti.
- **EN**: If you neither make a wish nor don’t make a wish and lead the spiritual life, you can’t win the fruit.’

## mn126:3.8
- **PLI**: Idha bhoto bhūmijassa satthā kiṁvādī kimakkhāyī”ti?
- **EN**: What does the worthy Bhūmija’s Teacher say about this? How does he explain it?”

## mn126:4.1  `nya4`
- **PLI**: “Na kho metaṁ, rājakumāra, bhagavato sammukhā sutaṁ, sammukhā paṭiggahitaṁ.
- **EN**: “Prince, I haven’t heard and learned this in the presence of the Buddha.
- **NOTE**: Like Aciravata in <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn125/en/sujato#3.2'>MN 125:3.2</a>, he is cautious in responding.

## mn126:4.2
- **PLI**: Ṭhānañca kho etaṁ vijjati yaṁ bhagavā evaṁ byākareyya:
- **EN**: But it’s possible that he might explain it like this:

## mn126:4.3
- **PLI**: ‘āsañcepi karitvā ayoniso brahmacariyaṁ caranti, abhabbā phalassa adhigamāya;
- **EN**: ‘If you lead the spiritual life irrationally, you can’t win the fruit, regardless of whether you make a wish,
- **NOTE**: “Irrationally” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>ayoniso</i>) here means, as the sutta will make clear, that the practices one does are not “rationally” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>yoniso</i>) or causally connected to the results one expects. This sutta illuminates the meaning of the key term <i lang='pi' translate='no'>yoniso</i> through a series of similes.

## mn126:4.4
- **PLI**: anāsañcepi karitvā ayoniso brahmacariyaṁ caranti, abhabbā phalassa adhigamāya;
- **EN**: you don’t make a wish,

## mn126:4.5
- **PLI**: āsañca anāsañcepi karitvā ayoniso brahmacariyaṁ caranti, abhabbā phalassa adhigamāya;
- **EN**: you both do and do not make a wish,

## mn126:4.6
- **PLI**: nevāsaṁ nānāsañcepi karitvā ayoniso brahmacariyaṁ caranti, abhabbā phalassa adhigamāya.
- **EN**: or you neither do nor don’t make a wish.

## mn126:4.7
- **PLI**: Āsañcepi karitvā yoniso brahmacariyaṁ caranti, bhabbā phalassa adhigamāya;

## mn126:5.1  `nya5`
- **PLI**: “Sace kho bhoto bhūmijassa satthā evaṁvādī evamakkhāyī, addhā bhoto bhūmijassa satthā sabbesaṁyeva puthusamaṇabrāhmaṇānaṁ muddhānaṁ maññe āhacca tiṭṭhatī”ti.
- **EN**: “If that’s what your teacher says, worthy Bhūmija, he clearly stands head and shoulders above all the various other ascetics and brahmins.”
