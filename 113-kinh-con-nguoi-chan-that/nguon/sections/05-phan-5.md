# Phần 5  (16 đoạn)

## mn113:27.10
- **PLI**: Ayampi, bhikkhave, sappurisadhammo.
- **EN**: This too is a quality of a true person.  (= mn113:4-6.13)

## mn113:28.1  `bj12.164, cck14.136, csp1ed11.93, csp2ed11.93, dr13.131, ms11M_297, nya28, sya14.144, vri14.93`
- **PLI**: Puna caparaṁ, bhikkhave, asappuriso sabbaso ākiñcaññāyatanaṁ samatikkamma nevasaññānāsaññāyatanaṁ upasampajja viharati.
- **EN**: the dimension of neither perception nor non-perception.

## mn113:28.2
- **PLI**: So iti paṭisañcikkhati:
- **EN**: They reflect:

## mn113:28.3
- **PLI**: ‘ahaṁ khomhi nevasaññānāsaññāyatanasamāpattiyā lābhī, ime panaññe bhikkhū nevasaññānāsaññāyatanasamāpattiyā na lābhino’ti.
- **EN**: ‘I have attained the dimension of neither perception nor non-perception, unlike these other mendicants.’

## mn113:28.4
- **PLI**: So tāya nevasaññānāsaññāyatanasamāpattiyā attānukkaṁseti, paraṁ vambheti.
- **EN**: And they glorify themselves and put others down on account of that.

## mn113:28.5
- **PLI**: Ayampi, bhikkhave, asappurisadhammo.
- **EN**: This too is a quality of an untrue person.

## mn113:28.6
- **PLI**: Sappuriso ca kho, bhikkhave, iti paṭisañcikkhati:
- **EN**: A true person reflects:

## mn113:28.7
- **PLI**: ‘nevasaññānāsaññāyatanasamāpattiyāpi kho atammayatā vuttā bhagavatā.
- **EN**: ‘The Buddha has spoken of not being determined even by the attainment of the dimension of neither perception nor non-perception.

## mn113:28.8
- **PLI**: Yena yena hi maññanti tato taṁ hoti aññathā’ti.
- **EN**: For however they imagine it, it turns out to be something else.’

## mn113:28.9
- **PLI**: So atammayataññeva antaraṁ karitvā tāya nevasaññānāsaññāyatanasamāpattiyā nevattānukkaṁseti, na paraṁ vambheti.
- **EN**: Keeping to themselves the fact that they are not determined by that, they don’t glorify themselves and put others down on account of their attainment of the dimension of neither perception nor non-perception.

## mn113:28.10
- **PLI**: Ayampi, bhikkhave, sappurisadhammo.
- **EN**: This too is a quality of a true person.

## mn113:29.1  `ms11M_298, nya29, pts-vp-pli3.45`
- **PLI**: Puna caparaṁ, bhikkhave, sappuriso sabbaso nevasaññānāsaññāyatanaṁ samatikkamma saññāvedayitanirodhaṁ upasampajja viharati. Paññāya cassa disvā āsavā parikkhīṇā honti.
- **EN**: Furthermore, take a true person who, going totally beyond the dimension of neither perception nor non-perception, enters and remains in the cessation of perception and feeling. And, having seen with wisdom, their defilements come to an end.

## mn113:29.2
- **PLI**: Ayaṁ, bhikkhave, bhikkhu na kiñci maññati, na kuhiñci maññati, na kenaci maññatī”ti.
- **EN**: This is a mendicant who does not conceive with anything, does not conceive regarding anything, does not conceive through anything.”

## mn113:29.3  `ms11M_299`
- **PLI**: Idamavoca bhagavā.
- **EN**: That is what the Buddha said.

## mn113:29.4
- **PLI**: Attamanā te bhikkhū bhagavato bhāsitaṁ abhinandunti.
- **EN**: Satisfied, the mendicants approved what the Buddha said.

## mn113:29.5
- **PLI**: Sappurisasuttaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ tatiyaṁ.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)
