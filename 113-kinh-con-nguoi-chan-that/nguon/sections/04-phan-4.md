# Phần 4  (50 đoạn)

## mn113:21.2
- **PLI**: So iti paṭisañcikkhati:
- **EN**: They reflect:

## mn113:21.3
- **PLI**: ‘ahaṁ khomhi paṭhamajjhānasamāpattiyā lābhī, ime panaññe bhikkhū paṭhamajjhānasamāpattiyā na lābhino’ti.
- **EN**: ‘I have attained the first absorption, unlike these other mendicants.’

## mn113:21.4
- **PLI**: So tāya paṭhamajjhānasamāpattiyā attānukkaṁseti, paraṁ vambheti.
- **EN**: And they glorify themselves and put others down on account of that.

## mn113:21.5
- **PLI**: Ayampi, bhikkhave, asappurisadhammo.
- **EN**: This too is a quality of an untrue person.

## mn113:21.6
- **PLI**: Sappuriso ca kho, bhikkhave, iti paṭisañcikkhati:
- **EN**: A true person reflects:

## mn113:21.7
- **PLI**: ‘paṭhamajjhānasamāpattiyāpi kho atammayatā vuttā bhagavatā.
- **EN**: ‘The Buddha has spoken of not being determined even by the attainment of the first absorption.
- **NOTE**: For <i lang='pi' translate='no'>atammayatā</i> see <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn47/en/sujato#13.4'>MN 47:13.4</a> and my note there. Here it means that one’s spiritual goal is not limited to this attainment.

## mn113:21.8
- **PLI**: Yena yena hi maññanti tato taṁ hoti aññathā’ti.
- **EN**: For however they imagine it, it turns out to be something else.’
- **NOTE**: This saying also appears at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/snp3.8/en/sujato#15.1'>Snp 3.8:15.1</a> and <a href='https://suttacentral.net/snp3.12/en/sujato#52.1'>Snp 3.12:52.1</a>, where it is not connected with absorption practice.

## mn113:21.9
- **PLI**: So atammayataññeva antaraṁ karitvā tāya paṭhamajjhānasamāpattiyā nevattānukkaṁseti, na paraṁ vambheti.
- **EN**: Keeping to themselves the fact that they are not determined by that, they don’t glorify themselves and put others down on account of their attainment of the first absorption.

## mn113:21.10
- **PLI**: Ayampi, bhikkhave, sappurisadhammo.
- **EN**: This too is a quality of a true person.
