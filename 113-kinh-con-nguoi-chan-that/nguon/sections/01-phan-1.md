# Phần 1  (50 đoạn)

## mn113:1.1  `bj12.154, cck14.126, dr13.122, ms11M_279, msdiv105, ndp11.101, nya1, sya14.134, vri14.85`
- **PLI**: Evaṁ me sutaṁ—
- **EN**: So I have heard.

## mn113:1.2
- **PLI**: ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme.
- **EN**: At one time the Buddha was staying near Sāvatthī in Jeta’s Grove, Anāthapiṇḍika’s monastery.

## mn113:1.3
- **PLI**: Tatra kho bhagavā bhikkhū āmantesi:
- **EN**: There the Buddha addressed the mendicants,

## mn113:1.4
- **PLI**: “bhikkhavo”ti.
- **EN**: “Mendicants!”

## mn113:1.5
- **PLI**: “Bhadante”ti te bhikkhū bhagavato paccassosuṁ.
- **EN**: “Venerable sir,” they replied.

## mn113:1.6
- **PLI**: Bhagavā etadavoca:
- **EN**: The Buddha said this:

## mn113:2.1  `nya2`
- **PLI**: “sappurisadhammañca vo, bhikkhave, desessāmi asappurisadhammañca.
- **EN**: “Mendicants, I will teach you the qualities of a true person and the qualities of an untrue person.

## mn113:2.2
- **PLI**: Taṁ suṇātha, sādhukaṁ manasi karotha, bhāsissāmī”ti.
- **EN**: Listen and apply your mind well, I will speak.”

## mn113:2.3
- **PLI**: “Evaṁ, bhante”ti kho te bhikkhū bhagavato paccassosuṁ.
- **EN**: “Yes, sir,” they replied.

## mn113:2.4
- **PLI**: Bhagavā etadavoca:
- **EN**: The Buddha said this:

## mn113:3.1  `csp1ed11.87, csp2ed11.87, ms11M_280, nya3, pts-vp-pli3.38`
- **PLI**: “Katamo ca, bhikkhave, asappurisadhammo?
- **EN**: “And what is a quality of an untrue person?

## mn113:3.2
- **PLI**: Idha, bhikkhave, asappuriso uccākulā pabbajito hoti.
- **EN**: Take an untrue person who has gone forth from an eminent family.

## mn113:3.3
- **PLI**: So iti paṭisañcikkhati:
- **EN**: They reflect:

## mn113:3.4
- **PLI**: ‘ahaṁ khomhi uccākulā pabbajito, ime panaññe bhikkhū na uccākulā pabbajitā’ti.
- **EN**: ‘I have gone forth from an eminent family, unlike these other mendicants.’

## mn113:3.5
- **PLI**: So tāya uccākulīnatāya attānukkaṁseti, paraṁ vambheti.
- **EN**: And they glorify themselves and put others down on account of that.

## mn113:3.6
- **PLI**: Ayaṁ, bhikkhave, asappurisadhammo.
- **EN**: This is a quality of an untrue person.

## mn113:3.7
- **PLI**: Sappuriso ca kho, bhikkhave, iti paṭisañcikkhati:
- **EN**: A true person reflects:

## mn113:3.8
- **PLI**: ‘na kho uccākulīnatāya lobhadhammā vā parikkhayaṁ gacchanti, dosadhammā vā parikkhayaṁ gacchanti, mohadhammā vā parikkhayaṁ gacchanti.
- **EN**: ‘It’s not because of one’s eminent family that thoughts of greed, hate, or delusion come to an end.

## mn113:3.9
- **PLI**: No cepi uccākulā pabbajito hoti;
- **EN**: Even if someone has not gone forth from an eminent family,

## mn113:3.10
- **PLI**: so ca hoti dhammānudhammappaṭipanno sāmīcippaṭipanno anudhammacārī, so tattha pujjo, so tattha pāsaṁso’ti.
- **EN**: if they practice in line with the teaching, practice properly, and live in line with the teaching, they are worthy of honor and praise for that.’

## mn113:3.11
- **PLI**: So paṭipadaṁyeva antaraṁ karitvā tāya uccākulīnatāya nevattānukkaṁseti na paraṁ vambheti.
- **EN**: Keeping the practice to themselves, they don’t glorify themselves and put others down on account of their eminent family.
- **NOTE**: By rejecting social measures of worth and focusing on inner purity, they are the opposite of the meditator at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn108/en/sujato#26.4'>MN 108:26.4</a>, who hides defilements within.

## mn113:3.12
- **PLI**: Ayaṁ, bhikkhave, sappurisadhammo.
- **EN**: This is a quality of a true person.
