# Phần 2  (50 đoạn)

## mn113:8.5
- **PLI**: Ayampi, bhikkhave, asappurisadhammo.
- **EN**: This too is a quality of an untrue person.

## mn113:8.6
- **PLI**: Sappuriso ca kho, bhikkhave, iti paṭisañcikkhati:
- **EN**: A true person reflects:

## mn113:8.7
- **PLI**: ‘na kho lābhena lobhadhammā vā parikkhayaṁ gacchanti, dosadhammā vā parikkhayaṁ gacchanti, mohadhammā vā parikkhayaṁ gacchanti.
- **EN**: ‘It’s not because of one’s material things that thoughts of greed, hate, or delusion come to an end.

## mn113:8.8
- **PLI**: No cepi lābhī hoti cīvarapiṇḍapātasenāsanagilānappaccayabhesajjaparikkhārānaṁ;
- **EN**: Even if someone doesn’t receive robes, almsfood, lodgings, and medicines and supplies for the sick,

## mn113:8.9
- **PLI**: so ca hoti dhammānudhammappaṭipanno sāmīcippaṭipanno anudhammacārī, so tattha pujjo, so tattha pāsaṁso’ti.
- **EN**: if they practice in line with the teaching, practice properly, and live in line with the teaching, they are worthy of honor and praise for that.’

## mn113:8.10
- **PLI**: So paṭipadaṁyeva antaraṁ karitvā tena lābhena nevattānukkaṁseti, na paraṁ vambheti.
- **EN**: Keeping the practice to themselves, they don’t glorify themselves and put others down on account of their material things.

## mn113:8.11
- **PLI**: Ayampi, bhikkhave, sappurisadhammo.
- **EN**: This too is a quality of a true person.
