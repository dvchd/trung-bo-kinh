# Phần 4  (14 đoạn)

## mn28:38.2
- **PLI**: Yato ca kho, āvuso, ajjhattiko ceva mano aparibhinno hoti, bāhirā ca dhammā āpāthaṁ āgacchanti, tajjo ca samannāhāro hoti, evaṁ tajjassa viññāṇabhāgassa pātubhāvo hoti.
- **EN**: But when the mind is intact internally and exterior ideas come into range and there is corresponding engagement, there is the manifestation of the corresponding type of consciousness.

## mn28:38.3
- **PLI**: Yaṁ tathābhūtassa rūpaṁ taṁ rūpupādānakkhandhe saṅgahaṁ gacchati, yā tathābhūtassa vedanā sā vedanupādānakkhandhe saṅgahaṁ gacchati, yā tathābhūtassa saññā sā saññupādānakkhandhe saṅgahaṁ gacchati, ye tathābhūtassa saṅkhārā te saṅkhārupādānakkhandhe saṅgahaṁ gacchanti, yaṁ tathābhūtassa viññāṇaṁ taṁ viññāṇupādānakkhandhe saṅgahaṁ gacchati.
- **EN**: The form produced in this way is included in the grasping aggregate of form. The feeling, perception, choices, and consciousness produced in this way are each included in the corresponding grasping aggregate.

## mn28:38.4
- **PLI**: So evaṁ pajānāti:
- **EN**: They understand:

## mn28:38.5
- **PLI**: ‘evañhi kira imesaṁ pañcannaṁ upādānakkhandhānaṁ saṅgaho sannipāto samavāyo hoti.
- **EN**: ‘So this is how there comes to be inclusion, gathering together, and assimilation into these five grasping aggregates.

## mn28:38.6
- **PLI**: Vuttaṁ kho panetaṁ bhagavatā:
- **EN**: But the Buddha has also said:

## mn28:38.7
- **PLI**: “yo paṭiccasamuppādaṁ passati so dhammaṁ passati;
- **EN**: “One who sees dependent origination sees the teaching.

## mn28:38.8
- **PLI**: yo dhammaṁ passati so paṭiccasamuppādaṁ passatī”ti.
- **EN**: One who sees the teaching sees dependent origination.”

## mn28:38.9
- **PLI**: Paṭiccasamuppannā kho panime yadidaṁ pañcupādānakkhandhā.
- **EN**: And these five grasping aggregates are indeed dependently originated.

## mn28:38.10
- **PLI**: Yo imesu pañcasu upādānakkhandhesu chando ālayo anunayo ajjhosānaṁ so dukkhasamudayo.
- **EN**: The desire, clinging, attraction, and attachment for these five grasping aggregates is the origin of suffering.

## mn28:38.11
- **PLI**: Yo imesu pañcasu upādānakkhandhesu chandarāgavinayo chandarāgappahānaṁ so dukkhanirodho’ti.
- **EN**: Giving up and getting rid of desire and greed for these five grasping aggregates is the cessation of suffering.’

## mn28:38.12
- **PLI**: Ettāvatāpi kho, āvuso, bhikkhuno bahukataṁ hotī”ti.
- **EN**: At this point, much has been done by that mendicant.”

## mn28:38.13  `ms9M_1075, sya12.361`
- **PLI**: Idamavoca āyasmā sāriputto.
- **EN**: That is what Venerable Sāriputta said.

## mn28:38.14
- **PLI**: Attamanā te bhikkhū āyasmato sāriputtassa bhāsitaṁ abhinandunti.
- **EN**: Satisfied, the mendicants approved what Sāriputta said.

## mn28:38.15
- **PLI**: Mahāhatthipadopamasuttaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ aṭṭhamaṁ.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)
