## mn96:2.1  `nya2`
- **PLI**: Atha kho esukārī brāhmaṇo yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavatā saddhiṁ sammodi.
- **EN**: Then Esukārī the brahmin went up to the Buddha, and exchanged greetings with him.
- **NOTE**: This brahmin appears only here, and there is little to distinguish him personally. A king of the same name features in a Jātaka (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/ja509/en/sujato'>Ja 509</a>). Could <i lang='pi' translate='no'>esukārī</i> be equivalent to <i lang='pi' translate='no'>ayaskārī</i>, “blacksmith”?

## mn96:2.2

- **EN**: When the greetings and polite conversation were over, he sat down to one side and said to the Buddha:

## mn96:3.1  `nya3`
- **PLI**: “brāhmaṇā, bho gotama, catasso pāricariyā paññapenti—
- **EN**: “Worthy Gotama, the brahmins prescribe four kinds of service:
- **NOTE**: To “serve” can be to look after, nurse, or care for (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/an5.76/en/sujato#3.5'>AN 5.76:3.5</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn31/en/sujato#29.2'>DN 31:29.2</a>), to offer religious devotions (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn12/en/sujato#61.2'>MN 12:61.2</a>), or even provide sexual favors (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-bu-vb-ss4/en/sujato#1.2.7'>Bu Ss 4:1.2.7</a>).

## mn96:3.2
- **PLI**: brāhmaṇassa pāricariyaṁ paññapenti, khattiyassa pāricariyaṁ paññapenti, vessassa pāricariyaṁ paññapenti, suddassa pāricariyaṁ paññapenti.
- **EN**: for a brahmin, an aristocrat, a peasant, and a menial.

## mn96:3.3
- **PLI**: Tatridaṁ, bho gotama, brāhmaṇā brāhmaṇassa pāricariyaṁ paññapenti:
- **EN**: This is the service they prescribe for a brahmin:

## mn96:3.4
- **PLI**: ‘brāhmaṇo vā brāhmaṇaṁ paricareyya, khattiyo vā brāhmaṇaṁ paricareyya, vesso vā brāhmaṇaṁ paricareyya, suddo vā brāhmaṇaṁ paricareyyā’ti.
- **EN**: ‘A brahmin, an aristocrat, a peasant, and a menial may all serve a brahmin.’
- **NOTE**: Manusmṛti 2.238 ff. allows that in abnormal times a brahmin may receive teachings from lower castes, except menials.

## mn96:4.1  `bj11.670, cck13.591, cck13.592, csp1ed10.390, csp2ed10.390, dr13.558, ms10M_1350, msdiv437, ndp10.442, nya4, pts-vp-pli2.179, sya13.612, vri13.396`
- **PLI**: “Kiṁ pana, brāhmaṇa, sabbo loko brāhmaṇānaṁ etadabbhanujānāti: ‘imā catasso pāricariyā paññapentū’”ti?
- **EN**: “But brahmin, did the whole world authorize the brahmins to prescribe these four kinds of service?”

## mn96:4.2
- **PLI**: “No hidaṁ, bho gotama”.
- **EN**: “No, worthy Gotama.”

## mn96:4.3
- **PLI**: “Seyyathāpi, brāhmaṇa, puriso daliddo assako anāḷhiyo. Tassa akāmassa bilaṁ olaggeyyuṁ: ‘idaṁ te, ambho purisa, maṁsaṁ khāditabbaṁ, mūlañca anuppadātabban’ti.
- **EN**: “It’s as if they were to force a chop on a poor, penniless person, telling them, ‘Eat this meat and pay for it!’

## mn96:4.4
- **PLI**: Evameva kho, brāhmaṇa, brāhmaṇā appaṭiññāya tesaṁ samaṇabrāhmaṇānaṁ, atha ca panimā catasso pāricariyā paññapenti.
- **EN**: In the same way, the brahmins have prescribed these four kinds of service without the consent of those ascetics and brahmins.
- **NOTE**: The reference to “these” ascetics and brahmins intrudes here, since none have been mentioned. In the parallel passage at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an6.57/en/sujato#9.4'>AN 6.57:9.4</a> it fits, so it may well have come from there.

## mn96:5.1  `nya5`
- **PLI**: Nāhaṁ, brāhmaṇa, ‘sabbaṁ paricaritabban’ti vadāmi; nāhaṁ, brāhmaṇa, ‘sabbaṁ na paricaritabban’ti vadāmi.
- **EN**: Brahmin, I don’t say that you should serve everyone, nor do I say that you shouldn’t serve anyone.

## mn96:5.2
- **PLI**: Yaṁ hissa, brāhmaṇa, paricarato pāricariyāhetu pāpiyo assa na seyyo, nāhaṁ taṁ ‘paricaritabban’ti vadāmi;
- **EN**: I say that you shouldn’t serve someone if serving them makes you worse, not better.

## mn96:5.3
