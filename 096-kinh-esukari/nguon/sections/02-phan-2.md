- **EN**: But some people who are exceptionally wealthy also refrain from killing living creatures … and have right view.

## mn96:8.12
- **PLI**: Tasmā ‘na uḷārabhogatā pāpiyaṁso’ti vadāmi.
- **EN**: That’s why I don’t say that any of these things makes you a better or worse person.

- **PLI**: Nāhaṁ, brāhmaṇa, ‘sabbaṁ paricaritabban’ti vadāmi, na panāhaṁ, brāhmaṇa, ‘sabbaṁ na paricaritabban’ti vadāmi.
- **EN**: Brahmin, I don’t say that you should serve everyone, nor do I say that you shouldn’t serve anyone.

## mn96:9.2
- **PLI**: Yaṁ hissa, brāhmaṇa, paricarato pāricariyāhetu saddhā vaḍḍhati, sīlaṁ vaḍḍhati, sutaṁ vaḍḍhati, cāgo vaḍḍhati, paññā vaḍḍhati, tamahaṁ ‘paricaritabban’ti vadāmi.
- **EN**: And I say that you should serve someone if serving them makes you grow in faith, ethics, learning, generosity, and wisdom.

## mn96:9.3
- **PLI**: Yaṁ hissa, brāhmaṇa, paricarato pāricariyāhetu na saddhā vaḍḍhati, na sīlaṁ vaḍḍhati, na sutaṁ vaḍḍhati, na cāgo vaḍḍhati, na paññā vaḍḍhati, nāhaṁ taṁ ‘paricaritabban’ti vadāmī”ti.
- **EN**: I say that you shouldn’t serve someone if serving them doesn’t make you grow in faith, ethics, learning, generosity, and wisdom.”

## mn96:10.1  `bj11.672, cck13.595, csp1ed10.391, csp2ed10.391, dr13.561, ms10M_1353, msdiv440, ndp10.444, nya10, sya13.615`
- **PLI**: Evaṁ vutte, esukārī brāhmaṇo bhagavantaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: When he had spoken, Esukārī said to him:

## mn96:10.2
- **PLI**: “brāhmaṇā, bho gotama, cattāri dhanāni paññapenti—
- **EN**: “Worthy Gotama, the brahmins prescribe four kinds of wealth:

## mn96:10.3
- **PLI**: brāhmaṇassa sandhanaṁ paññapenti, khattiyassa sandhanaṁ paññapenti, vessassa sandhanaṁ paññapenti, suddassa sandhanaṁ paññapenti.
- **EN**: for a brahmin, an aristocrat, a peasant, and a menial.
- **NOTE**: Within limits, one from a higher caste may adopt the livelihood of a lower, but not the other way around (Vāsiṣṭha Dharmasūtra 2.22–3).

## mn96:10.4
- **PLI**: Tatridaṁ, bho gotama, brāhmaṇā brāhmaṇassa sandhanaṁ paññapenti bhikkhācariyaṁ;
- **EN**: The wealth they prescribe for a brahmin is living on alms.
- **NOTE**: “Living on alms” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>bhikkhācariya</i>, Sanskrit <i lang='pi' translate='no'>bhikṣācarya</i>) is acknowledged as the best livelihood for the sages of old at Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad 3.5.1, 4.4.22. The later Vāsiṣṭha Dharmasūtra 2.13 says there are six occupations for a brahmin: study, recitation, sacrificing for oneself, or for others, giving, and receiving gifts. Manusmṛti 1.88 is similar.

## mn96:10.5
- **PLI**: bhikkhācariyañca pana brāhmaṇo sandhanaṁ atimaññamāno akiccakārī hoti gopova adinnaṁ ādiyamānoti.
- **EN**: A brahmin who scorns his own wealth, living on alms, fails in his duty like a guard who steals.

## mn96:10.6
- **PLI**: Idaṁ kho, bho gotama, brāhmaṇā brāhmaṇassa sandhanaṁ paññapenti.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)

## mn96:10.7
- **PLI**: Tatridaṁ, bho gotama, brāhmaṇā khattiyassa sandhanaṁ paññapenti dhanukalāpaṁ;
- **EN**: The wealth they prescribe for an aristocrat is the bow and quiver.
- **NOTE**: Vāsiṣṭha Dharmasūtra 2.16 and 2.18 say aristocrats and peasants share three the same as brahmins—recital, sacrifice, and giving. Their special livelihood is the use of weapons. Manusmṛti 1.89 says more diplomatically “protection of the people” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>prajānāṁ rakṣaṇaṁ</i>).

## mn96:10.8
- **PLI**: dhanukalāpañca pana khattiyo sandhanaṁ atimaññamāno akiccakārī hoti gopova adinnaṁ ādiyamānoti.

## mn96:10.9

- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)

## mn96:10.10
- **PLI**: Tatridaṁ, bho gotama, brāhmaṇā vessassa sandhanaṁ paññapenti kasigorakkhaṁ;
- **EN**: The wealth they prescribe for a peasant is farming and animal husbandry.
- **NOTE**: Vāsiṣṭha Dharmasūtra 2.19 says farming, trade, husbandry, and money-lending. This reflects a more developed economy in this later text. Manusmṛti 1.90 is similar.

## mn96:10.11

## mn96:10.13
- **PLI**: Tatridaṁ, bho gotama, brāhmaṇā suddassa sandhanaṁ paññapenti asitabyābhaṅgiṁ;
- **EN**: The wealth they prescribe for a menial is the scythe and flail.
- **NOTE**: Vāsiṣṭha Dharmasūtra 2.20 says that the only work of a menial is to serve the other castes (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>teṣāṁ paricaryā</i>). Manusmṛti 1.91 is similar, emphasizing “unbegrudged obedience” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>śuśrūṣāmanasūyayā</i>).
