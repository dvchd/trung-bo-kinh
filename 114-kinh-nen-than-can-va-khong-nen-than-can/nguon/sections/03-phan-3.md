- **EN**: And what acquisition of views causes unskillful qualities to grow while skillful qualities decline?
- **NOTE**: A view is a perception solidified into a persistent pattern of thought, which becomes a way of seeing the world. Views offer explanatory power, provide us with a convenient template for understanding, and often dismissing, what we encounter. Once a view has set in, it tends to reinforce itself, and hence is very slow to change.
