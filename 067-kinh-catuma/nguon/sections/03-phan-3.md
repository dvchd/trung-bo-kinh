# Phần 3  (27 đoạn)

## mn67:18.1  `bj11.214, cck13.207, dr13.183, mc2.136, ms10M_432, msdiv164, nya18, sya13.201`
- **PLI**: Katamañca, bhikkhave, āvaṭṭabhayaṁ?
- **EN**: And what, mendicants, is the danger of whirlpools?
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Āvaṭṭa</i> means a “whirlpool”, but also to “revert”. Cf. the “return to a lesser life” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>hīnāyāvattati</i>).

## mn67:18.2
- **PLI**: Idha, bhikkhave, ekacco kulaputto saddhā agārasmā anagāriyaṁ pabbajito hoti:
- **EN**: It’s when a gentleman has gone forth out of faith from the lay life to homelessness, thinking:

## mn67:18.3
- **PLI**: ‘otiṇṇomhi jātiyā jarāya maraṇena sokehi paridevehi dukkhehi domanassehi upāyāsehi dukkhotiṇṇo dukkhapareto;
- **EN**: ‘I’m swamped by rebirth, old age, and death; by sorrow, lamentation, pain, sadness, and distress. I’m swamped by suffering, mired in suffering.

## mn67:18.4
- **PLI**: appeva nāma imassa kevalassa dukkhakkhandhassa antakiriyā paññāyethā’ti.
- **EN**: Hopefully I can make an end to this entire mass of suffering.’

## mn67:18.5
- **PLI**: So evaṁ pabbajito samāno pubbaṇhasamayaṁ nivāsetvā pattacīvaramādāya gāmaṁ vā nigamaṁ vā piṇḍāya pavisati.
- **EN**: When they’ve gone forth, they robe up in the morning and, taking their bowl and robe, enter a village or town for alms without guarding body, speech, and mind, without establishing mindfulness, and without restraining the sense faculties.

## mn67:18.6
- **PLI**: Arakkhiteneva kāyena arakkhitāya vācāya anupaṭṭhitāya satiyā asaṁvutehi indriyehi so tattha passati gahapatiṁ vā gahapatiputtaṁ vā pañcahi kāmaguṇehi samappitaṁ samaṅgībhūtaṁ paricārayamānaṁ.
- **EN**: There they see a householder or their child amusing themselves, supplied and provided with the five kinds of sensual stimulation.

## mn67:18.7
- **PLI**: Tassa evaṁ hoti:
- **EN**: They think:

## mn67:18.8
- **PLI**: ‘mayaṁ kho pubbe agāriyabhūtā samānā pañcahi kāmaguṇehi samappitā samaṅgībhūtā paricārimhā.
- **EN**: ‘Formerly, as laypeople, we amused ourselves, supplied and provided with the five kinds of sensual stimulation.

## mn67:18.9
- **PLI**: Saṁvijjanti kho pana me kule bhogā.
- **EN**: And it’s true that my family is wealthy.
- **NOTE**: Here he reverts to the singular in his reflections. Obviously not all of his spiritual companions enjoy the same cushion.

## mn67:18.10
- **PLI**: Sakkā bhoge ca bhuñjituṁ puññāni ca kātun’ti.
- **EN**: I can both enjoy my wealth and make merit.’

## mn67:18.11
- **PLI**: So sikkhaṁ paccakkhāya hīnāyāvattati.
- **EN**: They resign the training and return to a lesser life.

## mn67:18.12
- **PLI**: Ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, āvaṭṭabhayassa bhīto sikkhaṁ paccakkhāya hīnāyāvatto.
- **EN**: This is called one who resigns the training and returns to a lesser life for fear of the danger of whirlpools.

## mn67:18.13
- **PLI**: ‘Āvaṭṭabhayan’ti kho, bhikkhave, pañcannetaṁ kāmaguṇānaṁ adhivacanaṁ.
- **EN**: ‘Danger of whirlpools’ is a term for the five kinds of sensual stimulation.

## mn67:19.1  `cck13.208, csp1ed10.125, csp2ed10.125, dr13.184, ms10M_433, msdiv165, ndp10.147, nya19, pts-vp-pli1.462, sya13.202, vri13.134`
- **PLI**: Katamañca, bhikkhave, susukābhayaṁ?
- **EN**: And what, mendicants, is the danger of sharks?
- **NOTE**: The <i lang='pi' translate='no'>susukā</i> is said to be a carnivorous fish (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>caṇḍamaccha</i>). The Sanskrit form <i lang='pi' translate='no'>śuṣkala</i> is said to mean both a species of fish and also “flesh”. The pun appears to be between <i lang='pi' translate='no'>susukā</i> and <i lang='pi' translate='no'>susukāḷakesā</i>, a stock description of attractive young people with “pristine black hair”. The bull shark, which is one of several shark species prowling the Ganges, has black-tipped fins.
