## mn98:11.42
- **PLI**: tamahaṁ brūmi brāhmaṇaṁ.
- **EN**: that’s who I declare a brahmin.
- **NOTE**: A brahmin is identified with Mitra, the “friendly” god of alliances (Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa 4.1.4). The Brahmanical tradition endorses harmlessness except in the sacrifice (Chāndogya Upaniṣad 8.15.1), which can be read as either sheer hypocrisy or as a sacred and effective limitation on the scope of killing. Either way, the Buddha made no such exception.
