## mn101:10.5

## mn101:16.2
- **PLI**: ‘taṁ kiṁ maññathāvuso nigaṇṭhā, yamidaṁ kammaṁ diṭṭhadhammavedanīyaṁ taṁ upakkamena vā padhānena vā samparāyavedanīyaṁ hotūti labbhametan’ti?
- **EN**: ‘What do you think, reverends? If a deed is to be experienced in this life, can exertion make it be experienced in lives to come?’
- **NOTE**: Just as different deeds have differing characteristics, the results of those deeds also have different characteristics, which constrain the result in certain ways. This does not mean that they are fully deterministic, however. Compare the sprouting of a seed. A mango seed cannot give rise to an orange tree. And it has certain conditions under which it cannot sprout—if it is out of season, or the soil is inadequate, or there is no water, etc. Still, there are many different ways that it <em>can</em> sprout, and the tree and its fruit that result will vary according to conditions.
