## mn101:22.1  `ms11M_31, nya22`
- **PLI**: Evaṁvādī, bhikkhave, nigaṇṭhā.
- **EN**: Such is the doctrine of the Jain ascetics.

## mn101:22.2

- **EN**: Saying this, the Jain ascetics deserve rebuttal and criticism on ten legitimate grounds.

## mn101:22.3  `cck14.12, dr13.12, ms11M_32, msdiv9, ndp11.11`
- **PLI**: Sace, bhikkhave, sattā pubbekatahetu sukhadukkhaṁ paṭisaṁvedenti;
- **EN**: If sentient beings experience pleasure and pain because of past deeds,

## mn101:22.4
- **PLI**: addhā, bhikkhave, nigaṇṭhā pubbe dukkaṭakammakārino yaṁ etarahi evarūpā dukkhā tibbā kaṭukā vedanā vediyanti.
- **EN**: clearly the Jains have done bad deeds in the past, since they now experience such intense pain.

## mn101:22.5
- **PLI**: Sace, bhikkhave, sattā issaranimmānahetu sukhadukkhaṁ paṭisaṁvedenti;
- **EN**: If sentient beings experience pleasure and pain because of God Almighty’s creation,
- **NOTE**: “God Almighty” is <i lang='pi' translate='no'>issara</i>, the creator deity. See also <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an3.61/en/sujato'>AN 3.61</a>.
