## mn54:15.1  `bj11.46, cck13.43, dr13.38, mc2.29, ms10M_79, msdiv42, nya15, vri13.29`
- **PLI**: “Seyyathāpi, gahapati, kukkuro jighacchādubbalyapareto goghātakasūnaṁ paccupaṭṭhito assa.
- **EN**: “Householder, suppose a dog weak with hunger was hanging around a butcher’s shop.
- **NOTE**: This list of similes is also found at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn22/en/sujato#3.8'>MN 22:3.8</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an5.76/en/sujato#11.2'>AN 5.76:11.2</a>, and <a href='https://suttacentral.net/thig16.1/en/sujato#41.1'>Thig 16.1:41.1</a>, as well as in various parallels to these texts. There, the list is expanded from seven to ten (with minor variations in parallels), and it is quoted as a familiar teaching without any need for explanation. Several of the similes require explanation to make sense; for example “fruit on a tree”, by itself, hardly evokes suffering. Note too that the explanation below at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn54/en/sujato#20.2'>MN 54:20.2</a> mentions the town of Āpaṇa where the discourse is set. These considerations suggest that the list of similes, together with their explanations, first appeared here.
