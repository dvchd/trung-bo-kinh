## mn63:8.1  `nya8`
- **PLI**: Kasmā cetaṁ, mālukyaputta, mayā abyākataṁ?
- **EN**: And why haven’t I declared these things?

## mn63:8.2
- **PLI**: Na hetaṁ, mālukyaputta, atthasaṁhitaṁ na ādibrahmacariyakaṁ na nibbidāya na virāgāya na nirodhāya na upasamāya na abhiññāya na sambodhāya na nibbānāya saṁvattati.
- **EN**: Because they aren’t beneficial or relevant to the fundamentals of the spiritual life. They don’t lead to disillusionment, dispassion, cessation, peace, insight, awakening, and extinguishment.

## mn63:8.3
- **PLI**: Tasmā taṁ mayā abyākataṁ.
- **EN**: That’s why I haven’t declared them.
