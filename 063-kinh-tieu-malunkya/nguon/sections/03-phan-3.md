## mn63:5.19
- **PLI**: ‘na tāvāhaṁ imaṁ sallaṁ āharissāmi yāva na taṁ dhanuṁ jānāmi yenamhi viddho, yadi vā cāpo yadi vā kodaṇḍo’ti;
- **EN**: I won’t extract this arrow as long as I don’t know whether the bow that wounded me was straight or recurved;
- **NOTE**: It would appear that <i lang='pi' translate='no'>dhanu</i> is the general word for “bow”, of which <i lang='pi' translate='no'>cāpa</i> and <i lang='pi' translate='no'>kodaṇḍa</i> are specialized varieties. These bows appear with others in a list of weapons at Arthaśāstra 2.18.8. While it is not clear what these were exactly, <i lang='pi' translate='no'>kodaṇḍa</i> is sometimes used for “eyebrows”, suggesting that the bow of that name was of the recurve type favored by horsemen, and the <i lang='pi' translate='no'>cāpa</i> by contrast may have been straight.

- **EN**: whether the bow-string is made of swallow-wort fibre, sunn hemp fibre, sinew, sanseveria fibre, or spurge fibre;
- **NOTE**: The identifications for these are from Ñāṇatusita’s notes to Bodhi’s translation.

- **EN**: and whether the arrowhead was spiked, razor-tipped, barbed, made of iron or a calf’s tooth, or lancet-shaped.’

## mn63:6.1  `bj11.160, cck13.156, cck13.157, csp1ed10.94, csp2ed10.94, dr13.139, dr13.140, mc2.102, ms10M_332, msdiv127, ndp10.112, nya6, pts-vp-pli1.431, sya13.151, vri13.101`
- **PLI**: ‘Sassato loko’ti, mālukyaputta, diṭṭhiyā sati brahmacariyavāso abhavissāti, evaṁ no.
- **EN**: It’s not true that if there were the view ‘the cosmos is eternal’ there would be the living of the spiritual life.
- **NOTE**: This is a double conditional, the sense being that if the pre-condition is met (locative absolute), the subsequent act would follow (conditional). The form echoes Māluṅkyaputta’s conditions, which is why the syntax here is more convoluted than in similar cases elsewhere.
