## mn143:2.1  `nya2`
- **PLI**: Tena kho pana samayena anāthapiṇḍiko gahapati ābādhiko hoti dukkhito bāḷhagilāno.
- **EN**: Now at that time the householder Anāthapiṇḍika was sick, suffering, gravely ill.
- **NOTE**: Renowned as the foremost of benefactors, Anāthapiṇḍika appears in over twenty discourses in the Saṁyutta and Aṅguttara Nikāyas, discussing matters such as generosity, guarding the mind, and the development of meditation; the story of his conversion is recorded in the Vinaya (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-kd16/en/sujato#4.1.1'>Kd 16:4.1.1</a>). He is well known for his offering of the monastery at which the Buddha most often stayed, but this discourse marks his only personal appearance in the Majjhima.

## mn143:3.1  `ms11M_1105, msdiv384, nya3, pts-vp-pli3.259`

- **EN**: Then Venerable Sāriputta robed up and, taking his bowl and robe, went with Venerable Ānanda as his second monk to Anāthapiṇḍika’s home. He sat down on the seat spread out and said to Anāthapiṇḍika,

## mn143:3.2
- **PLI**: “kacci te, gahapati, khamanīyaṁ, kacci yāpanīyaṁ? Kacci te dukkhā vedanā paṭikkamanti, no abhikkamanti; paṭikkamosānaṁ paññāyati, no abhikkamo”ti?
- **EN**: “I hope you’re keeping well, householder, I hope you’re all right. I hope your pain is fading, not growing; that its fading is evident, not its growing.”
- **NOTE**: The sick Anāthapiṇḍika was consoled by Ānanda at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn55.27/en/sujato#3.2'>SN 55.27:3.2</a> and by Sāriputta at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn55.26/en/sujato#4.2'>SN 55.26:4.2</a>. In other cases, we find the Buddha making such visits to sick monks (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn22.87/en/sujato#2.7'>SN 22.87:2.7</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn22.88/en/sujato#2.8'>SN 22.88:2.8</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn46.14/en/sujato#2.1'>SN 46.14:2.1</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn46.15/en/sujato#2.1'>SN 46.15:2.1</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an6.56/en/sujato#2.1'>AN 6.56:2.1</a>), but for some reason visits to householders are only attested for Sāriputta (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn97/en/sujato#28.2'>MN 97:28.2</a>) or Ānanda (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn47.29/en/sujato#2.2'>SN 47.29:2.2</a>).
