# Phần 2  (50 đoạn)

## mn3:4.3  `ms9M_90, msdiv31, ndp9.20, pts-vp-pli1.14`
- **PLI**: Tatra kho āyasmā sāriputto acirapakkantassa bhagavato bhikkhū āmantesi:
- **EN**: Then, not long after the Buddha left, Venerable Sāriputta said to the mendicants,
- **NOTE**: This marks the first time in the Majjhimanikāya that we hear a teaching from a disciple. Sāriputta was the Buddha’s leading disciple, famed for his wisdom. He gave many teachings in the suttas, typically, as here, preferring systematic expositions.

## mn3:4.4
- **PLI**: “āvuso bhikkhave”ti.
- **EN**: “Reverends, mendicants!”

## mn3:4.5
- **PLI**: “Āvuso”ti kho te bhikkhū āyasmato sāriputtassa paccassosuṁ.
- **EN**: “Reverend,” they replied.

## mn3:4.6
- **PLI**: Āyasmā sāriputto etadavoca:
- **EN**: Sāriputta said this:

## mn3:5.1  `dr12.22, ms9M_91, nya5, sya12.24, vri12.19`
- **PLI**: “Kittāvatā nu kho, āvuso, satthu pavivittassa viharato sāvakā vivekaṁ nānusikkhanti, kittāvatā ca pana satthu pavivittassa viharato sāvakā vivekamanusikkhantī”ti?
- **EN**: “Reverends, how do the disciples of a Teacher who lives in seclusion not train in seclusion? And how do they train in seclusion?”
- **NOTE**: Sāriputta continues on a similar theme, phrased in terms of “seclusion” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>viveka</i>) rather than “things of the flesh” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>āmisa</i>). Seclusion is explained in the commentaries as threefold: physical seclusion achieved by living a virtuous life in a quiet place; mental seclusion from the hindrances achieved through the <i lang='pi' translate='no'>jhānas</i>; and seclusion from attachments, namely Nibbāna.

## mn3:5.2
- **PLI**: “Dūratopi kho mayaṁ, āvuso, āgacchāma āyasmato sāriputtassa santike etassa bhāsitassa atthamaññātuṁ.
- **EN**: “Reverend, we would travel a long way to learn the meaning of this statement in the presence of Venerable Sāriputta.

## mn3:5.3
- **PLI**: Sādhu vatāyasmantaṁyeva sāriputtaṁ paṭibhātu etassa bhāsitassa attho;
- **EN**: May Venerable Sāriputta himself please clarify the meaning of this.

## mn3:5.4
- **PLI**: āyasmato sāriputtassa sutvā bhikkhū dhāressantī”ti.
- **EN**: The mendicants will listen and remember it.”

## mn3:5.5
- **PLI**: “Tena hāvuso, suṇātha, sādhukaṁ manasi karotha, bhāsissāmī”ti.
- **EN**: “Well then, reverends, listen and apply your mind well, I will speak.”

## mn3:5.6
- **PLI**: “Evamāvuso”ti kho te bhikkhū āyasmato sāriputtassa paccassosuṁ.
- **EN**: “Yes, reverend,” they replied.

## mn3:5.7
- **PLI**: Āyasmā sāriputto etadavoca:
- **EN**: Sāriputta said this:

## mn3:6.1  `bj10.34, cck12.23, csp1ed9.18, csp2ed9.18, dr12.23, ms9M_92, ndp9.21, nya6, sya12.25`

## mn3:6.2
- **PLI**: Idhāvuso, satthu pavivittassa viharato sāvakā vivekaṁ nānusikkhanti,
- **EN**: The disciples of a Teacher who lives in seclusion do not train in seclusion.

## mn3:6.3
- **PLI**: yesañca dhammānaṁ satthā pahānamāha, te ca dhamme nappajahanti,
- **EN**: They don’t give up what the Teacher tells them to give up.

## mn3:6.4
- **PLI**: bāhulikā ca honti, sāthalikā, okkamane pubbaṅgamā, paviveke nikkhittadhurā.
- **EN**: They’re indulgent and slack, leaders in backsliding, neglecting seclusion.

## mn3:6.5
- **PLI**: Tatrāvuso, therā bhikkhū tīhi ṭhānehi gārayhā bhavanti.
- **EN**: In this case, the senior mendicants should be criticized on three grounds.
- **NOTE**: “Senior mendicants” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>therā bhikkhū</i>) have been ordained for more than ten rains retreats, i.e. ten years.

## mn3:6.6
- **PLI**: ‘Satthu pavivittassa viharato sāvakā vivekaṁ nānusikkhantī’ti—
- **EN**: ‘The disciples of a Teacher who lives in seclusion do not train in seclusion.’

- **EN**: ‘They don’t give up what the Teacher tells them to give up.’

## mn3:6.9
- **PLI**: iminā dutiyena ṭhānena therā bhikkhū gārayhā bhavanti.
- **EN**: This is the second ground.

## mn3:6.10
- **PLI**: ‘Bāhulikā ca, sāthalikā, okkamane pubbaṅgamā, paviveke nikkhittadhurā’ti—
- **EN**: ‘They’re indulgent and slack, leaders in backsliding, neglecting seclusion.’

## mn3:6.11
- **PLI**: iminā tatiyena ṭhānena therā bhikkhū gārayhā bhavanti.
- **EN**: This is the third ground.

## mn3:6.12

- **NOTE**: “Middle mendicants” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>majjhimā bhikkhū</i>) have between five and ten rains.

- **PLI**: navā bhikkhū tīhi ṭhānehi gārayhā bhavanti.
- **EN**: and the junior mendicants should be criticized on the same three grounds.
