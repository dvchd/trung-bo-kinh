## mn3:8.1  `cck12.25, ms9M_94, msdiv33, nya8`
- **PLI**: Tatrāvuso, lobho ca pāpako doso ca pāpako.
- **EN**: The bad thing here is greed and hate.

## mn3:8.2
- **PLI**: Lobhassa ca pahānāya dosassa ca pahānāya atthi majjhimā paṭipadā cakkhukaraṇī ñāṇakaraṇī upasamāya abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṁvattati.
- **EN**: There is a middle way of practice for giving up greed and hate. It gives vision and knowledge, and leads to peace, direct knowledge, awakening, and extinguishment.
- **NOTE**: Sāriputta is adapting a passage from the Buddha’s first sermon, the Dhammacakkappavattanasutta (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn56.11/en/sujato#2.4'>SN 56.11:2.4</a>).

## mn3:8.3
- **PLI**: Katamā ca sā, āvuso, majjhimā paṭipadā cakkhukaraṇī ñāṇakaraṇī upasamāya abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṁvattati?
- **EN**: And what is that middle way of practice?

## mn3:8.4
- **PLI**: Ayameva ariyo aṭṭhaṅgiko maggo, seyyathidaṁ—
- **EN**: It is simply this noble eightfold path, that is:
- **NOTE**: The noble eightfold path is the fourth of the four noble truths. It is defined in detail at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn141/en/sujato#23.2'>MN 141:23.2</a>.

## mn3:8.5
- **PLI**: sammādiṭṭhi sammāsaṅkappo sammāvācā sammākammanto sammāājīvo sammāvāyāmo sammāsati sammāsamādhi.
- **EN**: right view, right purpose, right speech, right action, right livelihood, right effort, right mindfulness, and right immersion.

## mn3:8.6
- **PLI**: Ayaṁ kho sā, āvuso, majjhimā paṭipadā cakkhukaraṇī ñāṇakaraṇī upasamāya abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṁvattati.
- **EN**: This is that middle way of practice, which gives vision and knowledge, and leads to peace, direct knowledge, awakening, and extinguishment.
