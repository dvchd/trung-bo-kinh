## mn64:3.1  `cck13.160, mc2.105, ms10M_338, ndp10.115, nya3, pts-vp-pli1.433, sya13.155, vri13.104`
- **PLI**: “Kassa kho nāma tvaṁ, mālukyaputta, imāni evaṁ pañcorambhāgiyāni saṁyojanāni desitāni dhāresi?
- **EN**: “Who on earth do you remember being taught the five lower fetters in that way?
- **NOTE**: Given that Māluṅkyaputta got the answer right, it is not clear why the Buddha adopted the reproving tone that he would normally use in response to a dangerously mistaken view (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn22/en/sujato#6.1'>MN 22:6.1</a>). The commentary explains that he had the view that fetters were only present when active, hence the Buddha’s simile to follow. Regardless, as compared to <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn63/en/sujato'>MN 63</a>, Māluṅkyaputta is making progress, as he gives a serious answer to an important question and appears to no longer be on the verge of disrobing.
