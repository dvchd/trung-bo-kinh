## mn64:16.1  `bj11.172, ms10M_357, nya16`
- **PLI**: “Eso ce, bhante, maggo esā paṭipadā pañcannaṁ orambhāgiyānaṁ saṁyojanānaṁ pahānāya, atha kiñcarahi idhekacce bhikkhū cetovimuttino ekacce bhikkhū paññāvimuttino”ti?
- **EN**: “Sir, if this is the path and the practice for giving up the five lower fetters, how then are some mendicants here released in heart while others are released by wisdom?”
- **NOTE**: “Released in heart” and “released by wisdom” indicate an emphasis on either immersion or wisdom respectively.

## mn64:16.2
- **PLI**: “Ettha kho panesāhaṁ, ānanda, indriyavemattataṁ vadāmī”ti.
- **EN**: “In that case, I say it is the disparity of their faculties.”
- **NOTE**: The Buddha makes the same point at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn48.13/en/sujato'>SN 48.13</a> and <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn48.16/en/sujato'>SN 48.16</a>. For more on the “disparity of faculties”, see <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn66/en/sujato#14.6'>MN 66:14.6</a>.
