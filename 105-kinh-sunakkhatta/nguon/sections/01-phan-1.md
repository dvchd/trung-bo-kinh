## mn105:2.1  `nya2`
- **PLI**: Tena kho pana samayena sambahulehi bhikkhūhi bhagavato santike aññā byākatā hoti:
- **EN**: Now at that time several mendicants had declared their enlightenment in the Buddha’s presence:
- **NOTE**: Also at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn12.70/en/sujato#4.1'>SN 12.70:4.1</a>, which concerns the dubious ordination of the wanderer Susīma. | “Enlightenment” is <i lang='pi' translate='no'>aññā</i>, a technical term that refers to the knowledge of perfection (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>arahattā</i>). | A Buddhist mendicant incurs a penalty of expulsion if they deliberately lay false claim to awakening (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-bu-vb-pj4/en/sujato'>Bu Pj 4</a>), and a penalty of confession if they truthfully claim awakening to someone who is not ordained (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-bu-vb-pc8/en/sujato'>Bu Pc 8</a>). These rules directly concern laying claim regarding oneself, but the background stories apply when making claims with regards to another. Mendicants may declare their attainments among the community of monks and nuns who are fully ordained. Typically such a solemn matter is spoken of in a formal and respectful manner with one’s teacher.
