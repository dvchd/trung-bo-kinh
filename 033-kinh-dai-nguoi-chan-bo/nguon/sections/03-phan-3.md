## mn33:25.1  `ms9M_1180, nya25`
- **PLI**: Kathañca, bhikkhave, bhikkhu gocarakusalo hoti?
- **EN**: And how is a mendicant skilled in ranges?

## mn33:25.2
- **PLI**: Idha, bhikkhave, bhikkhu cattāro satipaṭṭhāne yathābhūtaṁ pajānāti.
- **EN**: It’s when a mendicant truly understands the four kinds of mindfulness meditation.

## mn33:25.3
- **PLI**: Evaṁ kho, bhikkhave, bhikkhu gocarakusalo hoti.
- **EN**: That’s how a mendicant is skilled in ranges.

## mn33:26.1  `cck12.397, ms9M_1181, nya26, vri12.289`
- **PLI**: Kathañca, bhikkhave, bhikkhu sāvasesadohī hoti?
- **EN**: And how does a mendicant not milk dry?

## mn33:26.2
- **PLI**: Idha, bhikkhave, bhikkhuṁ saddhā gahapatikā abhihaṭṭhuṁ pavārenti cīvarapiṇḍapātasenāsanagilānappaccayabhesajjaparikkhārehi.
- **EN**: It’s when a mendicant is invited by a householder to accept robes, almsfood, lodgings, and medicines and supplies for the sick,

## mn33:26.3
- **PLI**: Tatra bhikkhu mattaṁ jānāti paṭiggahaṇāya.
- **EN**: and that mendicant knows moderation in accepting.

## mn33:26.4
- **PLI**: Evaṁ kho, bhikkhave, bhikkhu sāvasesadohī hoti.
- **EN**: That’s how a mendicant doesn’t milk dry.

## mn33:27.1  `ms9M_1182, ndp9.276, nya27, sya12.417`
- **PLI**: Kathañca, bhikkhave, bhikkhu ye te bhikkhū therā rattaññū cirapabbajitā saṅghapitaro saṅghapariṇāyakā, te atirekapūjāya pūjetā hoti?
- **EN**: And how does a mendicant show extra respect to senior mendicants of long standing, long gone forth, fathers and leaders of the Saṅgha?

## mn33:27.2
- **PLI**: Idha, bhikkhave, bhikkhu ye te bhikkhū therā rattaññū cirapabbajitā saṅghapitaro saṅghapariṇāyakā tesu mettaṁ kāyakammaṁ paccupaṭṭhāpeti āvi ceva raho ca;
- **EN**: It’s when a mendicant consistently treats senior mendicants of long standing, long gone forth, fathers and leaders of the Saṅgha with kindness by way of body, speech, and mind, both in public and in private.

## mn33:27.3
- **PLI**: mettaṁ vacīkammaṁ paccupaṭṭhāpeti āvi ceva raho ca;
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)

## mn33:27.4
- **PLI**: mettaṁ manokammaṁ paccupaṭṭhāpeti āvi ceva raho ca.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)

## mn33:27.5
- **PLI**: Evaṁ kho, bhikkhave, bhikkhu ye te bhikkhū therā rattaññū cirapabbajitā saṅghapitaro saṅghapariṇāyakā te atirekapūjāya pūjetā hoti.
- **EN**: That’s how a mendicant shows extra respect to senior mendicants of long standing, long gone forth, fathers and leaders of the Saṅgha.
