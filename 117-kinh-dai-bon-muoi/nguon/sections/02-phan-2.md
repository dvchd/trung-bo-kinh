## mn117:15.2
- **PLI**: So sato micchāsaṅkappaṁ pajahati, sato sammāsaṅkappaṁ upasampajja viharati; sāssa hoti sammāsati.
- **EN**: Mindfully they give up wrong purpose and take up right purpose: that’s their right mindfulness.

## mn117:15.3

- **EN**: So these three things keep running and circling around right purpose, namely: right view, right effort, and right mindfulness.

- **PLI**: Sammāvācampahaṁ, bhikkhave, dvāyaṁ vadāmi—

## mn117:19.1  `nya19`
- **PLI**: Katamā ca, bhikkhave, sammāvācā sāsavā puññabhāgiyā upadhivepakkā?
- **EN**: And what is right speech that is accompanied by defilements, partakes of good deeds, and ripens in attachments?

## mn117:19.2
- **PLI**: Musāvādā veramaṇī, pisuṇāya vācāya veramaṇī, pharusāya vācāya veramaṇī, samphappalāpā veramaṇī—
- **EN**: Refraining from lying, divisive speech, harsh speech, and talking nonsense.
- **NOTE**: Right speech is defined solely as abstinence here, whereas elsewhere it also includes actually speaking in a constructive way (eg. <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn114/en/sujato#6.18'>MN 114:6.18</a>). The negative mode of definition is more technically precise, as it includes times when one is silent or in deep meditation.

## mn117:19.3
- **PLI**: ayaṁ, bhikkhave, sammāvācā sāsavā puññabhāgiyā upadhivepakkā.
- **EN**: This is right speech that is accompanied by defilements.

## mn117:20.1  `nya20`
- **PLI**: Katamā ca, bhikkhave, sammāvācā ariyā anāsavā lokuttarā maggaṅgā?
- **EN**: And what is right speech that is noble, undefiled, transcendent, a factor of the path?

## mn117:20.2
- **PLI**: Yā kho, bhikkhave, ariyacittassa anāsavacittassa ariyamaggasamaṅgino ariyamaggaṁ bhāvayato catūhi vacīduccaritehi ārati virati paṭivirati veramaṇī—
- **EN**: It’s the desisting, abstaining, abstinence, and refraining from the four kinds of bad verbal conduct in one intent on the noble, intent on the undefiled, who possesses the noble path and develops the noble path.
- **NOTE**: This sequence of synonyms (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>ārati virati paṭivirati veramaṇī</i>) is otherwise found only in late canonical texts (eg. <a href='https://suttacentral.net/cnd6/en/sujato#11.6'>Cnd 6:11.6</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/vb4/en/sujato#61.2'>Vb 4:61.2</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-pvr4/en/sujato#5.2'>Pvr 4:5.2</a>).

## mn117:20.3
- **PLI**: ayaṁ, bhikkhave, sammāvācā ariyā anāsavā lokuttarā maggaṅgā.
- **EN**: This is right speech that is noble.
