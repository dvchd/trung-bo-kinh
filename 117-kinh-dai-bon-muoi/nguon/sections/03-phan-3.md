# Phần 3  (50 đoạn)

## mn117:31.1  `nya31`
- **PLI**: Katamo ca, bhikkhave, sammāājīvo sāsavo puññabhāgiyo upadhivepakko?
- **EN**: And what is right livelihood that is accompanied by defilements, partakes of good deeds, and ripens in attachments?

## mn117:31.2
- **PLI**: Idha, bhikkhave, ariyasāvako micchāājīvaṁ pahāya sammāājīvena jīvikaṁ kappeti—
- **EN**: It’s when a noble disciple gives up wrong livelihood and earns a living by right livelihood.

## mn117:31.3
- **PLI**: ayaṁ, bhikkhave, sammāājīvo sāsavo puññabhāgiyo upadhivepakko.
- **EN**: This is right livelihood that is accompanied by defilements.

## mn117:32.1  `nya32`
- **PLI**: Katamo ca, bhikkhave, sammāājīvo ariyo anāsavo lokuttaro maggaṅgo?
- **EN**: And what is right livelihood that is noble, undefiled, transcendent, a factor of the path?

## mn117:32.2
- **PLI**: Yā kho, bhikkhave, ariyacittassa anāsavacittassa ariyamaggasamaṅgino ariyamaggaṁ bhāvayato micchāājīvā ārati virati paṭivirati veramaṇī—
- **EN**: It’s the desisting, abstaining, abstinence, and refraining from wrong livelihood in one intent on the noble, intent on the undefiled, who possesses the noble path and develops the noble path.

## mn117:32.3
- **PLI**: ayaṁ, bhikkhave, sammāājīvo ariyo anāsavo lokuttaro maggaṅgo.
- **EN**: This is right livelihood that is noble.

## mn117:33.1  `nya33`
- **PLI**: So micchāājīvassa pahānāya vāyamati, sammāājīvassa upasampadāya; svāssa hoti sammāvāyāmo.
- **EN**: They make an effort to give up wrong livelihood and embrace right livelihood: that’s their right effort.

## mn117:33.2
- **PLI**: So sato micchāājīvaṁ pajahati, sato sammāājīvaṁ upasampajja viharati; sāssa hoti sammāsati.
- **EN**: Mindfully they give up wrong livelihood and take up right livelihood: that’s their right mindfulness.

## mn117:33.3
- **PLI**: Itiyime tayo dhammā sammāājīvaṁ anuparidhāvanti anuparivattanti, seyyathidaṁ—sammādiṭṭhi, sammāvāyāmo, sammāsati.
- **EN**: So these three things keep running and circling around right livelihood, namely: right view, right effort, and right mindfulness.
- **NOTE**: This, the fifth factor of the path, is the last factor described as encompassed by right view, effort, and mindfulness. I think a purpose of this discourse is to make it clear that they <em>also</em> apply to the first five factors, the final three being taken as self-evident.

## mn117:34.1  `dr13.171, ms11M_401, msdiv141, ndp11.140, nya34, pts-vp-pli3.76`
- **PLI**: Tatra, bhikkhave, sammādiṭṭhi pubbaṅgamā hoti.
- **EN**: In this context, right view comes first.

## mn117:34.2
- **PLI**: Kathañca, bhikkhave, sammādiṭṭhi pubbaṅgamā hoti?
- **EN**: And how does right view come first?

## mn117:34.3
- **PLI**: Sammādiṭṭhissa, bhikkhave, sammāsaṅkappo pahoti, sammāsaṅkappassa sammāvācā pahoti, sammāvācassa sammākammanto pahoti, sammākammantassa sammāājīvo pahoti, sammāājīvassa sammāvāyāmo pahoti, sammāvāyāmassa sammāsati pahoti, sammāsatissa sammāsamādhi pahoti, sammāsamādhissa sammāñāṇaṁ pahoti, sammāñāṇassa sammāvimutti pahoti.
- **EN**: Right view produces right purpose. Right purpose produces right speech. Right speech produces right action. Right action produces right livelihood. Right livelihood produces right effort. Right effort produces right mindfulness. Right mindfulness produces right immersion. Right immersion produces right knowledge. Right knowledge produces right freedom.
- **NOTE**: This extends the linear arising to two additional factors: right knowledge and right freedom (see also <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn18/en/sujato#27.8'>DN 18:27.8</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an10.103/en/sujato#2.3'>AN 10.103:2.3</a>). These are not divided into two kinds, as they apply solely to the perfected ones.

## mn117:34.4
- **PLI**: Iti kho, bhikkhave, aṭṭhaṅgasamannāgato sekkho, dasaṅgasamannāgato arahā hoti.
- **EN**: So the trainee has eight factors, while the perfected one has ten factors.
- **NOTE**: Right knowledge is the wisdom perfected by the ending of defilements, while right freedom is the liberation from rebirth.

## mn117:34.5
- **PLI**: Tatrapi sammāñāṇena aneke pāpakā akusalā dhammā vigatā bhāvanāpāripūriṁ gacchanti.
- **EN**: And here too, the eradication of many bad, unskillful qualities is fully developed due to right knowledge.
