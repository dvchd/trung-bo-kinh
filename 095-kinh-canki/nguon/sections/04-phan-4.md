# Phần 4  (50 đoạn)

## mn95:14.14
- **PLI**: Saccamanurakkhatā, bhāradvāja, viññunā purisena nālamettha ekaṁsena niṭṭhaṁ gantuṁ:
- **EN**: For a sensible person who is preserving truth this is not sufficient to come to the categorical conclusion:
- **NOTE**: This sutta introduces the important epistemological distinction between the “preservation of truth” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>saccānurakkhaṇā</i>), the “awakening to the truth” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>saccānubodha</i>), and the “attainment of the truth” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>saccānuppatti</i>).

## mn95:14.15
- **PLI**: ‘idameva saccaṁ, moghamaññan’”ti.
- **EN**: ‘This is the only truth, anything else is futile.’”

## mn95:15.1  `bj11.658, cck13.582, csp1ed10.383, csp2ed10.383, ms10M_1329, msdiv429, ndp10.435, nya15, sya13.602`

- **EN**: “But worthy Gotama, how do you define the preservation of truth?”

## mn95:15.2

- **EN**: “If a person has faith,

## mn95:15.4
- **PLI**: ‘evaṁ me saddhā’ti—
- **EN**: they preserve truth by saying, ‘Such is my faith.’

## mn95:15.5
- **PLI**: iti vadaṁ saccamanurakkhati, na tveva tāva ekaṁsena niṭṭhaṁ gacchati:
- **EN**: But they don’t yet come to the categorical conclusion:

## mn95:15.6
- **PLI**: ‘idameva saccaṁ, moghamaññan’ti (…).
- **EN**: ‘This is the only truth, anything else is futile.’

- **EN**: If a person endorses something …

## mn95:15.8
- **PLI**: anussavo cepi, bhāradvāja, purisassa hoti …pe…
- **EN**: or has received an oral transmission …

## mn95:15.9
- **PLI**: ākāraparivitakko cepi, bhāradvāja, purisassa hoti …pe…
- **EN**: or has a reasoned train of thought about something …

## mn95:15.10
- **PLI**: diṭṭhinijjhānakkhanti cepi, bhāradvāja, purisassa hoti;
- **EN**: or has accepted a view after deliberation,

## mn95:15.11
- **PLI**: ‘evaṁ me diṭṭhinijjhānakkhantī’ti—
- **EN**: they preserve truth by saying, ‘Such is the view I have accepted after deliberation.’

## mn95:15.12
- **PLI**: iti vadaṁ saccamanurakkhati, na tveva tāva ekaṁsena niṭṭhaṁ gacchati:
- **EN**: But they don’t yet come to the categorical conclusion:

## mn95:15.13
- **PLI**: ‘idameva saccaṁ, moghamaññan’ti.
- **EN**: ‘This is the only truth, anything else is futile.’

## mn95:15.14
- **PLI**: Ettāvatā kho, bhāradvāja, saccānurakkhaṇā hoti, ettāvatā saccamanurakkhati, ettāvatā ca mayaṁ saccānurakkhaṇaṁ paññapema;
- **EN**: That’s how the preservation of truth is defined, Bhāradvāja. I describe the preservation of truth as defined in this way.
- **NOTE**: This is essentially the standard used in references today: one should accurately represent one’s sources.

## mn95:15.15
- **PLI**: na tveva tāva saccānubodho hotī”ti.
- **EN**: But this is not yet the awakening to the truth.”

## mn95:17.1  `nya17`
- **PLI**: “Idha, bhāradvāja, bhikkhu aññataraṁ gāmaṁ vā nigamaṁ vā upanissāya viharati.
- **EN**: “Bhāradvāja, take the case of a mendicant living supported by a town or village.

## mn95:17.2
- **PLI**: Tamenaṁ gahapati vā gahapatiputto vā upasaṅkamitvā tīsu dhammesu samannesati—
- **EN**: A householder or their child approaches and scrutinizes them for three qualities:

## mn95:17.3
- **PLI**: lobhanīyesu dhammesu, dosanīyesu dhammesu, mohanīyesu dhammesu.
- **EN**: qualities that arouse greed, things that provoke hate, and things that promote delusion.
- **NOTE**: For these phrases, compare <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an4.117/en/sujato'>AN 4.117</a> and <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an5.144/en/sujato'>AN 5.144</a>.

## mn95:17.4
- **PLI**: Atthi nu kho imassāyasmato tathārūpā lobhanīyā dhammā yathārūpehi lobhanīyehi dhammehi pariyādinnacitto ajānaṁ vā vadeyya—
- **EN**: ‘Does this venerable have any qualities that arouse greed? Such qualities that, were their mind to be overwhelmed by them, they might say

## mn95:17.5
- **PLI**: jānāmīti, apassaṁ vā vadeyya—
- **EN**: that they know, even though they don’t know, or that they see, even though they don’t see;

## mn95:17.6
- **PLI**: passāmīti, paraṁ vā tadatthāya samādapeyya yaṁ paresaṁ assa dīgharattaṁ ahitāya dukkhāyāti?
- **EN**: or that they might encourage others to do what is for their lasting harm and suffering?’

## mn95:17.7
- **PLI**: Tamenaṁ samannesamāno evaṁ jānāti:
- **EN**: Scrutinizing them they find:

## mn95:17.8
- **PLI**: ‘natthi kho imassāyasmato tathārūpā lobhanīyā dhammā yathārūpehi lobhanīyehi dhammehi pariyādinnacitto ajānaṁ vā vadeyya—
- **EN**: ‘This venerable has no such qualities that arouse greed.

## mn95:17.9
- **PLI**: jānāmīti, apassaṁ vā vadeyya—
- **EN**: that they know, even though they don’t know, or that they see, even though they don’t see;  (= mn95:17.5)

## mn95:17.10
- **PLI**: passāmīti, paraṁ vā tadatthāya samādapeyya yaṁ paresaṁ assa dīgharattaṁ ahitāya dukkhāya.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)

## mn95:17.11
- **PLI**: Tathārūpo kho panimassāyasmato kāyasamācāro tathārūpo vacīsamācāro yathā taṁ aluddhassa.
- **EN**: Rather, that venerable has bodily and verbal behavior like that of someone without greed.

## mn95:17.12
- **PLI**: Yaṁ kho pana ayamāyasmā dhammaṁ deseti, gambhīro so dhammo duddaso duranubodho santo paṇīto atakkāvacaro nipuṇo paṇḍitavedanīyo;
- **EN**: And the principle that they teach is deep, hard to see, hard to understand, peaceful, sublime, beyond the scope of logic, subtle, comprehensible to the astute.

## mn95:17.13
- **PLI**: na so dhammo sudesiyo luddhenā’ti.
- **EN**: It’s not easy for someone with greed to teach this.’
