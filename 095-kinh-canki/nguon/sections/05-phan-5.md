## mn95:20.1  `cck13.585, ms10M_1333, ndp10.437, nya20, sya13.605`
- **PLI**: Yato naṁ samannesamāno visuddhaṁ mohanīyehi dhammehi samanupassati;
- **EN**: Scrutinizing them in this way they see that they are purified of qualities that promote delusion.

## mn95:20.2
- **PLI**: atha tamhi saddhaṁ niveseti, saddhājāto upasaṅkamati, upasaṅkamanto payirupāsati, payirupāsanto sotaṁ odahati, ohitasoto dhammaṁ suṇāti, sutvā dhammaṁ dhāreti, dhatānaṁ dhammānaṁ atthaṁ upaparikkhati, atthaṁ upaparikkhato dhammā nijjhānaṁ khamanti, dhammanijjhānakkhantiyā sati chando jāyati, chandajāto ussahati, ussahitvā tuleti, tulayitvā padahati, pahitatto samāno kāyena ceva paramasaccaṁ sacchikaroti paññāya ca naṁ ativijjha passati.
- **EN**: Next, they place faith in them. When faith has arisen they approach the teacher. They pay homage, actively listen, hear the teachings, remember the teachings, examine their meaning, and accept them after deliberation. Then enthusiasm springs up; they make an attempt, weigh up, and strive. Striving, they directly realize the ultimate truth, and see it with penetrating wisdom.
- **NOTE**: Here we see the meaning of the Buddhist “grounded faith” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>ākāravatī saddhā</i>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn47/en/sujato#16.1'>MN 47:16.1</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn60/en/sujato#4.1'>MN 60:4.1</a>) as opposed to “blind faith”. But faith is only the beginning of the long and demanding process described here.
