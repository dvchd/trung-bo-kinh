# Phần 1  (50 đoạn)

## mn146:1.1  `bj12.552, dr13.443, ms11M_1161, msdiv398, ndp11.361, nya1, sya14.486, vri14.324`
- **PLI**: Evaṁ me sutaṁ—
- **EN**: So I have heard.
- **NOTE**: This discourse deals with the fortnightly teaching of the nuns, a practice ostensibly set up at Mahāpajāpati’s ordination (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/an8.51/en/sujato#13.1'>AN 8.51:13.1</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-kd20/en/sujato#1.4.7'>Kd 20:1.4.7</a>) and mandated in the Vinaya (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-bu-vb-pc21/en/sujato'>Bu Pc 21</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-bi-vb-pc59/en/sujato'>Bi Pc 59</a>). This is the only mention of such a teaching in the discourses. However it does not follow the pattern laid out in the Vinaya, where the teaching consists of the eight <i lang='pi' translate='no'>garudhammas</i> (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-bi-vb-pc58/en/sujato'>Bi Pc 58</a>), and the monks cannot go to the nuns’ monastery to advise them (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-bu-vb-pc23/en/sujato'>Bu Pc 23</a>).

## mn146:1.2
- **PLI**: ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme.
- **EN**: At one time the Buddha was staying near Sāvatthī in Jeta’s Grove, Anāthapiṇḍika’s monastery.

## mn146:2.1  `nya2`
- **PLI**: Atha kho mahāpajāpatigotamī pañcamattehi bhikkhunisatehi saddhiṁ yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavantaṁ abhivādetvā ekamantaṁ aṭṭhāsi. Ekamantaṁ ṭhitā kho mahāpajāpatigotamī bhagavantaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: Then Mahāpajāpati Gotamī together with around five hundred nuns approached the Buddha, bowed, stood to one side, and said to him,
- **NOTE**: Here we see Mahāpajāpati in the leadership role that would be expected were she the founder of the nuns. However, this is the only case where this occurs.

## mn146:2.2
- **PLI**: “ovadatu, bhante, bhagavā bhikkhuniyo;
- **EN**: “Sir, may the Buddha please advise

## mn146:2.3
- **PLI**: anusāsatu, bhante, bhagavā bhikkhuniyo;
- **EN**: and instruct the nuns.

## mn146:2.4
- **PLI**: karotu, bhante, bhagavā bhikkhunīnaṁ dhammiṁ kathan”ti.
- **EN**: Please give the nuns a Dhamma talk.”

## mn146:3.1  `cck14.442, csp1ed11.315, csp2ed11.315, ms11M_1162, nya3`
- **PLI**: Tena kho pana samayena therā bhikkhū bhikkhuniyo ovadanti pariyāyena.
- **EN**: Now at that time the senior monks were taking turns to advise the nuns.
- **NOTE**: Monks must be appointed by the Saṅgha to take on this role, and they must fulfill eight factors: they must be virtuous and learned, have memorized both Monastic Codes, be well-spoken, liked by the nuns, and capable, never having committed a heavy offense against a nun; and they must have been ordained at least twenty years (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-bu-vb-pc21/en/sujato'>Bu Pc 21</a>).

## mn146:3.2
- **PLI**: Āyasmā nandako na icchati bhikkhuniyo ovadituṁ pariyāyena.
- **EN**: But Venerable Nandaka didn’t want to take his turn.
- **NOTE**: Nandaka was praised for his teaching of monks (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/an9.4/en/sujato'>AN 9.4</a>). At <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an3.66/en/sujato'>AN 3.66</a> he taught the layman Sāḷha how to discern the true teaching, using the method of questioning as he does in the present discourse. On the strength of this teaching, Nandaka was declared the foremost in advising nuns (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/an1.229/en/sujato#1.1'>AN 1.229:1.1</a>). His four Theragāthā verses are at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/thag4.4/en/sujato'>Thag 4.4</a> according to the commentary. A second Nandaka at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/thag2.27/en/sujato'>Thag 2.27</a> was the brother of Bharata (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/thag2.28/en/sujato'>Thag 2.28</a>). | While the text does not explain why he was reluctant to teach, the commentary says that those nuns were his wives in past lives and he wanted to avoid the potential for gossip.

## mn146:5.3

## mn146:5.4
- **PLI**: ‘idaṁ, bhante, kathaṁ; imassa kvattho’”ti?
- **EN**: ‘Why, sir, does it say this? What does that mean?’”
- **NOTE**: Nandaka does not lecture the nuns, but engages with them. He makes it clear that, against patriarchal expectations, the nuns should ask for clarifications.

## mn146:5.5

- **EN**: “We’re already delighted and satisfied with Master Nandaka, since he invites us like this.”
- **NOTE**: Thus fulfilling one of the factors required for a teacher of nuns.
