# Phần 8  (9 đoạn)

## mn146:26.15
- **PLI**: Atha kho tā bhikkhuniyo bhagavantaṁ abhivādetvā padakkhiṇaṁ katvā pakkamiṁsu.
- **EN**: Then those nuns bowed and respectfully circled the Buddha, keeping him on their right, before leaving.  (= mn146:14.5)

## mn146:27.1  `nya27`
- **PLI**: Atha kho bhagavā acirapakkantīsu tāsu bhikkhunīsu bhikkhū āmantesi:
- **EN**: Soon after those nuns had left, the Buddha addressed the mendicants:

## mn146:27.2
- **PLI**: “seyyathāpi, bhikkhave, tadahuposathe pannarase na hoti bahuno janassa kaṅkhā vā vimati vā:
- **EN**: “Suppose, mendicants, it was the sabbath of the fifteenth day. You wouldn’t get lots of people

## mn146:27.3
- **PLI**: ‘ūno nu kho cando, puṇṇo nu kho cando’ti, atha kho puṇṇo cando tveva hoti;
- **EN**: wondering whether the moon is full or not, since it is obviously full.

## mn146:27.4
- **PLI**: evameva kho, bhikkhave, tā bhikkhuniyo nandakassa dhammadesanāya attamanā ceva paripuṇṇasaṅkappā ca.
- **EN**: In the same way, those nuns were uplifted by Nandaka’s Dhamma teaching, and they got all they wished for.

## mn146:27.5
- **PLI**: Tāsaṁ, bhikkhave, pañcannaṁ bhikkhunisatānaṁ yā pacchimikā bhikkhunī sā sotāpannā avinipātadhammā niyatā sambodhiparāyanā”ti.
- **EN**: Even the last of these five hundred nuns is a stream-enterer, not liable to be reborn in the underworld, assured, destined for awakening.”
- **NOTE**: As noted above, the nuns’ statements indicate that they had attained stream-entry even before being taught by Nandaka. Elsewhere, to have fulfilled one’s wishes like the moon on the fifteenth day unequivocally means arahantship (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/thag10.2/en/sujato#10.1'>Thag 10.2:10.1</a>). Indeed, parallels indicate that at this point they have all attained arahantship (SA 276 at T ii 75c16; T 1442 at T xxiii 794a14; D (3) ’dul ba, ja 59a1). Remarkably, this detail is also confirmed in the commentaries to the Aṅguttara Nikāya, Theragāthā, and Therīgāthā, which say they became arahants when listening to Nandaka’s second teaching.

## mn146:27.6  `ms11M_1183`
- **PLI**: Idamavoca bhagavā.
- **EN**: That is what the Buddha said.

## mn146:27.7
- **PLI**: Attamanā te bhikkhū bhagavato bhāsitaṁ abhinandunti.
- **EN**: Satisfied, the mendicants approved what the Buddha said.

- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)
