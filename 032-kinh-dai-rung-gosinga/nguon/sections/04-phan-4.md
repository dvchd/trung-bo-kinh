# Phần 4  (16 đoạn)

## mn32:16.21
- **PLI**: “Sādhu sādhu, moggallāna.
- **EN**: “Good, good, Moggallāna!

## mn32:16.22
- **PLI**: Yathā taṁ sāriputtova sammā byākaramāno byākareyya.
- **EN**: Sāriputta answered in the right way for him.

## mn32:16.23
- **PLI**: Sāriputto hi, moggallāna, cittaṁ vasaṁ vatteti no ca sāriputto cittassa vasena vattati.
- **EN**: For Sāriputta masters his mind and is not mastered by it …”

## mn32:16.24
- **PLI**: So yāya vihārasamāpattiyā ākaṅkhati pubbaṇhasamayaṁ viharituṁ, tāya vihārasamāpattiyā pubbaṇhasamayaṁ viharati;
- **EN**: In the morning, they abide in whatever meditation or attainment they want.  (= mn32:9.7)

## mn32:16.25
- **PLI**: yāya vihārasamāpattiyā ākaṅkhati majjhanhikasamayaṁ viharituṁ, tāya vihārasamāpattiyā majjhanhikasamayaṁ viharati;
- **EN**: At midday,  (= mn32:9.8)

## mn32:16.26
- **PLI**: yāya vihārasamāpattiyā ākaṅkhati sāyanhasamayaṁ viharituṁ, tāya vihārasamāpattiyā sāyanhasamayaṁ viharatī”ti.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)

## mn32:17.1  `csp1ed9.281, csp2ed9.281, ms9M_1153, msdiv345, nya17`
- **PLI**: Evaṁ vutte, āyasmā sāriputto bhagavantaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: When he had spoken, Sāriputta asked the Buddha,

## mn32:17.2
- **PLI**: “kassa nu kho, bhante, subhāsitan”ti?
- **EN**: “Sir, who has spoken well?”

## mn32:17.3
- **PLI**: “Sabbesaṁ vo, sāriputta, subhāsitaṁ pariyāyena.
- **EN**: “You’ve all spoken well in your own way.
- **NOTE**: The Buddha gives positive reinforcement, not only to the wholesome qualities of others, but to the fact that they appreciate their own good qualities. This is not conceit, but a form of rejoicing (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>muditā</i>).

## mn32:17.4
- **PLI**: Api ca mamapi suṇātha yathārūpena bhikkhunā gosiṅgasālavanaṁ sobheyya.
- **EN**: However, listen to me also as to what kind of mendicant would grace this sal forest park at Gosiṅga.

## mn32:17.5
- **PLI**: Idha, sāriputta, bhikkhu pacchābhattaṁ piṇḍapātapaṭikkanto nisīdati pallaṅkaṁ ābhujitvā ujuṁ kāyaṁ paṇidhāya parimukhaṁ satiṁ upaṭṭhapetvā:
- **EN**: It’s a mendicant who, after the meal, returns from almsround, sits down cross-legged, sets their body straight, and brings mindfulness to the present, thinking:
- **NOTE**: While the others spoke of results, the Buddha speaks of causes. Compare <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn123/en/sujato#22.1'>MN 123:22.1</a>, where the Buddha similarly brings the conversation back to meditation practice.

## mn32:17.6
- **PLI**: ‘na tāvāhaṁ imaṁ pallaṅkaṁ bhindissāmi yāva me nānupādāya āsavehi cittaṁ vimuccissatī’ti.
- **EN**: ‘I will not break this sitting posture until my mind is freed from the defilements by not grasping!’

## mn32:17.7
- **PLI**: Evarūpena kho, sāriputta, bhikkhunā gosiṅgasālavanaṁ sobheyyā”ti.
- **EN**: That’s the kind of mendicant who would grace this park.”

## mn32:17.8  `cck12.390, ms9M_1154`
- **PLI**: Idamavoca bhagavā.
- **EN**: That is what the Buddha said.

## mn32:17.9
- **PLI**: Attamanā te āyasmanto bhagavato bhāsitaṁ abhinandunti.
- **EN**: Satisfied, those venerables approved what the Buddha said.

## mn32:17.10
- **PLI**: Mahāgosiṅgasuttaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ dutiyaṁ.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)
