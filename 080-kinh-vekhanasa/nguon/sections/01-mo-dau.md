## mn80:2.1  `nya2`
- **PLI**: Atha kho vekhanaso paribbājako yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavatā saddhiṁ sammodi.
- **EN**: Then the wanderer Vekhanasa went up to the Buddha, and exchanged greetings with him.
- **NOTE**: Vekhanasa, the “descendant of Vikhānasa” was, according to the commentary, the teacher of Sakaludāyī and came to defend his teaching. The Vekhanasas were Vedic poets. They became known in later Sanskrit texts as an order of forest-dwelling ascetics, who evolved into the modern Vaikhānasa Vishnavite tradition. This sutta may be the earliest reference to a tradition of Vekhanasa ascetics, creating a link between the Vedic poets and the later tradition. The Buddha calls him by his personal or clan name Kaccāna.

## mn80:2.2

## mn80:2.3
- **PLI**: Ekamantaṁ ṭhito kho vekhanaso paribbājako bhagavato santike udānaṁ udānesi:
- **EN**: and expressed this heartfelt sentiment in the Buddha’s presence:

## mn80:2.4
- **PLI**: “ayaṁ paramo vaṇṇo, ayaṁ paramo vaṇṇo”ti.
- **EN**: “This is the ultimate splendor, this is the ultimate splendor.”
- **NOTE**: The Rig Veda features two Vaikhānasa poems, one of which describes Soma as “most resplendent” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>śubhraśastamaḥ</i>, Rig Veda 9.66.26), a phrase notably parallel in meaning to the Pali “ultimate splendor” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>paramo vaṇṇo</i>). These verses are rich with similar imagery: Soma is a “gleaming light” (24) whose “glittering drops” “flash with radiance” (25), “pervading with rays” (27), a “heaven-bright milk” (30). This poem is ascribed to “a hundred Vaikhānasas”, suggesting that an order already existed at the time. Perhaps the Vekhanasa lineage developed their philosophy from these verses, which they had recited over so many centuries that the original referent, Soma, was forgotten.

- **EN**: “Kaccāna, there are some ascetics and brahmins who, not knowing the first beginning or seeing the final end, nevertheless claim:

## mn80:16.2
- **PLI**: ‘khīṇā jāti, vusitaṁ brahmacariyaṁ, kataṁ karaṇīyaṁ, nāparaṁ itthattāyāti—pajānāmā’ti—paṭijānanti;
- **EN**: ‘We understand: “Rebirth is ended, the spiritual journey has been completed, what had to be done has been done, there is nothing further for this place.”’

## mn80:16.3
- **PLI**: tesaṁ soyeva sahadhammiko niggaho hoti.
- **EN**: There is a legitimate refutation of them.

## mn80:16.4
- **PLI**: Api ca, kaccāna, tiṭṭhatu pubbanto, tiṭṭhatu aparanto.
- **EN**: Nevertheless, Kaccāna, leave aside the first beginning and the final end.

## mn80:16.5
- **PLI**: Etu viññū puriso asaṭho amāyāvī ujujātiko, ahamanusāsāmi ahaṁ dhammaṁ desemi.
- **EN**: Let a sensible person come—neither devious nor deceitful, a person of integrity. I teach and instruct them.

## mn80:16.6
- **PLI**: Yathānusiṭṭhaṁ tathā paṭipajjamāno nacirasseva sāmaññeva ñassati sāmaṁ dakkhiti—
- **EN**: Practicing as instructed they will soon know and see for themselves,

## mn80:16.7
- **PLI**: evaṁ kira sammā bandhanā vippamokkho hoti, yadidaṁ avijjā bandhanā.
- **EN**: ‘So this is how to be rightly released from the bond, that is, the bond of ignorance.’

## mn80:16.8
- **PLI**: Seyyathāpi, kaccāna, daharo kumāro mando uttānaseyyako kaṇṭhapañcamehi bandhanehi baddho assa suttabandhanehi;
- **EN**: Suppose there was a little baby bound with swaddling up to the neck.

## mn80:16.9
- **PLI**: tassa vuddhimanvāya indriyānaṁ paripākamanvāya tāni bandhanāni mucceyyuṁ;
- **EN**: As they grow up and their senses mature, they’re accordingly released from those bonds.

## mn80:16.10
- **PLI**: so mokkhomhīti kho jāneyya no ca bandhanaṁ.
- **EN**: They’d know ‘I’m released,’ and there would be no more bonds.

## mn80:16.11
- **PLI**: Evameva kho, kaccāna, etu viññū puriso asaṭho amāyāvī ujujātiko, ahamanusāsāmi, ahaṁ dhammaṁ desemi;
- **EN**: In the same way, let a sensible person come—neither devious nor deceitful, a person of integrity. I teach and instruct them.

## mn80:16.12
- **PLI**: yathānusiṭṭhaṁ tathā paṭipajjamāno nacirasseva sāmaññeva ñassati, sāmaṁ dakkhiti:
- **EN**: Practicing as instructed they will soon know and see for themselves,

## mn80:16.13
- **PLI**: ‘evaṁ kira sammā bandhanā vippamokkho hoti, yadidaṁ avijjā bandhanā’”ti.
- **EN**: ‘So this is how to be rightly released from the bond, that is, the bond of ignorance.’”
