## mn123:2.7

- **EN**: They’re amazing, and they have amazing qualities.”
- **NOTE**: This is a key example of the genre of “amazing stories” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>abbhutadhamma</i>), one of the nine categories (eg. <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn22/en/sujato#10.2'>MN 22:10.2</a>), linking this sutta with the previous (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn122/en/sujato#20.1'>MN 122:20.1</a>). This genre is closely connected to Ānanda, so much so that he may be regarded as its originator (eg. <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn1/en/sujato#3.74.2'>DN 1:3.74.2</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn15/en/sujato#1.4'>DN 15:1.4</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn16/en/sujato#3.11.2'>DN 16:3.11.2</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn51.22/en/sujato#2.2'>SN 51.22:2.2</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an4.76/en/sujato#5.5'>AN 4.76:5.5</a>, etc.). When Ānanda was at a low point, the Buddha turned it around to speak of Ānanda’s amazing qualities (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn16/en/sujato#5.16.15'>DN 16:5.16.15</a> = <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an4.129/en/sujato'>AN 4.129</a>).

## mn123:3.1  `bj12.288, csp1ed11.161, csp2ed11.161, ms11M_537, nya3, sya14.248`

## mn123:3.2
- **PLI**: ‘sato sampajāno, ānanda, bodhisatto tusitaṁ kāyaṁ upapajjī’ti.
- **EN**: ‘Mindful and aware, the being intent on awakening was reborn in the host of joyful gods.’
- **NOTE**: The first three items are unique to this sutta. | Normally in early Pali, the word <i lang='pi' translate='no'>bodhisatta</i> is reserved for the Buddha-to-be once he has left home and is practicing “intent on awakening” (eg. <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn26/en/sujato#13.1'>MN 26:13.1</a>). <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn14/en/sujato#1.17.1'>DN 14:1.17.1</a> and <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an4.127/en/sujato#1.3'>AN 4.127:1.3</a> extend the usage back as far as the end of the immediate past life. Here, he is called <i lang='pi' translate='no'>bodhisatta</i> from the beginning of the immediate past life. The Chinese parallel at MA 32 goes back even further, depicting the <i lang='pi' translate='no'>bodhisatta</i> making his initial vow to become a Buddha in the time of the Buddha Kassapa. Compare <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn81/en/sujato'>MN 81</a> and its parallel MA 63, where no such vow is mentioned. Only then does he take rebirth in the Joyful heaven. These differences indicate a development of the <i lang='pi' translate='no'>bodhisatta</i> doctrine even in early texts.

- **EN**: This I remember as an incredible quality of the Buddha.
