# Phần 4  (9 đoạn)

## mn102:24.6
- **PLI**: Yañca kho ayamāyasmā:
- **EN**: And when they regard themselves like this:

## mn102:24.7
- **PLI**: ‘santohamasmi, nibbutohamasmi, anupādānohamasmī’ti samanupassati tadapi imassa bhoto samaṇassa brāhmaṇassa upādānamakkhāyati.
- **EN**: ‘I am at peace; I am quenched; I am free of grasping,’ that’s also said to be grasping on their part.

## mn102:24.8
- **PLI**: ‘Tayidaṁ saṅkhataṁ oḷārikaṁ atthi kho pana saṅkhārānaṁ nirodho atthetan’ti—
- **EN**: ‘All that is conditioned and crude. But there is the cessation of conditions—<em>that</em> is real.’

## mn102:24.9
- **PLI**: iti viditvā tassa nissaraṇadassāvī tathāgato tadupātivatto.
- **EN**: Understanding this and seeing the escape from it, the Realized One has gone beyond all that.

## mn102:25.1  `cck14.39, dr13.36, ms11M_69, ndp11.30, nya25`
- **PLI**: Idaṁ kho pana, bhikkhave, tathāgatena anuttaraṁ santivarapadaṁ abhisambuddhaṁ yadidaṁ—
- **EN**: But the Realized One has awakened to the supreme state of sublime peace, that is,

## mn102:25.2
- **PLI**: channaṁ phassāyatanānaṁ samudayañca atthaṅgamañca assādañca ādīnavañca nissaraṇañca yathābhūtaṁ viditvā anupādāvimokkho”ti.
- **EN**: liberation by not grasping after truly understanding the six fields of contact’s origin, disappearance, gratification, drawback, and escape.”
- **NOTE**: The Brahmajālasutta also emphasizes how liberation from the “sixty-two” wrong views is achieved through insight into the “six fields of contact” (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn1/en/sujato#3.72.1'>DN 1:3.72.1</a>).

## mn102:25.3  `ms11M_70, pts-vp-pli2.238`
- **PLI**: Idamavoca bhagavā.
- **EN**: That is what the Buddha said.

## mn102:25.4
- **PLI**: Attamanā te bhikkhū bhagavato bhāsitaṁ abhinandunti.
- **EN**: Satisfied, the mendicants approved what the Buddha said.

## mn102:25.5
- **PLI**: Pañcattayasuttaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ dutiyaṁ.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)
