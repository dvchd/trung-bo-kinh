## mn88:10.1  `ms10M_1061, msdiv360, nya10`
- **PLI**: Katamo pana, bhante ānanda, kāyasamācāro opārambho samaṇehi brāhmaṇehi viññūhī”ti?
- **EN**: But Honorable Ānanda, what kind of bodily behavior is faulted by sensible ascetics and brahmins?”
- **NOTE**: This passage describes bad actions from five perspectives, showing that the Buddha’s analysis of morality was not based on a single factor.

## mn88:10.2
- **PLI**: “Yo kho, mahārāja, kāyasamācāro akusalo”.
- **EN**: “Unskillful behavior.”
- **NOTE**: “Unskillful” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>akusala</i>) is an unwholesome quality of the mind, so this is the psychological dimension of immorality. What is unskillful is that which leads to the result opposite to that desired: we want happiness, but it leads to suffering.

## mn88:10.3  `ms10M_1062, vri13.324`

- **EN**: “Blameworthy behavior.”
- **NOTE**: “Blameworthy” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>sāvajja</i>) behavior is that which makes one liable to criticism by others. This is the social dimension of immorality.
