# Phần 8  (7 đoạn)

## mn22:46.1  `ms9M_833, nya46`
- **PLI**: Evaṁ svākkhāto, bhikkhave, mayā dhammo uttāno vivaṭo pakāsito chinnapilotiko.
- **EN**: The teaching well-explained thus by me is clarified, revealed, illuminated, and stripped of patchwork.  (= mn22:42.1)

## mn22:46.2
- **PLI**: Evaṁ svākkhāte, bhikkhave, mayā dhamme uttāne vivaṭe pakāsite chinnapilotike ye te bhikkhū dhammānusārino saddhānusārino sabbe te sambodhiparāyanā.
- **EN**: In this teaching thus well-explained, those mendicants who are followers of teachings or followers by faith are all are assured, destined for awakening.
- **NOTE**: A “follower of teachings” is someone who accepts the teachings after reflecting on them with wisdom, while a “follower by faith” accepts the teachings due to their confidence in the teacher. They are both considered to be “practicing to realize the fruit of stream-entry” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>sotāpattiphalasacchikiriyāya paṭipanno</i>), and, with the maturing of their faculties (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn48.18/en/sujato#1.5'>SN 48.18:1.5</a>), will assuredly attain stream-entry in this life (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn25.1/en/sujato'>SN 25.1</a>).

## mn22:47.1  `ms9M_834, nya47`
- **PLI**: Evaṁ svākkhāto, bhikkhave, mayā dhammo uttāno vivaṭo pakāsito chinnapilotiko.
- **EN**: The teaching well-explained thus by me is clarified, revealed, illuminated, and stripped of patchwork.

## mn22:47.2
- **PLI**: Evaṁ svākkhāte, bhikkhave, mayā dhamme uttāne vivaṭe pakāsite chinnapilotike yesaṁ mayi saddhāmattaṁ pemamattaṁ sabbe te saggaparāyanā”ti.
- **EN**: In this teaching thus well-explained, those who have a degree of faith and love for me are all bound for heaven.”
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Saddhāmatta</i> (“a degree of faith”) is to be distinguished from <i lang='pi' translate='no'>saddhāmattaka</i> (“mere faith”). The former is sufficient for one to progress in the path (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn70/en/sujato#21.2'>MN 70:21.2</a>), but with the latter one just gets by (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn65/en/sujato#28.7'>MN 65:28.7</a>).
