## mn22:3.5  `bj10.326, cck12.247, csp1ed9.183, csp2ed9.183, dr12.235, ms9M_774, ndp9.175, sya12.262`
- **PLI**: Atha kho tepi bhikkhū ariṭṭhaṁ bhikkhuṁ gaddhabādhipubbaṁ etasmā pāpakā diṭṭhigatā vivecetukāmā samanuyuñjanti samanugāhanti samanubhāsanti:
- **EN**: Then, wishing to dissuade Ariṭṭha from his view, the mendicants pursued, pressed, and grilled him,
- **NOTE**: The stock phrase “pursued, pressed, and grilled” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>samanuyuñjanti samanugāhanti samanubhāsanti</i>) is sometimes rendered as if it meant to “question”, but here there is no question. While these terms are commonly used in a context of questioning, they do not, in and of themselves, mean to question. Rather they mean to engage with a person and push for an answer or response.

## mn22:3.6
- **PLI**: “mā hevaṁ, āvuso ariṭṭha, avaca, mā bhagavantaṁ abbhācikkhi; na hi sādhu bhagavato abbhakkhānaṁ, na hi bhagavā evaṁ vadeyya.
- **EN**: “Don’t say that, Ariṭṭha! Don’t misrepresent the Buddha, for misrepresentation of the Buddha is not good. And the Buddha would not say that.
- **NOTE**: Ariṭṭha makes two mistakes: misunderstanding sensual pleasures, and misrepresenting the Buddha.

## mn22:3.7
- **PLI**: Anekapariyāyenāvuso ariṭṭha, antarāyikā dhammā antarāyikā vuttā bhagavatā, alañca pana te paṭisevato antarāyāya.
- **EN**: In many ways the Buddha has said that obstructive acts are obstructive, and that they really do obstruct the one who performs them.
- **NOTE**: Ariṭṭha did not specify what “obstructions” he was referring to, but this reply by the mendicants indicates that he meant indulgence in sensual pleasures, a conclusion supported by the commentary and several parallel texts. See too <a href='https://suttacentral.net/thig16.1/en/sujato#45.2'>Thig 16.1:45.2</a> where sensual pleasures are said to be “obstructive”. Other things said to be obstructive are “possessions, honor, and popularity” (eg. <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn17.2/en/sujato#1.2'>SN 17.2:1.2</a>) and “false speech” (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-kd2/en/sujato#3.3.14'>Kd 2:3.3.14</a>). In the latter context, “obstructive” is explained as preventing the attainment of <i lang='pi' translate='no'>jhāna</i> and higher spiritual realizations.
