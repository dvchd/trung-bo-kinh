## mn16:26.1  `cck12.198, csp1ed9.149, csp2ed9.149, dr12.191, ms9M_686, msdiv189, nya26, pts-vp-pli1.104, sya12.211`
- **PLI**: So chandasamādhipadhānasaṅkhārasamannāgataṁ iddhipādaṁ bhāveti,
- **EN**: They develop the basis of psychic power that has immersion due to enthusiasm, and active effort …
- **NOTE**: The four <i lang='pi' translate='no'>iddhipada</i> are included in the 37 “qualities on the side of awakening” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>bodhipakkhiyadhamma</i>). They are an alternate presentation of the path of meditation, with a special focus on developing <i lang='pi' translate='no'>samādhi</i>. It seems the Buddha designed this presentation of Dhamma to appeal to the many meditators who are fascinated by psychic powers, with the aim to lead them on to the higher goal of liberation. | “Enthusiasm” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>chanda</i>) is the desire or will to practice the Dhamma for liberation.

## mn16:26.2
- **PLI**: vīriyasamādhipadhānasaṅkhārasamannāgataṁ iddhipādaṁ bhāveti,
- **EN**: the basis of psychic power that has immersion due to energy, and active effort …
- **NOTE**: One who has the requisite enthusiasm will put forth “effort” to achieve the goal.

## mn16:26.3
- **PLI**: cittasamādhipadhānasaṅkhārasamannāgataṁ iddhipādaṁ bhāveti,
- **EN**: the basis of psychic power that has immersion due to mental development, and active effort …
- **NOTE**: When striving, one will purify and develop the “mind”. When <i lang='pi' translate='no'>citta</i> appears in meditation contexts, it is often virtually a synonym of <i lang='pi' translate='no'>samādhi</i> (see eg. <i lang='pi' translate='no'>cittabhāvanā</i>, <i lang='pi' translate='no'>cittasampadā</i>).

## mn16:26.4

- **NOTE**: The mind clarified in <i lang='pi' translate='no'>samādhi</i> can investigate the true nature of reality. | <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Ussoḷhi</i> (“vigor”) is similar in meaning to “energy” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>viriya</i>) and “active effort” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>saṅkhāra</i>), underscoring the importance of effort here.

## mn16:27.1  `nya27`
- **PLI**: Sa kho so, bhikkhave, evaṁ ussoḷhipannarasaṅgasamannāgato bhikkhu bhabbo abhinibbidāya, bhabbo sambodhāya, bhabbo anuttarassa yogakkhemassa adhigamāya.
- **EN**: A mendicant who possesses these fifteen factors, including vigor, is capable of breaking out, becoming awakened, and reaching the supreme sanctuary from the yoke.
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Yogakkhema</i> (“sanctuary from the yoke”) is a metaphor for Nibbāna that draws from the literal sense of <i lang='pi' translate='no'>khema</i> as an oasis or sanctuary for men and beasts. After a hard day’s journey, a caravan would stop to rest, “unyoke” the draft animals, lay down the burden, and refresh themselves in the cool waters.
