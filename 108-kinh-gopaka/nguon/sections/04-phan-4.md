# Phần 4  (8 đoạn)

## mn108:28.5
- **PLI**: Atha kho vassakāro brāhmaṇo magadhamahāmatto āyasmato ānandassa bhāsitaṁ abhinanditvā anumoditvā uṭṭhāyāsanā pakkāmi.
- **EN**: Then Vassakāra the brahmin, having approved and agreed with what Venerable Ānanda said, rose from his seat and left.

## mn108:29.1  `bj12.114, cck14.96, csp1ed11.66, csp2ed11.66, ms11M_196, nya29`
- **PLI**: Atha kho gopakamoggallāno brāhmaṇo acirapakkante vassakāre brāhmaṇe magadhamahāmatte āyasmantaṁ ānandaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: Soon after he had left, Moggallāna the Guardian said to Ānanda,

## mn108:29.2
- **PLI**: “yaṁ no mayaṁ bhavantaṁ ānandaṁ apucchimhā taṁ no bhavaṁ ānando na byākāsī”ti.
- **EN**: “Worthy Ānanda, you still haven’t answered my question.”

## mn108:29.3
- **PLI**: “Nanu te, brāhmaṇa, avocumhā:
- **EN**: “But brahmin, didn’t I say:

## mn108:29.4
- **PLI**: ‘natthi kho, brāhmaṇa, ekabhikkhupi tehi dhammehi sabbenasabbaṁ sabbathāsabbaṁ samannāgato yehi dhammehi samannāgato so bhagavā ahosi arahaṁ sammāsambuddho.
- **EN**: ‘There is no single mendicant who has all the same qualities in each and every way as possessed by the worthy Gotama, the perfected one, the fully awakened Buddha.
- **NOTE**: The Chinese parallel differs here, emphasizing instead that there is no difference between the liberation of the Buddha and his arahant disciples (MA 145 at T i 655c27).

## mn108:29.5
- **PLI**: So hi, brāhmaṇa, bhagavā anuppannassa maggassa uppādetā, asañjātassa maggassa sañjanetā, anakkhātassa maggassa akkhātā, maggaññū, maggavidū, maggakovido.
- **EN**: For the Blessed One gave rise to the unarisen path, gave birth to the unborn path, and explained the unexplained path. He is the knower of the path, the discoverer of the path, the expert on the path.

## mn108:29.6
- **PLI**: Maggānugā ca pana etarahi sāvakā viharanti pacchā samannāgatā’”ti.
- **EN**: And now the disciples live following the path; they acquire it later.’”
- **NOTE**: Ānanda is clarifying that his original statement was complete, namely, this is the quality that distinguishes the Buddha from his followers. | The Pali lacks the normal ending, whereas the Chinese ends with Moggallāna inviting Ānanda for a meal.

## mn108:29.7
- **PLI**: Gopakamoggallānasuttaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ aṭṭhamaṁ.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)
