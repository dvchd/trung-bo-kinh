## mn66:6.4
- **PLI**: Mayañhi, bhante, pubbe sāyañceva bhuñjāma pāto ca divā ca vikāle.
- **EN**: For we used to eat at dusk, at daybreak, and at the wrong time of day.
- **NOTE**: This sutta distinguishes the “wrong time of day”, i.e. the afternoon, and the “wrong time of night”, i.e. any time when it is dark. The common expression “eating at night and at the wrong time” (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn27/en/sujato#13.9'>MN 27:13.9</a>) thus refers to both these periods. The Vinaya rule defines <i lang='pi' translate='no'>vikāla</i> for the purpose of this rule to include both afternoon and nighttime (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-bu-vb-pc37/en/sujato#2.1.5'>Bu Pc 37:2.1.5</a>), and it is this understanding that prevails in the tradition.

## mn66:6.5

## mn66:6.9

- **EN**: But when we considered our fondness and respect for the Buddha, and our sense of conscience and prudence, we gave up that meal at the wrong time of day.

Rig Veda 1.133.5a, Atharva Veda 1.16, 5.29, 6.32, 8.6). | This interjection is found at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/ja525/en/sujato#2.1'>Ja 525:2.1</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/ja547/en/sujato#62.3'>Ja 547:62.3</a>, and <a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-kd15/en/sujato#10.2.4'>Kd 15:10.2.4</a>.
