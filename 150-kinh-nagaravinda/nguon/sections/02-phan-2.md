# Phần 2  (7 đoạn)

## mn150:6.9
- **PLI**: Evaṁ puṭṭhā tumhe, gahapatayo, tesaṁ aññatitthiyānaṁ paribbājakānaṁ evaṁ byākareyyāthā”ti.
- **EN**: When questioned by wanderers of other religions, that’s how you should answer them.”

## mn150:7.1  `dr13.484, ms11M_1255, nya7`
- **PLI**: Evaṁ vutte, nagaravindeyyakā brāhmaṇagahapatikā bhagavantaṁ etadavocuṁ:
- **EN**: When he had spoken, the brahmins and householders of Nagaravinda said to the Buddha,

## mn150:7.2
- **PLI**: “abhikkantaṁ, bho gotama, abhikkantaṁ, bho gotama.
- **EN**: “Excellent, worthy Gotama! Excellent!

## mn150:7.3
- **PLI**: Seyyathāpi, bho gotama, nikkujjitaṁ vā ukkujjeyya, paṭicchannaṁ vā vivareyya, mūḷhassa vā maggaṁ ācikkheyya, andhakāre vā telapajjotaṁ dhāreyya: ‘cakkhumanto rūpāni dakkhantī’ti; evamevaṁ bhotā gotamena anekapariyāyena dhammo pakāsito.
- **EN**: As if he were righting the overturned, or revealing the hidden, or pointing out the path to the lost, or lighting a lamp in the dark so people with clear eyes can see what’s there, worthy Gotama has made the Teaching clear in many ways.

## mn150:7.4
- **PLI**: Ete mayaṁ bhavantaṁ gotamaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāma dhammañca bhikkhusaṅghañca.
- **EN**: We go for refuge to the worthy Gotama, to the teaching, and to the mendicant Saṅgha.

## mn150:7.5
- **PLI**: Upāsake no bhavaṁ gotamo dhāretu ajjatagge pāṇupete saraṇaṁ gate”ti.
- **EN**: From this day forth, may the worthy Gotama remember us as lay followers who have gone for refuge for life.”

## mn150:7.6
- **PLI**: Nagaravindeyyasuttaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ aṭṭhamaṁ.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)
