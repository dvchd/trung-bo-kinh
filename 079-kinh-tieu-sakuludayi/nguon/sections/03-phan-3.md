## mn79:18.1  `ms10M_775, nya18, sya13.360`
- **PLI**: “Ato kho te, udāyi, bahū hi bahutarā devā ye imesaṁ candimasūriyānaṁ ābhā nānubhonti, tyāhaṁ pajānāmi.
- **EN**: “Beyond this, Udāyī, I know very many gods on whom the luminosity of the sun and moon makes no impression.
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Ābhā nanubhonti</i> (“luminosity makes no impression”) is glossed as <i lang='pi' translate='no'>obhāsaṁ na vaḷañjanti</i>, “does not partake of the light”. At <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an4.127/en/sujato#1.4'>AN 4.127:1.4</a> the same phrase, in regard to the space where the light of the sun does not reach, is glossed <i lang='pi' translate='no'>pabhā nappahonti</i>, i.e. “light is ineffective”. Compare <i lang='pi' translate='no'>ananubhūtaṁ</i> at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn49/en/sujato#11.1'>MN 49:11.1</a>.
