## mn79:8.1  `bj11.380, cck13.354, dr13.328, ms10M_761, ndp10.258, nya8, sya13.356`
- **PLI**: “Ahañhi, bhante, yāvatakampi me iminā attabhāvena paccanubhūtaṁ tampi nappahomi sākāraṁ sauddesaṁ anussarituṁ,
- **EN**: “Well sir, I can’t even recall with features and details what I’ve undergone in this incarnation.
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Attabhāva</i> is literally “the state of the self”, and refers to the body in which consciousness has been incarnated. While <i lang='pi' translate='no'>attā</i> usually has a psychological or metaphysical sense, it sometimes means the “body”, a usage found also in the Sanskrit <i lang='pi' translate='no'>ātman</i> (eg. Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad 1.2.4).

## mn79:8.2
- **PLI**: kuto panāhaṁ anekavihitaṁ pubbenivāsaṁ anussarissāmi, seyyathidaṁ—ekampi jātiṁ dvepi jātiyo …pe… iti sākāraṁ sauddesaṁ anekavihitaṁ pubbenivāsaṁ anussarissāmi, seyyathāpi bhagavā?
- **EN**: How should I possibly recollect my many kinds of past lives with features and details, like the Buddha?

## mn79:8.3
- **PLI**: Ahañhi, bhante, etarahi paṁsupisācakampi na passāmi,
- **EN**: For I can’t even see a mud-goblin right now.
- **NOTE**: The curious mud-goblin (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>paṁsupisācaka</i>) is similarly mentioned at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/ud4.4/en/sujato#7.3'>Ud 4.4:7.3</a>.

## mn79:8.4
- **PLI**: kuto panāhaṁ dibbena cakkhunā visuddhena atikkantamānusakena satte passissāmi cavamāne upapajjamāne hīne paṇīte suvaṇṇe dubbaṇṇe sugate duggate yathākammūpage satte pajānissāmi, seyyathāpi bhagavā?
- **EN**: How should I possibly, with clairvoyance that is purified and superhuman, see sentient beings passing away and being reborn, like the Buddha?

- **EN**: When this doesn’t exist, this doesn’t come to be; due to the cessation of this, this ceases.”’ But that is even more unclear to me.
