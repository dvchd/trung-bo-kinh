# Phần 2  (50 đoạn)

## mn31:8.2
- **PLI**: Kacci pana vo, anuruddhā, appamattā ātāpino pahitattā viharathā”ti?
- **EN**: But I hope you’re living diligently, keen, and resolute?”
- **NOTE**: Normally I render <i lang='pi' translate='no'>viharati</i> in such instances as “meditate”. But Anuruddha’s response avoids talking about meditation. I think he is deliberately avoiding the topic, skirting the obvious intended meaning by seizing on an ambiguity. His reticence echoes Yājñavalkya’s reticence to answer Janaka on the topic of a person’s light (Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad 4.3.1).

## mn31:9.1  `nya9`
- **PLI**: “Taggha mayaṁ, bhante, appamattā ātāpino pahitattā viharāmā”ti.
- **EN**: “Indeed, sir, we live diligently.”

## mn31:9.2
- **PLI**: “Yathā kathaṁ pana tumhe, anuruddhā, appamattā ātāpino pahitattā viharathā”ti?
- **EN**: “But how do you live this way?”

## mn31:9.3
- **PLI**: “Idha, bhante, amhākaṁ yo paṭhamaṁ gāmato piṇḍāya paṭikkamati so āsanāni paññapeti, pānīyaṁ paribhojanīyaṁ upaṭṭhāpeti, avakkārapātiṁ upaṭṭhāpeti.
- **EN**: “In this case, sir, whoever returns first from almsround prepares the seats, and puts out the drinking water and the rubbish bin.

## mn31:9.4
- **PLI**: Yo pacchā gāmato piṇḍāya paṭikkamati, sace hoti bhuttāvaseso sace ākaṅkhati bhuñjati, no ce ākaṅkhati appaharite vā chaḍḍeti, appāṇake vā udake opilāpeti.
- **EN**: If there’s remainder, whoever returns last eats it if they like. Otherwise they throw it out where there is little that grows, or drop it into water that has no living creatures.

## mn31:9.5
- **PLI**: So āsanāni paṭisāmeti, pānīyaṁ paribhojanīyaṁ paṭisāmeti, avakkārapātiṁ paṭisāmeti, bhattaggaṁ sammajjati.
- **EN**: Then they put away the seats, drinking water, and rubbish bin, and sweep the refectory.

## mn31:9.6
- **PLI**: Yo passati pānīyaghaṭaṁ vā paribhojanīyaghaṭaṁ vā vaccaghaṭaṁ vā rittaṁ tucchaṁ so upaṭṭhāpeti.
- **EN**: If someone sees that the pot of water for washing, drinking, or the toilet is empty they set it up.

## mn31:9.7
- **PLI**: Sacassa hoti avisayhaṁ, hatthavikārena dutiyaṁ āmantetvā hatthavilaṅghakena upaṭṭhāpema, na tveva mayaṁ, bhante, tappaccayā vācaṁ bhindāma.
- **EN**: If he can’t do it, he summons another with a wave of the hand, and they set it up by lending each other a hand to lift. But we don’t break into speech for that reason.
- **NOTE**: For <i lang='pi' translate='no'>hatthavilaṅghaka</i> (“lending a hand up”) see <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn125/en/sujato#9.2'>MN 125:9.2</a>. | The Chinese parallels (MA 128 at T i 729c9 and EA 24.8 at T ii 629a23) both add that they then retire for meditation.

## mn31:9.8
- **PLI**: Pañcāhikaṁ kho pana mayaṁ, bhante, sabbarattikaṁ dhammiyā kathāya sannisīdāma.
- **EN**: And every five days we sit together for the whole night and discuss the teachings.
- **NOTE**: That is, three times in each <i lang='pi' translate='no'>uposatha</i> period lasting a fortnight. | The Chinese parallels add that they might also sit in noble silence (in accord with <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn26/en/sujato#4.13'>MN 26:4.13</a>).

## mn31:9.9
- **PLI**: Evaṁ kho mayaṁ, bhante, appamattā ātāpino pahitattā viharāmā”ti.
- **EN**: That’s how we live diligently, keen, and resolute.”
- **NOTE**: Here the Vinaya account ends, as it is concerned with the settlement of the quarrel among the monks of Kosambī.

## mn31:10.1  `ms9M_1128, msdiv328, nya10`

- **EN**: “Good, good, Anuruddha and friends!

## mn31:10.2
- **PLI**: Atthi pana vo, anuruddhā, evaṁ appamattānaṁ ātāpīnaṁ pahitattānaṁ viharantānaṁ uttari manussadhammā alamariyañāṇadassanaviseso adhigato phāsuvihāro”ti?
- **EN**: But as you live diligently like this, have you achieved any superhuman distinction in knowledge and vision worthy of the noble ones, a comfortable meditation?”

## mn31:10.3
- **PLI**: “Kiñhi no siyā, bhante.
- **EN**: “How could we not, sir?

## mn31:10.4
- **PLI**: Idha mayaṁ, bhante, yāvadeva ākaṅkhāma vivicceva kāmehi vivicca akusalehi dhammehi savitakkaṁ savicāraṁ vivekajaṁ pītisukhaṁ paṭhamaṁ jhānaṁ upasampajja viharāma.
- **EN**: Whenever we want, quite secluded from sensual pleasures, secluded from unskillful qualities, we enter and remain in the first absorption, which has the rapture and bliss born of seclusion, while placing the mind and keeping it connected.
- **NOTE**: The first absorption is defined at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-bu-vb-pj4/en/sujato#4.1.3.1'>Bu Pj 4:4.1.3.1</a> as the most basic “superhuman distinction”. Thus Anuruddha is answering the question directly but humbly, revealing as little as possible.

## mn31:10.5
- **PLI**: Ayaṁ kho no, bhante, amhākaṁ appamattānaṁ ātāpīnaṁ pahitattānaṁ viharantānaṁ uttari manussadhammā alamariyañāṇadassanaviseso adhigato phāsuvihāro”ti.
- **EN**: This is a superhuman distinction in knowledge and vision worthy of the noble ones, a comfortable meditation, that we have achieved while living diligent, keen, and resolute.”
