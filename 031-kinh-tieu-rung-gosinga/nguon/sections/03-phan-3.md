- **EN**: Then the native spirit Dīgha Parajana went up to the Buddha, bowed, stood to one side, and said to him,
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Parajana</i> means “stranger” (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/mil6.3.1/en/sujato#13.2'>Mil 6.3.1:13.2</a>) or “strange spirit” (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn25/en/sujato#5.20'>MN 25:5.20</a>). This passage shows how the intercession of even such a humble spirit can resound among all the gods.
