# Phần 4  (12 đoạn)

## mn49:30.9
- **PLI**: Asammāsambuddhāva pana te, pāpima, samānā sammāsambuddhāmhāti paṭijāniṁsu.
- **EN**: Those who formerly claimed to be fully awakened Buddhas were not in fact fully awakened Buddhas.

## mn49:30.10
- **PLI**: Ahaṁ kho pana, pāpima, sammāsambuddhova samāno sammāsambuddhomhīti paṭijānāmi.
- **EN**: But I am.

## mn49:30.11
- **PLI**: Desentopi hi, pāpima, tathāgato sāvakānaṁ dhammaṁ tādisova adesentopi hi, pāpima, tathāgato sāvakānaṁ dhammaṁ tādisova.
- **EN**: The Realized One remains such whether or not he teaches disciples.

## mn49:30.12
- **PLI**: Upanentopi hi, pāpima, tathāgato sāvake tādisova, anupanentopi hi, pāpima, tathāgato sāvake tādisova.
- **EN**: The Realized One remains as such whether or not he presents the teaching to disciples.

## mn49:30.13
- **PLI**: Taṁ kissa hetu?
- **EN**: Why is that?

## mn49:30.14
- **PLI**: Tathāgatassa, pāpima, ye āsavā saṅkilesikā ponobbhavikā sadarā dukkhavipākā āyatiṁ jātijarāmaraṇiyā—
- **EN**: Because the Realized One has given up the defilements that are corrupting, leading to future lives, hurtful, resulting in suffering and future rebirth, old age, and death.

## mn49:30.15
- **PLI**: te pahīnā ucchinnamūlā tālāvatthukatā anabhāvaṅkatā āyatiṁ anuppādadhammā.
- **EN**: He has cut them off at the root, made them like a palm stump, obliterated them so they are unable to arise in the future.

## mn49:30.16
- **PLI**: Seyyathāpi, pāpima, tālo matthakacchinno abhabbo puna virūḷhiyā;
- **EN**: Just as a palm tree with its crown cut off is incapable of further growth,

## mn49:30.17
- **PLI**: evameva kho, pāpima, tathāgatassa ye āsavā saṅkilesikā ponobbhavikā sadarā dukkhavipākā āyatiṁ jātijarāmaraṇiyā—
- **EN**: the Realized One has given up the defilements that are corrupting, leading to future lives, hurtful, resulting in suffering and future rebirth, old age, and death.

## mn49:30.18
- **PLI**: te pahīnā ucchinnamūlā tālāvatthukatā anabhāvaṅkatā āyatiṁ anuppādadhammā’ti.
- **EN**: He has cut them off at the root, made them like a palm stump, obliterated them so they are unable to arise in the future.’”

## mn49:31.1  `ms9M_1731, nya31`
- **PLI**: Iti hidaṁ mārassa ca anālapanatāya brahmuno ca abhinimantanatāya, tasmā imassa veyyākaraṇassa brahmanimantanikantveva adhivacanan”ti.
- **EN**: And so, because of the silencing of Māra, and because of the invitation of the Divinity, the name of this discussion is “On the Invitation of Divinity”.
- **NOTE**: There is no record of Baka’s conversion.

## mn49:31.2
- **PLI**: Brahmanimantanikasuttaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ navamaṁ.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)
