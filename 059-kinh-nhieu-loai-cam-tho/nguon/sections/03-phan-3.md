# Phần 3  (9 đoạn)

## mn59:16.1  `bj11.106, dr13.93, ms10M_251, msdiv91, nya16, vri13.70`
- **PLI**: Ṭhānaṁ kho panetaṁ, ānanda, vijjati yaṁ aññatitthiyā paribbājakā evaṁ vadeyyuṁ:
- **EN**: It’s possible that wanderers of other religions might say,

## mn59:16.2
- **PLI**: ‘saññāvedayitanirodhaṁ samaṇo gotamo āha; tañca sukhasmiṁ paññapeti.
- **EN**: ‘The ascetic Gotama spoke of the cessation of perception and feeling, and he includes it in happiness.

## mn59:16.3
- **PLI**: Tayidaṁ kiṁsu, tayidaṁ kathaṁsū’ti?
- **EN**: What’s up with that?’

## mn59:16.4
- **PLI**: Evaṁvādino, ānanda, aññatitthiyā paribbājakā evamassu vacanīyā:
- **EN**: When wanderers of other religions say this, you should say to them,

## mn59:16.5
- **PLI**: ‘na kho, āvuso, bhagavā sukhaṁyeva vedanaṁ sandhāya sukhasmiṁ paññapeti;
- **EN**: ‘Reverends, when the Buddha describes what’s included in happiness, he’s not just referring to pleasant feeling.

## mn59:16.6
- **PLI**: api ca, āvuso, yattha yattha sukhaṁ upalabbhati yahiṁ yahiṁ taṁ taṁ tathāgato sukhasmiṁ paññapetī’”ti.
- **EN**: The Realized One describes pleasure as included in happiness wherever it is found, and in whatever context.’”

## mn59:16.7  `cck13.104, ms10M_252`
- **PLI**: Idamavoca bhagavā.
- **EN**: That is what the Buddha said.

## mn59:16.8
- **PLI**: Attamano āyasmā ānando bhagavato bhāsitaṁ abhinandīti.
- **EN**: Satisfied, Venerable Ānanda approved what the Buddha said.

## mn59:16.9
- **PLI**: Bahuvedanīyasuttaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ navamaṁ.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)
