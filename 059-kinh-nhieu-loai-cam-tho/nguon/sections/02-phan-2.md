## mn59:6.8
- **PLI**: ime kho, ānanda, pañca kāmaguṇā.
- **EN**: These are the five kinds of sensual stimulation.

## mn59:6.9
- **PLI**: Yaṁ kho, ānanda, ime pañca kāmaguṇe paṭicca uppajjati sukhaṁ somanassaṁ idaṁ vuccati kāmasukhaṁ.
- **EN**: The pleasure and happiness that arise from these five kinds of sensual stimulation is called sensual pleasure.

- **EN**: There are those who might say that this is the highest pleasure and happiness that sentient beings experience. But I don’t grant them that.

## mn59:7.2
- **PLI**: Taṁ kissa hetu?
- **EN**: Why is that?

- **EN**: Because there is another pleasure that is finer than that.
- **NOTE**: In discussing the path, the Buddha focuses on pleasure.

- **EN**: It’s when a mendicant, quite secluded from sensual pleasures, secluded from unskillful qualities, enters and remains in the first absorption, which has the rapture and bliss born of seclusion, while placing the mind and keeping it connected.
