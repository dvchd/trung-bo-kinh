## mn77:6.30
- **PLI**: Bahū kho pana pūraṇassa kassapassa sāvakā vādaṁ āropetvā apakkantā:
- **EN**: Indeed, many of his disciples have left him after refuting his doctrine:

## mn77:6.31
- **PLI**: “na tvaṁ imaṁ dhammavinayaṁ ājānāsi, ahaṁ imaṁ dhammavinayaṁ ājānāmi, kiṁ tvaṁ imaṁ dhammavinayaṁ ājānissasi? Micchāpaṭipanno tvamasi, ahamasmi sammāpaṭipanno, sahitaṁ me, asahitaṁ te, purevacanīyaṁ pacchā avaca, pacchāvacanīyaṁ pure avaca, adhiciṇṇaṁ te viparāvattaṁ, āropito te vādo, niggahitosi, cara vādappamokkhāya, nibbeṭhehi vā sace pahosī”ti.
- **EN**: “You don’t understand this teaching and training. I understand this teaching and training. What, you understand this teaching and training? You’re practicing wrong. I’m practicing right. I stay on topic, you don’t. You said last what you should have said first. You said first what you should have said last. What you’ve thought so much about has been disproved. Your doctrine is refuted. Go on, save your doctrine! You’re trapped; get yourself out of this—if you can!”

## mn77:6.32
- **PLI**: Iti pūraṇo kassapo sāvakānaṁ na sakkato na garukato na mānito na pūjito, na ca pana pūraṇaṁ kassapaṁ sāvakā sakkatvā garuṁ katvā upanissāya viharanti.
- **EN**: That’s how Pūraṇa Kassapa is not honored, respected, revered, venerated, and esteemed by his disciples. On the contrary, his disciples, not honoring and respecting him, don’t remain loyal to him.

## mn77:6.33
- **PLI**: Akkuṭṭho ca pana pūraṇo kassapo dhammakkosenā’ti.
- **EN**: Rather, he’s reviled, and rightly so.’
- **NOTE**: Taking <i lang='pi' translate='no'>dhamma</i> here in the sense of “right, legitimate”, rather than “teaching”, as the example criticizes him not his teaching.

- **EN**: “Whatever the Buddha teaches, we shall listen to it.”

- **PLI**: Seyyathāpi nāma puriso cātummahāpathe khuddamadhuṁ anelakaṁ pīḷeyya.
- **EN**: It’s like when there’s a person at the crossroads pressing out pure dwarf-bee honey,
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Khuddā</i> (literally “small one”) is said to be a species of small bee, also known in Sanskrit as <i lang='pi' translate='no'>kṣudrā</i>. The “dwarf bee” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>apis florea</i>) fits the bill, as it is a small wild honeybee found in India.

## mn77:6.70
- **PLI**: Yepi samaṇassa gotamassa sāvakā sabrahmacārīhi sampayojetvā sikkhaṁ paccakkhāya hīnāyāvattanti tepi satthu ceva vaṇṇavādino honti, dhammassa ca vaṇṇavādino honti, saṅghassa ca vaṇṇavādino honti, attagarahinoyeva honti anaññagarahino, “mayamevamhā alakkhikā mayaṁ appapuññā te mayaṁ evaṁ svākkhāte dhammavinaye pabbajitvā nāsakkhimhā yāvajīvaṁ paripuṇṇaṁ parisuddhaṁ brahmacariyaṁ caritun”ti.
- **EN**: Even when disciples of the ascetic Gotama, having clashed with their spiritual companions, resign the training and return to a lesser life, they speak only praise of the Teacher, the teaching, and the Saṅgha. They blame only themselves, not others: “We were unlucky, we had little merit. For even after going forth in such a well explained teaching and training we weren’t able to practice for life the perfectly full and pure spiritual life.”
- **NOTE**: For “having clashed” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>sampayojetvā</i>), see <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn11.24/en/sujato#1.2'>SN 11.24:1.2</a>.
