- **EN**: Furthermore, I have explained to my disciples a practice that they use to develop the eight liberations.
- **NOTE**: The eight liberations (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>vimokkhā</i>) are an alternative way of describing the meditative experiences of <i lang='pi' translate='no'>jhāna</i>. Elsewhere they are listed at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn15/en/sujato#35.1'>DN 15:35.1</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn16/en/sujato#3.33.1'>DN 16:3.33.1</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn33/en/sujato#3.1.168'>DN 33:3.1.168</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn34/en/sujato#2.1.191'>DN 34:2.1.191</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an8.66/en/sujato'>AN 8.66</a>, and referred to at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an4.189/en/sujato#1.8'>AN 4.189:1.8</a> and <a href='https://suttacentral.net/thag20.1/en/sujato#33.1'>Thag 20.1:33.1</a>. At <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an8.120/en/sujato'>AN 8.120</a> and <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn137/en/sujato#27.1'>MN 137:27.1</a> they are listed but not called the eight liberations.

## mn77:22.2
- **PLI**: Rūpī rūpāni passati,
- **EN**: Having physical form, they see forms.
- **NOTE**: Someone sees a meditative vision based on the perception of their own body, such as through mindfulness of breathing or one’s own body parts. Such “visions” or “forms” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>rūpā</i>) are the lights or other meditation phenomena that today are sometimes called <i lang='pi' translate='no'>nimitta</i>. | The first three liberations all cover the four <i lang='pi' translate='no'>jhānas</i>.

## mn77:22.3
- **PLI**: ayaṁ paṭhamo vimokkho;
- **EN**: This is the first liberation.

## mn77:22.4
- **PLI**: ajjhattaṁ arūpasaññī bahiddhā rūpāni passati,
- **EN**: Not perceiving form internally, they see forms externally.
- **NOTE**: A meditator grounds their practice on some external focus, such as a light, the sight of a corpse, or an external element such as earth.

## mn77:22.5
- **PLI**: ayaṁ dutiyo vimokkho;
- **EN**: This is the second liberation.

## mn77:22.6
- **PLI**: subhanteva adhimutto hoti,
- **EN**: They’re focused only on beauty.
- **NOTE**: This is a practice based on wholly pure and exalted meditation, such as the meditation on love, or the sight of a pure brilliant color like the sky.

## mn77:22.7
- **PLI**: ayaṁ tatiyo vimokkho;
- **EN**: This is the third liberation.

## mn77:22.8
- **PLI**: sabbaso rūpasaññānaṁ samatikkamā paṭighasaññānaṁ atthaṅgamā nānattasaññānaṁ amanasikārā ‘ananto ākāso’ti ākāsānañcāyatanaṁ upasampajja viharati,
- **EN**: Going totally beyond perceptions of form, with the disappearance of perceptions of impingement, not focusing on perceptions of diversity, aware that ‘space is infinite’, they enter and remain in the dimension of infinite space.
