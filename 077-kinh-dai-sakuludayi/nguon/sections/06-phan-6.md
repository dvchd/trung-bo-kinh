## mn77:24.11
- **PLI**: viññāṇakasiṇameko sañjānāti uddhamadho tiriyaṁ advayaṁ appamāṇaṁ.
- **EN**: the meditation on universal consciousness above, below, across, undivided and limitless.
- **NOTE**: That Pali <i lang='pi' translate='no'>kasiṇa</i> (Sanskrit <i lang='pi' translate='no'>kṛtsna</i>) means “universal”, “totality” is shown by this passage where it is “undivided and limitless”. It is a name for the state of absorption, not for the meditation disk used in preliminary practice. Yājñavalkya says that, just as salt is “entirely” salty, the Self is an “entire mass of consciousness” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>kṛtsnaḥ prajñānaghana eva</i>, Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad 4.5.13).
