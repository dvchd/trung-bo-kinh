## mn15:6.1  `ms9M_627, msdiv183, ndp9.134, nya6, sya12.194`
- **PLI**: Tatrāvuso, bhikkhunā attanāva attānaṁ evaṁ anuminitabbaṁ:
- **EN**: In such a case, a mendicant should measure themselves like this.
- **NOTE**: “Should measure against” is <i lang='pi' translate='no'>anuminitabba</i>, which in its noun form <i lang='pi' translate='no'>anumāna</i> lends the sutta its title. The normal sense in later literature is “inference” (eg. <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mil6.4.1/en/sujato'>Mil 6.4.1</a>). <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Anumāna</i> occurs in the early texts only here and in the passive form <i lang='pi' translate='no'>anumīyati</i> at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn22.36/en/sujato#1.4'>SN 22.36:1.4</a>, where the sense must be to “measure against”. This passage also discusses how to measure oneself in relation to another, an external standard, whereas the next section on reflection applies an inner standard. Hence the commentary glosses with: “should compare, should judge” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>tuletabbo tīretabbo</i>).

## mn15:6.2
- **PLI**: ‘yo khvāyaṁ puggalo pāpiccho, pāpikānaṁ icchānaṁ vasaṁ gato, ayaṁ me puggalo appiyo amanāpo;
- **EN**: ‘This individual has corrupt wishes, having fallen under the sway of corrupt wishes. And I don’t like or approve of this individual.

## mn15:6.3
- **PLI**: ahañceva kho panassaṁ pāpiccho pāpikānaṁ icchānaṁ vasaṁ gato, ahampāssaṁ paresaṁ appiyo amanāpo’ti.
- **EN**: And if I were to fall under the sway of corrupt wishes, others wouldn’t like or approve of me.’

## mn15:6.4
- **PLI**: Evaṁ jānantenāvuso, bhikkhunā ‘na pāpiccho bhavissāmi, na pāpikānaṁ icchānaṁ vasaṁ gato’ti cittaṁ uppādetabbaṁ.
- **EN**: A mendicant who knows this should give rise to the thought: ‘I will not fall under the sway of corrupt wishes.’ …
