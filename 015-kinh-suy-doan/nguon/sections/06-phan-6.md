# Phần 6  (11 đoạn)

## mn15:7.92
- **PLI**: ‘asandiṭṭhiparāmāsī khomhi anādhānaggāhī suppaṭinissaggī’ti, tenāvuso, bhikkhunā teneva pītipāmojjena vihātabbaṁ ahorattānusikkhinā kusalesu dhammesu.
- **EN**: they’re not attached to their own views, holding them tight, but let them go easily. Then they should meditate with rapture and joy, training day and night in skillful qualities.

## mn15:8.1  `ms9M_659, nya8, vri12.144`
- **PLI**: Sace, āvuso, bhikkhu paccavekkhamāno sabbepime pāpake akusale dhamme appahīne attani samanupassati, tenāvuso, bhikkhunā sabbesaṁyeva imesaṁ pāpakānaṁ akusalānaṁ dhammānaṁ pahānāya vāyamitabbaṁ.
- **EN**: Suppose that, upon checking, a mendicant sees that they haven’t given up all these bad, unskillful qualities. Then they should make an effort to give them all up.

## mn15:8.2
- **PLI**: Sace panāvuso, bhikkhu paccavekkhamāno sabbepime pāpake akusale dhamme pahīne attani samanupassati, tenāvuso, bhikkhunā teneva pītipāmojjena vihātabbaṁ, ahorattānusikkhinā kusalesu dhammesu.
- **EN**: But suppose that, upon checking, a mendicant sees that they have given up all these bad, unskillful qualities. Then they should meditate with rapture and joy, training day and night in skillful qualities.

## mn15:8.3  `cck12.192, ms9M_660, ndp9.136, sya12.204`
- **PLI**: Seyyathāpi, āvuso, itthī vā puriso vā, daharo yuvā maṇḍanajātiko, ādāse vā parisuddhe pariyodāte, acche vā udakapatte, sakaṁ mukhanimittaṁ paccavekkhamāno, sace tattha passati rajaṁ vā aṅgaṇaṁ vā, tasseva rajassa vā aṅgaṇassa vā pahānāya vāyamati;
- **EN**: Suppose there was a woman or man who was young, youthful, and fond of adornments, and they check their own reflection in a clean bright mirror or a clear bowl of water. If they see any dirt or blemish there, they’d try to remove it.
- **NOTE**: Moggallāna also takes part in a similar conversation about blemishes at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn5/en/sujato#3.1'>MN 5:3.1</a>.

## mn15:8.4
- **PLI**: no ce tattha passati rajaṁ vā aṅgaṇaṁ vā, teneva attamano hoti:
- **EN**: But if they don’t see any dirt or blemish there, they’re happy, thinking:

## mn15:8.5
- **PLI**: ‘lābhā vata me, parisuddhaṁ vata me’ti.
- **EN**: ‘How fortunate that I’m clean!’

## mn15:8.6
- **PLI**: Evameva kho, āvuso, sace bhikkhu paccavekkhamāno sabbepime pāpake akusale dhamme appahīne attani samanupassati, tenāvuso, bhikkhunā sabbesaṁyeva imesaṁ pāpakānaṁ akusalānaṁ dhammānaṁ pahānāya vāyamitabbaṁ.
- **EN**: In the same way, suppose that, upon checking, a mendicant sees that they haven’t given up all these bad, unskillful qualities. Then they should make an effort to give them all up.

## mn15:8.7
- **PLI**: Sace panāvuso, bhikkhu paccavekkhamāno sabbepime pāpake akusale dhamme pahīne attani samanupassati, tenāvuso, bhikkhunā teneva pītipāmojjena vihātabbaṁ, ahorattānusikkhinā kusalesu dhammesū”ti.
- **EN**: But suppose that, upon checking, a mendicant sees that they have given up all these bad, unskillful qualities. Then they should meditate with rapture and joy, training day and night in skillful qualities.”
- **NOTE**: The Chinese parallels continue at this point, expanding the sequence that leads from joy to immersion to insight and liberation.

## mn15:8.8  `dr12.185, ms9M_661`
- **PLI**: Idamavocāyasmā mahāmoggallāno.
- **EN**: That is what Venerable Mahāmoggallāna said.

## mn15:8.9
- **PLI**: Attamanā te bhikkhū āyasmato mahāmoggallānassa bhāsitaṁ abhinandunti.
- **EN**: Satisfied, the mendicants approved what Mahāmoggallāna said.
- **NOTE**: The commentary says that the ancient teachers called this the “Bhikkhupātimokkha”, perhaps an echo of the memory that it was recited on the <i lang='pi' translate='no'>pavāraṇā</i>, which replaces the recitation of the Pātimokkha normally held on the full moon day. It goes on to say that a mendicant should use these teachings to review themselves three times a day, or at least once.

## mn15:8.10
- **PLI**: Anumānasuttaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ pañcamaṁ.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)
