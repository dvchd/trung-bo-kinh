## mn13:30.1  `ms9M_559, nya30`
- **PLI**: Kiñca, bhikkhave, rūpānaṁ nissaraṇaṁ?
- **EN**: And what is the escape from forms?

## mn13:30.2
- **PLI**: Yo, bhikkhave, rūpesu chandarāgavinayo chandarāgappahānaṁ—idaṁ rūpānaṁ nissaraṇaṁ.
- **EN**: Removing and giving up desire and greed for forms: this is the escape from forms.
- **NOTE**: Since “forms” also includes the refined visions of meditation, the full understanding of forms only occurs with arahantship.
