## mn91:32.10  `ms10M_1180, ndp10.393`
- **PLI**: “Kathaṁ kho brāhmaṇo hoti,
- **EN**: “How do you become a brahmin?
- **NOTE**: For some reason, the Buddha does not actually answer these questions, except the final two on the sage and the awakened one. Most of the others are technical terms in Brahmanism, and they are elsewhere defined by the Buddha in his own terms (eg. <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn39/en/sujato#23.1'>MN 39:23.1</a>ff.). The Chinese parallels at MA 161 and T 76 have fewer items than the Pali, but do a better job of matching the answers to the questions.

## mn91:32.11

- **EN**: And how do you become a knowledge master?
