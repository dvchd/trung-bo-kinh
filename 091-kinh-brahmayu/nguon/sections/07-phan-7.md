# Phần 7  (6 đoạn)

## mn91:39.5
- **PLI**: Tassa kā gati, ko abhisamparāyo”ti?
- **EN**: Where has he been reborn in his next life?”

## mn91:39.6
- **PLI**: “Paṇḍito, bhikkhave, brahmāyu brāhmaṇo paccapādi dhammassānudhammaṁ, na ca maṁ dhammādhikaraṇaṁ vihesesi.
- **EN**: “Mendicants, the brahmin Brahmāyu was astute. He practiced in line with the teaching, and did not trouble me about the teachings.

## mn91:39.7
- **PLI**: Brahmāyu, bhikkhave, brāhmaṇo pañcannaṁ orambhāgiyānaṁ saṁyojanānaṁ parikkhayā opapātiko hoti, tattha parinibbāyī, anāvattidhammo tasmā lokā”ti.
- **EN**: With the ending of the five lower fetters, he’s been reborn spontaneously and will become extinguished there, not liable to return from that world.”

## mn91:39.8  `ms10M_1189, sya13.547`
- **PLI**: Idamavoca bhagavā.
- **EN**: That is what the Buddha said.

## mn91:39.9
- **PLI**: Attamanā te bhikkhū bhagavato bhāsitaṁ abhinandunti.
- **EN**: Satisfied, the mendicants approved what the Buddha said.

## mn91:39.10
- **PLI**: Brahmāyusuttaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ paṭhamaṁ.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)
