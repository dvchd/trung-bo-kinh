# Phần 3  (50 đoạn)

## mn91:12.3
- **PLI**: na adduvena adduvaṁ āropetvā nisīdati; na ca gopphakena gopphakaṁ āropetvā nisīdati;
- **EN**: He doesn’t sit with his knees or ankles crossed.

## mn91:12.4
- **PLI**: na ca pāṇinā hanukaṁ upadahitvā nisīdati.
- **EN**: He doesn’t sit with his hand holding his chin.

## mn91:12.5
- **PLI**: So antaraghare nisinno samāno na chambhati na kampati na vedhati na paritassati. So achambhī akampī avedhī aparitassī vigatalomahaṁso.
- **EN**: When sitting in inhabited areas he doesn’t shake, tremble, quake, or get nervous, and so he is not nervous at all.

## mn91:12.6
- **PLI**: Vivekavatto ca so bhavaṁ gotamo antaraghare nisinno hoti.
- **EN**: When sitting in inhabited areas he still practices seclusion.

## mn91:13.1  `nya13`
- **PLI**: So pattodakaṁ paṭiggaṇhanto na pattaṁ unnāmeti, na pattaṁ onāmeti; na pattaṁ sannāmeti, na pattaṁ vināmeti.
- **EN**: When receiving water for rinsing the bowl, he holds the bowl neither too straight nor too bent, neither too tight nor too loose.

## mn91:14.1  `nya14`
- **PLI**: So pattodakaṁ paṭiggaṇhāti nātithokaṁ nātibahuṁ.
- **EN**: He receives neither too little nor too much water.

## mn91:14.2
- **PLI**: So na khulukhulukārakaṁ pattaṁ dhovati, na samparivattakaṁ pattaṁ dhovati, na pattaṁ bhūmiyaṁ nikkhipitvā hatthe dhovati; hatthesu dhotesu patto dhoto hoti, patte dhote hatthā dhotā honti.
- **EN**: He rinses the bowl without making a sloshing noise, or spinning it around. He doesn’t put the bowl on the ground to rinse his hands; his hands and bowl are rinsed at the same time.

## mn91:14.3
- **PLI**: So pattodakaṁ chaḍḍeti nātidūre nāccāsanne, na ca vicchaḍḍayamāno.
- **EN**: He doesn’t throw the bowl rinsing water away too far or too near, or splash it about.

## mn91:14.4
- **PLI**: So odanaṁ paṭiggaṇhanto na pattaṁ unnāmeti, na pattaṁ onāmeti; na pattaṁ sannāmeti, na pattaṁ vināmeti.
- **EN**: When receiving rice, he holds the bowl neither too straight nor too bent, neither too close nor too loose.

## mn91:14.5
- **PLI**: So odanaṁ paṭiggaṇhāti nātithokaṁ nātibahuṁ.
- **EN**: He receives neither too little nor too much rice.

## mn91:14.6
- **PLI**: Byañjanaṁ kho pana bhavaṁ gotamo byañjanamattāya āhāreti, na ca byañjanena ālopaṁ atināmeti.
- **EN**: He eats sauce in a moderate proportion, and doesn’t put too much sauce on his portions.

## mn91:14.7
- **PLI**: Dvattikkhattuṁ kho bhavaṁ gotamo mukhe ālopaṁ samparivattetvā ajjhoharati;
- **EN**: He chews over each portion two or three times before swallowing.

## mn91:14.8
- **PLI**: na cassa kāci odanamiñjā asambhinnā kāyaṁ pavisati, na cassa kāci odanamiñjā mukhe avasiṭṭhā hoti;
- **EN**: But no grain of rice enters his body unchewed, and none remain in his mouth.

## mn91:14.9
- **PLI**: athāparaṁ ālopaṁ upanāmeti.
- **EN**: Only then does he raise another portion to his lips.

## mn91:14.10
- **PLI**: Rasapaṭisaṁvedī kho pana so bhavaṁ gotamo āhāraṁ āhāreti, no ca rasarāgapaṭisaṁvedī.
- **EN**: He eats experiencing the taste, but without experiencing greed for the taste.

## mn91:17.1  `nya17`
- **PLI**: So bhuttāvī muhuttaṁ tuṇhī nisīdati, na ca anumodanassa kālamatināmeti.
- **EN**: After eating he sits a while in silence, but doesn’t wait too long to give the verses of appreciation.

## mn91:17.2
- **PLI**: So bhuttāvī anumodati, na taṁ bhattaṁ garahati, na aññaṁ bhattaṁ paṭikaṅkhati;
- **EN**: After eating he expresses appreciation without criticizing the meal or expecting another one.
- **NOTE**: This is the <i lang='pi' translate='no'>anumodanā</i> recited for the meal offering. Examples are found at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn16/en/sujato#1.31.1'>DN 16:1.31.1</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/snp3.7/en/sujato#34.3'>Snp 3.7:34.3</a> = <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn92/en/sujato#25.6'>MN 92:25.6</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn55.26/en/sujato#20.1'>SN 55.26:20.1</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-kd1/en/sujato#15.14.4'>Kd 1:15.14.4</a>, and <a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-kd1/en/sujato#1.5.1'>Kd 1:1.5.1</a>. | It is worth noting that no early <i lang='pi' translate='no'>anomodanā</i> uses the imperative verb form <i lang='pi' translate='no'>-tu</i> signifying giving a blessing (eg. <i lang='pi' translate='no'>bhavatu sabbamaṅgalaṁ</i>, “may all blessings be”). They strictly use the indicative <i lang='pi' translate='no'>-ti</i> to teach cause and effect: if you do this, that happens.
