- **EN**: Why don’t I make an effort to realize the same teaching that Āḷāra Kālāma says he has realized with his own insight?’

## mn26:15.21
- **PLI**: So kho ahaṁ, bhikkhave, nacirasseva khippameva taṁ dhammaṁ sayaṁ abhiññā sacchikatvā upasampajja vihāsiṁ.
- **EN**: I quickly realized that teaching with my own insight, and lived having achieved it.
- **NOTE**: This phrasing echoes part of the description of arahantship (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn85/en/sujato#51.12'>MN 85:51.12</a>).

## mn26:15.23  `dr12.285, ms9M_951`
- **PLI**: ‘Ettāvatā no, āvuso kālāma, imaṁ dhammaṁ sayaṁ abhiññā sacchikatvā upasampajja pavedesī’ti?
- **EN**: ‘Reverend Kālāma, is it up to this point that you realized this teaching with your own insight, and declare having achieved it?’
- **NOTE**: Having achieved that state, Āḷāra Kālāma “declares” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>pavedeti</i>) it as the teacher, while the Bodhisatta “dwells” in it (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>viharati</i>).
