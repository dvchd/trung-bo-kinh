## mn87:28.2
- **PLI**: piyā te kāsikosalā”ti?
- **EN**: Do you love the realms of Kāsi and Kosala?”
- **NOTE**: Kosala is the native realm of Pasenadi. Kāsi had formerly been an independent kingdom, but was taken over by Pasenadi’s father Mahākosala. Towards the end of the Buddha’s life it was contested between Kosala and Magadha (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn3.14/en/sujato'>SN 3.14</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn3.15/en/sujato'>SN 3.15</a>). Ultimately it became part of the greater Magadhan empire.
