- **EN**: Or how do you see this?”

- **PLI**: “Addhā kho, bho kaccāna, evaṁ sante, ime cattāro vaṇṇā samasamā honti.

- **PLI**: Nesaṁ ettha kiñci nānākaraṇaṁ samanupassāmī”ti.
- **EN**: I can’t see any difference between them.”
