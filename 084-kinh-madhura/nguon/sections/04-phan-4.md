# Phần 4  (19 đoạn)

## mn84:9.19
- **PLI**: ‘brāhmaṇova seṭṭho vaṇṇo, hīno añño vaṇṇo;
- **EN**: ‘Only brahmins are the best class; other classes are inferior.

## mn84:9.20
- **PLI**: brāhmaṇova sukko vaṇṇo, kaṇho añño vaṇṇo;
- **EN**: Only brahmins are the light class; other classes are dark.

## mn84:9.21
- **PLI**: brāhmaṇāva sujjhanti, no abrāhmaṇā;
- **EN**: Only brahmins are purified, not others.

## mn84:9.22
- **PLI**: brāhmaṇāva brahmuno puttā orasā mukhato jātā brahmajā brahmanimmitā brahmadāyādā’”ti.
- **EN**: Only brahmins are the Divinity’s bosom sons, born from his mouth, born of the Divinity, created by the Divinity, heirs of the Divinity.’”

## mn84:10.1  `bj11.478, cck13.430, csp1ed10.277, csp2ed10.277, dr13.403, ms10M_921, msdiv323, ndp10.317, nya10, pts-vp-pli2.90, sya13.439, vri13.286`
- **PLI**: Evaṁ vutte, rājā mādhuro avantiputto āyasmantaṁ mahākaccānaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: When he had spoken, King Avantiputta of Madhurā said to Mahākaccāna,

## mn84:10.2
- **PLI**: “abhikkantaṁ, bho kaccāna, abhikkantaṁ, bho kaccāna.
- **EN**: “Excellent, worthy Kaccāna! Excellent!

## mn84:10.3
- **PLI**: Seyyathāpi, bho kaccāna, nikkujjitaṁ vā ukkujjeyya, paṭicchannaṁ vā vivareyya, mūḷhassa vā maggaṁ ācikkheyya, andhakāre vā telapajjotaṁ dhāreyya ‘cakkhumanto rūpāni dakkhantī’ti; evamevaṁ bhotā kaccānena anekapariyāyena dhammo pakāsito.
- **EN**: As if he were righting the overturned, or revealing the hidden, or pointing out the path to the lost, or lighting a lamp in the dark so people with clear eyes can see what’s there, the worthy Kaccāna has made the teaching clear in many ways.

## mn84:10.4
- **PLI**: Esāhaṁ bhavantaṁ kaccānaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi dhammañca bhikkhusaṅghañca.
- **EN**: I go for refuge to the worthy Kaccāna, to the teaching, and to the mendicant Saṅgha.

## mn84:10.5
- **PLI**: Upāsakaṁ maṁ bhavaṁ kaccāno dhāretu ajjatagge pāṇupetaṁ saraṇaṁ gatan”ti.
- **EN**: From this day forth, may the worthy Kaccāna remember me as a lay follower who has gone for refuge for life.”

## mn84:10.6
- **PLI**: “Mā kho maṁ tvaṁ, mahārāja, saraṇaṁ agamāsi.
- **EN**: “Great king, don’t go for refuge to me.

## mn84:10.7
- **PLI**: Tameva tvaṁ bhagavantaṁ saraṇaṁ gaccha yamahaṁ saraṇaṁ gato”ti.
- **EN**: You should go for refuge to that same Blessed One to whom I have gone for refuge.”

## mn84:10.8
- **PLI**: “Kahaṁ pana, bho kaccāna, etarahi so bhagavā viharati arahaṁ sammāsambuddho”ti?
- **EN**: “But where is that Blessed One at present, the perfected one, the fully awakened Buddha?”

## mn84:10.9
- **PLI**: “Parinibbuto kho, mahārāja, etarahi so bhagavā arahaṁ sammāsambuddho”ti.
- **EN**: “Great king, the Buddha has already become fully quenched.”

## mn84:11.1  `nya11`
- **PLI**: “Sacepi mayaṁ, bho kaccāna, suṇeyyāma taṁ bhagavantaṁ dasasu yojanesu, dasapi mayaṁ yojanāni gaccheyyāma taṁ bhagavantaṁ dassanāya arahantaṁ sammāsambuddhaṁ.
- **EN**: “Worthy Kaccāna, if I heard that the Buddha was within ten leagues, or twenty, or even up to a hundred leagues away, I’d go a hundred leagues to see him.

## mn84:11.2
- **PLI**: Sacepi mayaṁ, bho kaccāna, suṇeyyāma taṁ bhagavantaṁ vīsatiyā yojanesu, tiṁsāya yojanesu, cattārīsāya yojanesu, paññāsāya yojanesu, paññāsampi mayaṁ yojanāni gaccheyyāma taṁ bhagavantaṁ dassanāya arahantaṁ sammāsambuddhaṁ.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)

## mn84:11.3
- **PLI**: Yojanasate cepi mayaṁ bho kaccāna, suṇeyyāma taṁ bhagavantaṁ, yojanasatampi mayaṁ gaccheyyāma taṁ bhagavantaṁ dassanāya arahantaṁ sammāsambuddhaṁ.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)

## mn84:11.4
- **PLI**: Yato ca, bho kaccāna, parinibbuto so bhagavā, parinibbutampi mayaṁ bhagavantaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāma dhammañca bhikkhusaṅghañca.
- **EN**: But since the Buddha has become fully quenched, I go for refuge to that fully quenched Buddha, to the teaching, and to the mendicant Saṅgha.

## mn84:11.5
- **PLI**: Upāsakaṁ maṁ bhavaṁ kaccāno dhāretu ajjatagge pāṇupetaṁ saraṇaṁ gatan”ti.
- **EN**: From this day forth, may the worthy Kaccāna remember me as a lay follower who has gone for refuge for life.”
