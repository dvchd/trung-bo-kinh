## mn55:11.1  `cck13.54, ms10M_115, ndp10.42, nya11, pts-vp-pli1.371`

## mn55:11.6
- **PLI**: “Yena kho, jīvaka, rāgena yena dosena yena mohena vihesavā assa arativā assa paṭighavā assa so rāgo so doso so moho tathāgatassa pahīno ucchinnamūlo tālāvatthukato anabhāvaṅkato āyatiṁ anuppādadhammo.
- **EN**: “Any greed, hate, or delusion that might give rise to cruelty, discontent, or aversion has been given up by the Realized One, cut off at the root, made like a palm stump, obliterated, and is unable to arise in the future.
- **NOTE**: Just as ill will is the opposite of love, “cruelty” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>vihesā</i>), “discontent” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>arati</i>), and “repulsion” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>paṭigha</i>) are the respective opposites of compassion, rejoicing, and equanimity (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn62/en/sujato#18.2'>MN 62:18.2</a> ff).

- **EN**: If that’s what you were referring to, I grant it.”

## mn55:11.8

## mn55:12.1  `bj11.58, dr13.49, ms10M_116, msdiv55, nya12, sya13.52, vri13.38`
- **PLI**: “Yo kho, jīvaka, tathāgataṁ vā tathāgatasāvakaṁ vā uddissa pāṇaṁ ārabhati so pañcahi ṭhānehi bahuṁ apuññaṁ pasavati.
- **EN**: “Jīvaka, anyone who slaughters a living creature specially for the Realized One or the Realized One’s disciple brims with much wickedness for five reasons.

## mn55:12.2
- **PLI**: Yampi so, gahapati, evamāha:
- **EN**: When they say:

## mn55:12.3
- **PLI**: ‘gacchatha, amukaṁ nāma pāṇaṁ ānethā’ti, iminā paṭhamena ṭhānena bahuṁ apuññaṁ pasavati.
- **EN**: ‘Go, fetch that living creature,’ this is the first reason.

## mn55:12.4
- **PLI**: Yampi so pāṇo galappaveṭhakena ānīyamāno dukkhaṁ domanassaṁ paṭisaṁvedeti, iminā dutiyena ṭhānena bahuṁ apuññaṁ pasavati.
- **EN**: When that living creature experiences pain and sadness as it’s led along by a collar, this is the second reason.

## mn55:12.5
- **PLI**: Yampi so evamāha:
- **EN**: When they say:

## mn55:12.6
- **PLI**: ‘gacchatha imaṁ pāṇaṁ ārabhathā’ti, iminā tatiyena ṭhānena bahuṁ apuññaṁ pasavati.
- **EN**: ‘Go, slaughter that living creature,’ this is the third reason.

## mn55:12.7
- **PLI**: Yampi so pāṇo ārabhiyamāno dukkhaṁ domanassaṁ paṭisaṁvedeti, iminā catutthena ṭhānena bahuṁ apuññaṁ pasavati.
- **EN**: When that living creature experiences pain and sadness as it’s being slaughtered, this is the fourth reason.

## mn55:12.8
- **PLI**: Yampi so tathāgataṁ vā tathāgatasāvakaṁ vā akappiyena āsādeti, iminā pañcamena ṭhānena bahuṁ apuññaṁ pasavati.
- **EN**: When they serve the Realized One or the Realized One’s disciple with unallowable food, this is the fifth reason.

## mn55:12.9
- **PLI**: Yo kho, jīvaka, tathāgataṁ vā tathāgatasāvakaṁ vā uddissa pāṇaṁ ārabhati so imehi pañcahi ṭhānehi bahuṁ apuññaṁ pasavatī”ti.
- **EN**: Anyone who slaughters a living creature specially for the Realized One or the Realized One’s disciple brims with much wickedness for five reasons.”

- **EN**: Excellent, sir! Excellent! …

## mn55:13.6
- **PLI**: upāsakaṁ maṁ bhagavā dhāretu ajjatagge pāṇupetaṁ saraṇaṁ gatan”ti.
- **EN**: From this day forth, may the Buddha remember me as a lay follower who has gone for refuge for life.”
- **NOTE**: This sutta marks the occasion of Jīvaka’s conversion. It would seem that he had previously harbored doubts due to his Jain upbringing. Elsewhere he appears as a devotee of the Buddha. <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an1.256/en/sujato#1.1'>AN 1.256:1.1</a> records that he was the foremost disciple with “confidence in a person”, establishing that he was a stream-enterer. The occasion of his stream-entry, however, is not recorded in Pali.
