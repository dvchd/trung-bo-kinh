# Phần 1  (50 đoạn)

## mn46:1.1  `bj10.728, cck12.537, dr12.496, ms9M_1654, msdiv473, ndp9.380, nya1, pts-vp-pli1.310, sya12.563, vri12.391`
- **PLI**: Evaṁ me sutaṁ—
- **EN**: So I have heard.
- **NOTE**: The Buddha delves into this topic in more detail in <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn114/en/sujato'>MN 114</a>.

## mn46:1.2
- **PLI**: ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme.
- **EN**: At one time the Buddha was staying near Sāvatthī in Jeta’s Grove, Anāthapiṇḍika’s monastery.

## mn46:1.3
- **PLI**: Tatra kho bhagavā bhikkhū āmantesi:
- **EN**: There the Buddha addressed the mendicants,

## mn46:1.4
- **PLI**: “bhikkhavo”ti.
- **EN**: “Mendicants!”

## mn46:1.5
- **PLI**: “Bhadante”ti te bhikkhū bhagavato paccassosuṁ.
- **EN**: “Venerable sir,” they replied.

## mn46:1.6
- **PLI**: Bhagavā etadavoca:
- **EN**: The Buddha said this:

## mn46:2.1  `nya2`
- **PLI**: “yebhuyyena, bhikkhave, sattā evaṅkāmā evaṁchandā evaṁadhippāyā:
- **EN**: “Mendicants, sentient beings typically have the wish, desire, and hope:

## mn46:2.2
- **PLI**: ‘aho vata aniṭṭhā akantā amanāpā dhammā parihāyeyyuṁ, iṭṭhā kantā manāpā dhammā abhivaḍḍheyyun’ti.
- **EN**: ‘Oh, if only unlikable, undesirable, and disagreeable things would decrease, and likable, desirable, and agreeable things would increase!’

## mn46:2.3
- **PLI**: Tesaṁ, bhikkhave, sattānaṁ evaṅkāmānaṁ evaṁchandānaṁ evaṁadhippāyānaṁ aniṭṭhā akantā amanāpā dhammā abhivaḍḍhanti, iṭṭhā kantā manāpā dhammā parihāyanti.
- **EN**: But exactly the opposite happens to them.

## mn46:2.4
- **PLI**: Tatra tumhe, bhikkhave, kaṁ hetuṁ paccethā”ti?
- **EN**: What do you take to be the reason for this?”

## mn46:2.5
- **PLI**: “Bhagavaṁmūlakā no, bhante, dhammā, bhagavaṁnettikā, bhagavaṁpaṭisaraṇā. Sādhu vata, bhante, bhagavantaññeva paṭibhātu etassa bhāsitassa attho; bhagavato sutvā bhikkhū dhāressantī”ti.
- **EN**: “Our teachings are rooted in the Buddha. He is our guide and our refuge. Sir, may the Buddha himself please clarify the meaning of this. The mendicants will listen and remember it.”

## mn46:2.6
- **PLI**: “Tena hi, bhikkhave, suṇātha, sādhukaṁ manasi karotha, bhāsissāmī”ti.
- **EN**: “Well then, mendicants, listen and apply your mind well, I will speak.”

## mn46:2.7
- **PLI**: “Evaṁ, bhante”ti kho te bhikkhū bhagavato paccassosuṁ.
- **EN**: “Yes, sir,” they replied.

## mn46:2.8
- **PLI**: Bhagavā etadavoca:
- **EN**: The Buddha said this:

## mn46:3.1  `cck12.538, csp1ed9.385, csp2ed9.385, dr12.497, ms9M_1655, msdiv474, ndp9.381, nya3, sya12.564`
- **PLI**: “Idha, bhikkhave, assutavā puthujjano, ariyānaṁ adassāvī ariyadhammassa akovido ariyadhamme avinīto, sappurisānaṁ adassāvī sappurisadhammassa akovido sappurisadhamme avinīto,
- **EN**: “Take an unlearned ordinary person who has not seen the noble ones, and is neither skilled nor trained in the teaching of the noble ones. They’ve not seen true persons, and are neither skilled nor trained in the teaching of the true persons.

## mn46:3.2
- **PLI**: sevitabbe dhamme na jānāti asevitabbe dhamme na jānāti, bhajitabbe dhamme na jānāti abhajitabbe dhamme na jānāti.
- **EN**: They don’t know what practices they should cultivate and foster, and what practices they shouldn’t cultivate and foster.

## mn46:3.3
- **PLI**: So sevitabbe dhamme ajānanto asevitabbe dhamme ajānanto, bhajitabbe dhamme ajānanto abhajitabbe dhamme ajānanto, asevitabbe dhamme sevati sevitabbe dhamme na sevati, abhajitabbe dhamme bhajati bhajitabbe dhamme na bhajati.
- **EN**: So they cultivate and foster practices they shouldn’t, and don’t cultivate and foster practices they should.

## mn46:3.4
- **PLI**: Tassa asevitabbe dhamme sevato sevitabbe dhamme asevato, abhajitabbe dhamme bhajato bhajitabbe dhamme abhajato aniṭṭhā akantā amanāpā dhammā abhivaḍḍhanti, iṭṭhā kantā manāpā dhammā parihāyanti.
- **EN**: When they do so, unlikable, undesirable, and disagreeable things increase, and likable, desirable, and agreeable things decrease.

## mn46:3.5
- **PLI**: Taṁ kissa hetu?
- **EN**: Why is that?

## mn46:3.6
- **PLI**: Evañhetaṁ, bhikkhave, hoti yathā taṁ aviddasuno.
- **EN**: Because that’s what it’s like for the ignorant.
