- **EN**: When it comes to the way of taking up practices that is pleasant now and results in future pleasure, an ignoramus …

## mn46:9.2
- **PLI**: ‘idaṁ kho dhammasamādānaṁ paccuppannasukhañceva āyatiñca sukhavipākan’ti.

- **PLI**: Tassa taṁ asevato, taṁ parivajjayato, aniṭṭhā akantā amanāpā dhammā abhivaḍḍhanti, iṭṭhā kantā manāpā dhammā parihāyanti.
- **EN**: and disagreeable things increase …

- **PLI**: Taṁ kissa hetu?

- **EN**: Because that’s what it’s like for the ignorant.
