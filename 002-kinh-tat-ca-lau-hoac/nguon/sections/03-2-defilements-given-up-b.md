# 2. Defilements Given Up by Restraint  (12 đoạn)

## mn2:12.0
- **PLI**: 2. Saṁvarāpahātabbaāsava
- **EN**: 2. Defilements Given Up by Restraint

## mn2:12.1  `cck12.16, dr12.15, ms9M_65, msdiv22, nya12, sya12.17`
- **PLI**: Katame ca, bhikkhave, āsavā saṁvarā pahātabbā?
- **EN**: And what are the defilements that should be given up by restraint?
- **NOTE**: In the Gradual Training (eg. <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn38/en/sujato#35.1'>MN 38:35.1</a>), sense restraint (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>saṁvarā</i>) comes before seeing the four noble truths. The sequence in this sutta does not follow the order of practice; rather, it starts and ends with the most important items.

## mn2:12.2
- **PLI**: Idha, bhikkhave, bhikkhu paṭisaṅkhā yoniso cakkhundriyasaṁvarasaṁvuto viharati.
- **EN**: Take a mendicant who, reflecting rationally, lives restraining the eye faculty.

## mn2:12.3
- **PLI**: Yañhissa, bhikkhave, cakkhundriyasaṁvaraṁ asaṁvutassa viharato uppajjeyyuṁ āsavā vighātapariḷāhā, cakkhundriyasaṁvaraṁ saṁvutassa viharato evaṁsa te āsavā vighātapariḷāhā na honti.
- **EN**: For the distressing and feverish defilements that might arise in someone who lives without restraint of the eye faculty do not arise when there is such restraint.
- **NOTE**: Sense restraint is not about denial, but about freeing the mind from addiction.

## mn2:12.4
- **PLI**: Paṭisaṅkhā yoniso sotindriyasaṁvarasaṁvuto viharati …pe…
- **EN**: Reflecting rationally, they live restraining the ear faculty …

## mn2:12.5
- **PLI**: ghānindriyasaṁvarasaṁvuto viharati …pe…
- **EN**: the nose faculty …

## mn2:12.6
- **PLI**: jivhindriyasaṁvarasaṁvuto viharati …pe…
- **EN**: the tongue faculty …

## mn2:12.7
- **PLI**: kāyindriyasaṁvarasaṁvuto viharati …pe…
- **EN**: the body faculty …

## mn2:12.8
- **PLI**: manindriyasaṁvarasaṁvuto viharati.
- **EN**: the mind faculty.

## mn2:12.9
- **PLI**: Yañhissa, bhikkhave, manindriyasaṁvaraṁ asaṁvutassa viharato uppajjeyyuṁ āsavā vighātapariḷāhā, manindriyasaṁvaraṁ saṁvutassa viharato evaṁsa te āsavā vighātapariḷāhā na honti.
- **EN**: For the distressing and feverish defilements that might arise in someone who lives without restraint of the mind faculty do not arise when there is such restraint.
- **NOTE**: In the first and last items in this sutta, “giving up” defilements means their permanent eradication by means of the noble path. For the interim items, however, “giving up” refers to the more modest goal of practicing so that they do not arise in the mind. This creates the supportive conditions for deeper realizations, while giving the mind time for understanding to mature.

## mn2:12.10  `ms9M_66, pts-vp-pli1.10`
- **PLI**: Yañhissa, bhikkhave, saṁvaraṁ asaṁvutassa viharato uppajjeyyuṁ āsavā vighātapariḷāhā, saṁvaraṁ saṁvutassa viharato evaṁsa te āsavā vighātapariḷāhā na honti.
- **EN**: For the distressing and feverish defilements that might arise in someone who lives without restraint do not arise when there is such restraint.

## mn2:12.11
- **PLI**: Ime vuccanti, bhikkhave, āsavā saṁvarā pahātabbā.
- **EN**: These are called the defilements that should be given up by restraint.
