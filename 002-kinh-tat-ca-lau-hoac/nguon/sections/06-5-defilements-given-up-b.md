# 5. Defilements Given Up by Avoiding  (6 đoạn)

## mn2:19.0
- **PLI**: 5. Parivajjanāpahātabbaāsava
- **EN**: 5. Defilements Given Up by Avoiding

## mn2:19.1  `cck12.18, csp1ed9.14, csp2ed9.14, ms9M_74, msdiv25, ndp9.16, nya19, pts-vp-pli1.11, sya12.19, vri12.15`
- **PLI**: Katame ca, bhikkhave, āsavā parivajjanā pahātabbā?
- **EN**: And what are the defilements that should be given up by avoiding?
- **NOTE**: This item shows that endurance is not always the right response to adversity. Some things are best avoided (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>parivajjana</i>) where possible. This is different, of course, from a strategy or compulsion of avoiding things that are uncomfortable.

## mn2:19.2
- **PLI**: Idha, bhikkhave, bhikkhu paṭisaṅkhā yoniso caṇḍaṁ hatthiṁ parivajjeti, caṇḍaṁ assaṁ parivajjeti, caṇḍaṁ goṇaṁ parivajjeti, caṇḍaṁ kukkuraṁ parivajjeti, ahiṁ khāṇuṁ kaṇṭakaṭṭhānaṁ sobbhaṁ papātaṁ candanikaṁ oḷigallaṁ.
- **EN**: Take a mendicant who, reflecting rationally, avoids a wild elephant, a wild horse, a wild ox, a wild dog, a snake, a stump, thorny ground, a pit, a cliff, a swamp, and a sewer.

## mn2:19.3
- **PLI**: Yathārūpe anāsane nisinnaṁ yathārūpe agocare carantaṁ yathārūpe pāpake mitte bhajantaṁ viññū sabrahmacārī pāpakesu ṭhānesu okappeyyuṁ, so tañca anāsanaṁ tañca agocaraṁ te ca pāpake mitte paṭisaṅkhā yoniso parivajjeti.
- **EN**: Reflecting rationally, they avoid sitting on inappropriate seats, walking in inappropriate neighborhoods, and mixing with bad friends—whatever sensible spiritual companions would believe to be a bad setting.
- **NOTE**: This introduces the hard-to-translate idea of <i lang='pi' translate='no'>gocara</i>, literally “cowpasture”. It refers to the places or people to whom a mendicant “resorts”, especially when on alms. What is appropriate depends on context. For example, whereas there is no issue with accepting a meal from a sex worker (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn16/en/sujato#2.14.7'>DN 16:2.14.7</a>), it would provoke suspicion if a monk entered a brothel for the meal. | <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Okappeti</i> means “believe, trust” rather than “suspect”.

## mn2:19.4  `ms9M_75`
- **PLI**: Yañhissa, bhikkhave, aparivajjayato uppajjeyyuṁ āsavā vighātapariḷāhā, parivajjayato evaṁsa te āsavā vighātapariḷāhā na honti.
- **EN**: For the distressing and feverish defilements that might arise in someone who lives without avoiding these things do not arise when they are avoided.

## mn2:19.5
- **PLI**: Ime vuccanti, bhikkhave, āsavā parivajjanā pahātabbā.
- **EN**: These are called the defilements that should be given up by avoiding.
