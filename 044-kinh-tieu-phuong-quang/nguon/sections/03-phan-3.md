## mn44:28.6
- **PLI**: ‘kudāssu nāmāhaṁ tadāyatanaṁ upasampajja viharissāmi yadariyā etarahi āyatanaṁ upasampajja viharantī’ti?
- **EN**: ‘Oh, when will I enter and remain in the same dimension that the noble ones enter and remain in today?’
- **NOTE**: Also at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn137/en/sujato#13.3'>MN 137:13.3</a>. Sometimes, “that dimension” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>tadāyatana</i>) is said to be Nibbāna (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/ud8.1/en/sujato#3.1'>Ud 8.1:3.1</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn35.117/en/sujato#8.2'>SN 35.117:8.2</a>), but it is also used of the stages of <i lang='pi' translate='no'>samādhi</i> (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/an9.43/en/sujato#2.2'>AN 9.43:2.2</a>). That must be the case here, since it is identified with (one of) the several “liberations” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>vimokkhā</i>), which typically refer to various states of <i lang='pi' translate='no'>samādhi</i> (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn15/en/sujato#35.1'>DN 15:35.1</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn6/en/sujato#10.1'>MN 6:10.1</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-kd2/en/sujato#3.7.6'>Kd 2:3.7.6</a>).

- **EN**: Nursing such a longing for the supreme liberations gives rise to sadness due to longing.
- **NOTE**: This is a kind of “suffering not of the flesh” (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn10/en/sujato#32.8'>MN 10:32.8</a>).

- **EN**: With this they give up aversion, and the underlying tendency to aversion does not lie within that.

## mn44:28.9

- **EN**: Take a mendicant who, with the giving up of pleasure and pain and the disappearance of former happiness and sadness, enters and remains in the fourth absorption, without pleasure or pain, with pure equanimity and mindfulness.

- **EN**: With this they give up ignorance, and the underlying tendency to ignorance does not lie within that.”
