## mn76:16.2
- **PLI**: ‘sattime kāyā akaṭā akaṭavidhā animmitā animmātā vañjhā kūṭaṭṭhā esikaṭṭhāyiṭṭhitā,
- **EN**: ‘There are these seven substances that are not made, not derived, not created, without a creator, barren, steady as a mountain peak, standing firm like a pillar.
- **NOTE**: This combines the view attributed to Pakudha Kaccāyana (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn2/en/sujato#26.2'>DN 2:26.2</a>) with the second part of Gosāla’s view (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn2/en/sujato#20.7'>DN 2:20.7</a>). There are several inconsistencies in the presentation of these views in Pali, and still more when parallels are considered. These were closely-related teachers, who often practiced together for a time, and it is hardly surprising that they should share some views in common, or that their exact views have become confused.

- **EN**: They don’t move or deteriorate or obstruct each other. They’re unable to cause pleasure, pain, or both pleasure and pain to each other.
