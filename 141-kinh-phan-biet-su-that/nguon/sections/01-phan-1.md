## mn141:2.1  `cck14.411, dr13.409, ms11M_1046, nya2, sya14.450`
- **PLI**: “Tathāgatena, bhikkhave, arahatā sammāsambuddhena bārāṇasiyaṁ isipatane migadāye anuttaraṁ dhammacakkaṁ pavattitaṁ appaṭivattiyaṁ samaṇena vā brāhmaṇena vā devena vā mārena vā brahmunā vā kenaci vā lokasmiṁ, yadidaṁ—

- **NOTE**: This is in reference to the Dhammacakkappavattanasutta, the so-called First Sermon (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn56.11/en/sujato'>SN 56.11</a>), for which the current discourse serves as a canonical commentary.

## mn141:3.1  `nya3`
- **PLI**: Dukkhassa ariyasaccassa ācikkhanā desanā paññāpanā paṭṭhapanā vivaraṇā vibhajanā uttānīkammaṁ, dukkhasamudayassa ariyasaccassa ācikkhanā desanā paññāpanā paṭṭhapanā vivaraṇā vibhajanā uttānīkammaṁ, dukkhanirodhassa ariyasaccassa ācikkhanā desanā paññāpanā paṭṭhapanā vivaraṇā vibhajanā uttānīkammaṁ, dukkhanirodhagāminiyā paṭipadāya ariyasaccassa ācikkhanā desanā paññāpanā paṭṭhapanā vivaraṇā vibhajanā uttānīkammaṁ.
- **EN**: The noble truths of suffering, the origin of suffering, the cessation of suffering, and the practice that leads to the cessation of suffering.
- **NOTE**: The four noble truths are sometimes said to be like a doctor who identifies the illness, discerns its cause, understands the recovery, and administers the cure until the patient is restored to health (SA 389 at T ii 105a25; SA-2 254 at T ii 462c10). This analogy is not found in the Pali canon.

- **EN**: They’re astute, and they support their spiritual companions.

## mn141:5.4
- **PLI**: Seyyathāpi, bhikkhave, janetā, evaṁ sāriputto;
- **EN**: Sāriputta is like one who gives birth,
- **NOTE**: To extol his leading students, the Buddha recalls his own upbringing, born of Māyā and raised by Mahāpajāpatī (who appears in the next discourse). Nonetheless, <i lang='pi' translate='no'>janetā</i> is a masculine noun and it would be misleading to translate it as “mother”. Both mother and father “give birth” (Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad 6.4.14; the creator is male at 1.4.4).

## mn141:5.5
- **PLI**: seyyathāpi jātassa āpādetā, evaṁ moggallāno.
- **EN**: while Moggallāna is like one who raises the child.
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Āpādetā</i> is a causative agent noun from <i lang='pi' translate='no'>ā</i> + <i lang='pi' translate='no'>√pad</i> in the sense “one who raises”. Again, both mother and father are “raisers” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>āpādakā</i>) of the child (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/an4.63/en/sujato#2.6'>AN 4.63:2.6</a>).

## mn141:5.6

- **EN**: Sāriputta guides people to the fruit of stream-entry, Moggallāna to the highest goal.

- **EN**: Sāriputta is able to explain, teach, assert, establish, clarify, analyze, and reveal the four noble truths in detail.”
