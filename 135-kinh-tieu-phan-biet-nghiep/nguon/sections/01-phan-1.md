## mn135:1.1  `bj12.432, dr13.342, ms11M_874, msdiv289, ndp11.280, nya1, sya14.376, vri14.250`
- **PLI**: Evaṁ me sutaṁ—
- **EN**: So I have heard.
- **NOTE**: This discourse has more parallels than any other Majjhima sutta: six in Chinese, two in Tibetan, two in Sanskrit, and fragments in Khotanese, Sogdian, and Tocharian. Its popularity is due to the detailed yet straightforward way it connects deeds with results, a subject of perennial fascination.

## mn135:5.1  `ms11M_878, nya5, vri14.251`
- **PLI**: “Idha, māṇava, ekacco itthī vā puriso vā pāṇātipātī hoti luddo lohitapāṇi hatapahate niviṭṭho adayāpanno pāṇabhūtesu.
- **EN**: “Take some woman or man who kills living creatures. They’re violent, bloody-handed, a hardened killer, merciless to living beings.
- **NOTE**: The phrase “some woman or man” is used only here and in a few suttas that also deal with kamma (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/an3.70/en/sujato'>AN 3.70</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an8.42/en/sujato'>AN 8.42</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an8.43/en/sujato'>AN 8.43</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an8.45/en/sujato'>AN 8.45</a>). The mention of “woman or man” undercuts assumptions that women lack agency, or that their rebirth is lesser.

## mn135:5.2
- **PLI**: So tena kammena evaṁ samattena evaṁ samādinnena kāyassa bhedā paraṁ maraṇā apāyaṁ duggatiṁ vinipātaṁ nirayaṁ upapajjati.
- **EN**: Because of undertaking such deeds, when their body breaks up, after death, they’re reborn in a place of loss, a bad place, the underworld, hell.

## mn135:5.3
- **PLI**: No ce kāyassa bhedā paraṁ maraṇā apāyaṁ duggatiṁ vinipātaṁ nirayaṁ upapajjati, sace manussattaṁ āgacchati yattha yattha paccājāyati appāyuko hoti.
- **EN**: If they’re not reborn in a place of loss, but return to the human realm, then wherever they’re reborn they’re short-lived.
- **NOTE**: Rebirth in the human realm is always due to good deeds in the past, but while in this realm, bad kamma can take effect. Of course, not all afflictions are caused by past kamma, a doctrine of the Jains that the Buddha rejected.

## mn135:5.4
- **PLI**: Appāyukasaṁvattanikā esā, māṇava, paṭipadā yadidaṁ—
- **EN**: For killing living creatures is the path leading to a short lifespan.
