# 4. Going Up  (4 đoạn)

## mn8:15.0
- **PLI**: 4. Uparibhāgapariyāya
- **EN**: 4. Going Up

## mn8:15.1  `cck12.75, ms9M_251, msdiv86, nya15, pts-vp-pli1.45`
- **PLI**: Seyyathāpi, cunda, ye keci akusalā dhammā sabbe te adhobhāgaṅgamanīyā, ye keci kusalā dhammā sabbe te uparibhāgaṅgamanīyā;
- **EN**: Cunda, all unskillful qualities lead downwards, while all skillful qualities lead upwards.
- **NOTE**: This round emphasizes the unifying quality of good and bad, so that any good deed, no matter how small or insignificant, leads in the same direction.

## mn8:15.2
- **PLI**: evameva kho, cunda, vihiṁsakassa purisapuggalassa avihiṁsā hoti uparibhāgāya, pāṇātipātissa purisapuggalassa pāṇātipātā veramaṇī hoti uparibhāgāya …pe…
- **EN**: In the same way, a cruel individual is led upwards by not being cruel. An individual who kills is led upwards by not killing …

## mn8:15.3
- **PLI**: sandiṭṭhiparāmāsiādhānaggāhiduppaṭinissaggissa purisapuggalassa asandiṭṭhiparāmāsianādhānaggāhisuppaṭinissaggitā hoti uparibhāgāya.
- **EN**: An individual who is attached to their own views, holding them tight, and refusing to let go, is led upwards by not being attached to their own views, not holding them tight, but letting them go easily.
