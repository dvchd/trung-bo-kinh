# 3. Bypassing  (6 đoạn)

## mn8:14.0
- **PLI**: 3. Parikkamanapariyāya
- **EN**: 3. Bypassing

## mn8:14.1  `cck12.73, dr12.69, ms9M_248, msdiv85, ndp9.59, nya14, pts-vp-pli1.44, sya12.79`
- **PLI**: Seyyathāpi, cunda, visamo maggo assa, tassa añño samo maggo parikkamanāya;
- **EN**: Cunda, suppose there was a rough path and another smooth path to get around it.
- **NOTE**: When the path is rough, the Buddha encourages us to find a smoother path. The undertakings of the Buddhist path should be pleasant and lead to happiness. While the path can be difficult, the difficulty itself is of no value. This is in implicit contrast with the Jains, who gave themselves to “burning off” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>tapas</i>) kamma through agonizing austerities.
