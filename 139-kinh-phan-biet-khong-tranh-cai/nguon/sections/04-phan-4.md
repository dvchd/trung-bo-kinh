## mn139:14.1  `dr13.394, ms11M_1004, ndp11.321, nya14`
- **PLI**: Tasmātiha, bhikkhave, ‘saraṇañca dhammaṁ jānissāma, araṇañca dhammaṁ jānissāma;
- **EN**: So you should train like this: ‘We shall know the principles beset by strife and the principles free of strife.

## mn139:14.2
- **PLI**: saraṇañca dhammaṁ ñatvā araṇañca dhammaṁ ñatvā araṇapaṭipadaṁ paṭipajjissāmā’ti evañhi vo, bhikkhave, sikkhitabbaṁ.
- **EN**: Knowing this, we will practice the way free of strife.’

## mn139:14.3
- **PLI**: Subhūti ca pana, bhikkhave, kulaputto araṇapaṭipadaṁ paṭipanno”ti.
- **EN**: And, mendicants, the gentleman Subhūti practices the way free of strife.”
- **NOTE**: This was apparently prompted by Subhūti’s support of his fellow monk Saddha (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/an11.14/en/sujato'>AN 11.14</a>), for which he was declared the foremost in living without strife (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/an1.201/en/sujato#1.1'>AN 1.201:1.1</a>; he was additionally praised as worthy of donations (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/an1.202/en/sujato#1.1'>AN 1.202:1.1</a>). Subhūti’s meditation is praised at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/ud6.7/en/sujato'>Ud 6.7</a>, and his verse opens the Theragāthā at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/thag1.1/en/sujato'>Thag 1.1</a>.
