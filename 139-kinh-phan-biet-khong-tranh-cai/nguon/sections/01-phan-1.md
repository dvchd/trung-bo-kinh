## mn139:3.1  `ms11M_975, nya3`
- **PLI**: “Na kāmasukhamanuyuñjeyya hīnaṁ gammaṁ pothujjanikaṁ anariyaṁ anatthasaṁhitaṁ, na ca attakilamathānuyogamanuyuñjeyya dukkhaṁ anariyaṁ anatthasaṁhitaṁ.
- **EN**: “Don’t indulge in sensual pleasure, which is low, crude, ordinary, ignoble, and pointless. And don’t indulge in self-mortification, which is painful, ignoble, and pointless.
- **NOTE**: This is adapted from the first sermon; text has <i lang='pi' translate='no'>kāmasukhamanuyuñjeyya</i> which is a briefer expression that the original <i lang='pi' translate='no'>kāmesu kāmasukhallikānuyogo</i> at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn56.11/en/sujato#2.3'>SN 56.11:2.3</a>.

## mn139:3.2
- **PLI**: Ete kho, bhikkhave, ubho ante anupagamma majjhimā paṭipadā tathāgatena abhisambuddhā, cakkhukaraṇī ñāṇakaraṇī upasamāya abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṁvattati.
- **EN**: Avoiding these two extremes, the Realized One understood the middle way of practice, which gives vision and knowledge, and leads to peace, direct knowledge, awakening, and extinguishment.

## mn139:3.3
- **PLI**: Ussādanañca jaññā, apasādanañca jaññā;
- **EN**: Know what it means to flatter and to rebuke.

## mn139:3.4
- **PLI**: ussādanañca ñatvā apasādanañca ñatvā nevussādeyya, na apasādeyya, dhammameva deseyya.
- **EN**: Knowing these, avoid them, and just teach Dhamma.

## mn139:3.5
- **PLI**: Sukhavinicchayaṁ jaññā;
- **EN**: Know how to evaluate different kinds of pleasure.

## mn139:3.6
- **PLI**: sukhavinicchayaṁ ñatvā ajjhattaṁ sukhamanuyuñjeyya.
- **EN**: Knowing this, pursue inner pleasure.

## mn139:3.7
- **PLI**: Rahovādaṁ na bhāseyya, sammukhā na khīṇaṁ bhaṇe.
- **EN**: Don’t talk behind people’s backs, and don’t speak sharply in their presence.

## mn139:3.8
- **PLI**: Ataramānova bhāseyya, no taramāno.
- **EN**: Don’t speak hurriedly.

## mn139:3.9
- **PLI**: Janapadaniruttiṁ nābhiniveseyya, samaññaṁ nātidhāveyyāti—
- **EN**: Don’t insist on popular definitions and don’t overstep normal labels.
- **NOTE**: “Popular definitions” is <i lang='pi' translate='no'>janapadanirutti</i>. | <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Samaññā</i> (“label”, Sanskrit <i lang='pi' translate='no'>samājñā</i>) ought not be confused with <i lang='pi' translate='no'>sāmañña</i> (“agreement”, “equality”, Sanskrit <i lang='pi' translate='no'>sāmānya</i>).

## mn139:3.10
- **PLI**: ayamuddeso araṇavibhaṅgassa.
- **EN**: This is the summary recital for the analysis of no strife.

- **EN**: Indulging in self-mortification is painful, ignoble, and pointless. It is a principle beset by pain, harm, stress, and fever, and it is the wrong way.
