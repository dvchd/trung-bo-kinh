## mn61:17.5
- **PLI**: ‘yaṁ kho ahaṁ idaṁ manasā kammaṁ akāsiṁ idaṁ me manokammaṁ attabyābādhāyapi saṁvattati, parabyābādhāyapi saṁvattati, ubhayabyābādhāyapi saṁvattati—
- **EN**: ‘This act of mind that I have done leads to hurting myself, hurting others, or hurting both.

## mn61:17.6
- **PLI**: akusalaṁ idaṁ manokammaṁ dukkhudrayaṁ dukkhavipākan’ti, evarūpaṁ pana te, rāhula, manokammaṁ aṭṭīyitabbaṁ harāyitabbaṁ jigucchitabbaṁ;
- **EN**: It’s unskillful, with suffering as its outcome and result.’ Then, Rāhula, you should be horrified, repelled, and disgusted by that deed.
- **NOTE**: Acts of mind are not covered under the Vinaya and so need not be confessed, unlike those of body (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn61/en/sujato#11.6'>MN 61:11.6</a>) and speech (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn61/en/sujato#14.6'>MN 61:14.6</a>). They are matters of personal integrity rather than social reinforcement.

- **EN**: And being repelled, you should restrain yourself in future.

- **EN**: ‘This act with the mind that I have done doesn’t lead to hurting myself, hurting others, or hurting both.

## mn61:17.10
- **PLI**: kusalaṁ idaṁ manokammaṁ sukhudrayaṁ sukhavipākan’ti, teneva tvaṁ, rāhula, pītipāmojjena vihareyyāsi ahorattānusikkhī kusalesu dhammesu.
- **EN**: It’s skillful, with happiness as its outcome and result.’ Then, Rāhula, you should live in rapture and joy because of this, training day and night in skillful qualities.
