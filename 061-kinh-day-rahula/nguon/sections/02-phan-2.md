## mn61:10.2
- **PLI**: ‘yannu kho ahaṁ idaṁ kāyena kammaṁ karomi idaṁ me kāyakammaṁ attabyābādhāyapi saṁvattati, parabyābādhāyapi saṁvattati, ubhayabyābādhāyapi saṁvattati—
- **EN**: ‘Does this act with the body that I am doing lead to hurting myself, hurting others, or hurting both?

- **EN**: It’s skillful, with happiness as its outcome and result.’ Then, Rāhula, you should continue doing such a deed.
