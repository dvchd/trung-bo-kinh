# Phần 3  (50 đoạn)

## mn21:9.36
- **PLI**: ‘Kiṁ, ayye’ti?
- **EN**: ‘What is it, ma’am?’

## mn21:9.37
- **PLI**: ‘Kiṁ, je, divā uṭṭhāsī’ti?
- **EN**: ‘You’re getting up even later in the day—what’s up with you, wench?’

## mn21:9.38
- **PLI**: ‘Na khvayye, kiñcī’ti.
- **EN**: ‘Nothing, ma’am.’

## mn21:9.39
- **PLI**: ‘No vata re kiñci, pāpi dāsi, divā uṭṭhāsī’ti kupitā anattamanā aggaḷasūciṁ gahetvā sīse pahāraṁ adāsi, sīsaṁ vobhindi.
- **EN**: ‘Oh, so nothing’s up, you naughty maid, but you get up even later in the day!’ Angry and upset, she grabbed a door-pin and hit Kāḷī on the head, cracking it open.
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Aggaḷasūci</i> is a “pin” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>sūci</i>) for fastening the “door” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>aggala</i>). It is sometimes translated as “rolling pin”, but that would be Sanskrit <i lang='pi' translate='no'>vellana</i> (Hindi <i lang='pi' translate='no'>belan</i>).

## mn21:9.40
- **PLI**: Atha kho, bhikkhave, kāḷī dāsī bhinnena sīsena lohitena galantena paṭivissakānaṁ ujjhāpesi:
- **EN**: Then Kāḷī, with blood pouring from her cracked skull, denounced her mistress to the neighbors,

## mn21:9.41
- **PLI**: ‘passathayye, soratāya kammaṁ;
- **EN**: ‘See, ladies, what the gentle one did!

## mn21:9.42
- **PLI**: passathayye, nivātāya kammaṁ, passathayye, upasantāya kammaṁ.
- **EN**: See what the placid one did! See what the calm one did!

## mn21:9.43
- **PLI**: Kathañhi nāma ekadāsikāya divā uṭṭhāsīti kupitā anattamanā aggaḷasūciṁ gahetvā sīse pahāraṁ dassati, sīsaṁ vobhindissatī’ti.
- **EN**: How on earth can she grab a door-pin and hit her only maid on the head, cracking it open, just for getting up late?’
- **NOTE**: Arthaśāstra 3.13.9 says that inflicting punishment (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>daṇḍapreṣaṇam</i>; cf. Pali <i lang='pi' translate='no'>daṇḍāpesuṁ</i> at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-bi-vb-ss1/en/sujato#1.38'>Bi Ss 1:1.38</a>) on a slave is a crime for which a master incurs a fine equivalent to the cost of the slave.

## mn21:9.44  `ms9M_761, ndp9.169`
- **PLI**: Atha kho, bhikkhave, vedehikāya gahapatāniyā aparena samayena evaṁ pāpako kittisaddo abbhuggacchi:
- **EN**: Then after some time the housewife Vedehikā got this bad reputation:

## mn21:9.45
- **PLI**: ‘caṇḍī vedehikā gahapatānī, anivātā vedehikā gahapatānī, anupasantā vedehikā gahapatānī’ti.
- **EN**: ‘The housewife Vedehikā is fierce, ill-tempered, and not calm at all.’
- **NOTE**: No blame is given to Kāḷī for her deliberate provocation.

## mn21:10.1  `cck12.240, csp1ed9.178, csp2ed9.178, dr12.229, ms9M_762, nya10, sya12.255, vri12.179`
- **PLI**: Evameva kho, bhikkhave, idhekacco bhikkhu tāvadeva soratasorato hoti nivātanivāto hoti upasantūpasanto hoti yāva na amanāpā vacanapathā phusanti.
- **EN**: In the same way, a mendicant may be the gentlest of the gentle, the most placid of the placid, the calmest of the calm, so long as they don’t encounter any disagreeable criticism.

## mn21:10.2
- **PLI**: Yato ca, bhikkhave, bhikkhuṁ amanāpā vacanapathā phusanti, atha bhikkhu ‘sorato’ti veditabbo, ‘nivāto’ti veditabbo, ‘upasanto’ti veditabbo.
- **EN**: But it’s when they encounter disagreeable criticism that you’ll know whether they’re really gentle, placid, and calm.

## mn21:10.3
- **PLI**: Nāhaṁ taṁ, bhikkhave, bhikkhuṁ ‘suvaco’ti vadāmi yo cīvarapiṇḍapātasenāsanagilānappaccayabhesajjaparikkhārahetu suvaco hoti, sovacassataṁ āpajjati.
- **EN**: I don’t say that a mendicant is easy to admonish if they make themselves easy to admonish only for the sake of robes, almsfood, lodgings, and medicines and supplies for the sick.

## mn21:10.4
- **PLI**: Taṁ kissa hetu?
- **EN**: Why is that?

## mn21:10.5
- **PLI**: Tañhi so, bhikkhave, bhikkhu cīvarapiṇḍapātasenāsanagilānappaccayabhesajjaparikkhāraṁ alabhamāno na suvaco hoti, na sovacassataṁ āpajjati.
- **EN**: Because when they don’t get robes, almsfood, lodgings, and medicines and supplies for the sick, they’re no longer easy to admonish.

## mn21:10.6
- **PLI**: Yo ca kho, bhikkhave, bhikkhu dhammaṁyeva sakkaronto, dhammaṁ garuṁ karonto, dhammaṁ mānento, dhammaṁ pūjento, dhammaṁ apacāyamāno suvaco hoti, sovacassataṁ āpajjati, tamahaṁ ‘suvaco’ti vadāmi.
- **EN**: But when a mendicant is easy to admonish purely because they honor, respect, revere, worship, and venerate the teaching, then I say that they’re easy to admonish.

## mn21:10.7
- **PLI**: Tasmātiha, bhikkhave, ‘dhammaṁyeva sakkarontā, dhammaṁ garuṁ karontā, dhammaṁ mānentā, dhammaṁ pūjentā, dhammaṁ apacāyamānā suvacā bhavissāma, sovacassataṁ āpajjissāmā’ti.
- **EN**: So, mendicants, you should train yourselves: ‘We will be easy to admonish purely because we honor, respect, revere, worship, and venerate the teaching.’

## mn21:10.8
- **PLI**: Evañhi vo, bhikkhave, sikkhitabbaṁ.
- **EN**: That’s how you should train.

## mn21:11.1  `bj10.318, cck12.241, ms9M_763, msdiv227, ndp9.170, nya11, pts-vp-pli1.127, sya12.256`

- **EN**: Their speech may be timely or untimely,

## mn21:11.3
- **PLI**: bhūtena vā abhūtena vā;
- **EN**: true or false,

## mn21:11.4
- **PLI**: saṇhena vā pharusena vā;
- **EN**: gentle or harsh,

## mn21:11.5

- **EN**: beneficial or harmful,

## mn21:11.9

## mn21:11.12
- **PLI**: Tatrāpi vo, bhikkhave, evaṁ sikkhitabbaṁ:
- **EN**: If that happens, you should train like this:

## mn21:11.13
- **PLI**: ‘na ceva no cittaṁ vipariṇataṁ bhavissati, na ca pāpikaṁ vācaṁ nicchāressāma, hitānukampī ca viharissāma mettacittā, na dosantarā.
- **EN**: ‘Our minds will not degenerate. We will blurt out no bad words. We will remain full of sympathy, with a heart of love and no secret hate.

## mn21:11.14
- **PLI**: Tañca puggalaṁ mettāsahagatena cetasā pharitvā viharissāma, tadārammaṇañca sabbāvantaṁ lokaṁ mettāsahagatena cittena vipulena mahaggatena appamāṇena averena abyābajjhena pharitvā viharissāmā’ti.
- **EN**: We will meditate spreading a heart of love to that individual. And with them as a basis, we will meditate spreading a heart full of love to everyone in the world—abundant, expansive, limitless, free of enmity and ill will.’
- **NOTE**: Up until now, the sutta has concerned itself with good behavior and motivations in everyday life. Now this forms a basis to support meditation.

## mn21:11.15
- **PLI**: Evañhi vo, bhikkhave, sikkhitabbaṁ.
- **EN**: That’s how you should train.
