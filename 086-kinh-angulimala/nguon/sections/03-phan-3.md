## mn86:15.1  `bj11.524, ms10M_1013, nya15, vri13.313`
- **PLI**: “Tena hi tvaṁ, aṅgulimāla, yena sā itthī tenupasaṅkama; upasaṅkamitvā taṁ itthiṁ evaṁ vadehi:
- **EN**: “Well then, Aṅgulimāla, go to that woman and say this:

## mn86:15.2
- **PLI**: ‘yatohaṁ, bhagini, jāto nābhijānāmi sañcicca pāṇaṁ jīvitā voropetā, tena saccena sotthi te hotu, sotthi gabbhassā’”ti.
- **EN**: ‘Ever since I was born, sister, I don’t recall having intentionally taken the life of a living creature. By this truth, may both you and your baby be safe.’”
- **NOTE**: The asseveration of truth—the belief that “by this truth” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>tena saccena</i>, also at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/snp2.1/en/sujato'>Snp 2.1</a>) effective results could be won—can be traced to Rig Veda 1.21.6 (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>tena satyena</i>).

- **EN**: For I have intentionally killed many living creatures.”

## mn86:15.5
- **PLI**: “Tena hi tvaṁ, aṅgulimāla, yena sā itthī tenupasaṅkama; upasaṅkamitvā taṁ itthiṁ evaṁ vadehi:
- **EN**: “In that case, Aṅgulimāla, go to that woman and say this:

## mn86:15.6
- **PLI**: ‘yatohaṁ, bhagini, ariyāya jātiyā jāto, nābhijānāmi sañcicca pāṇaṁ jīvitā voropetā, tena saccena sotthi te hotu, sotthi gabbhassā’”ti.
- **EN**: ‘Ever since I was born in the noble birth, sister, I don’t recall having intentionally taken the life of a living creature. By this truth, may both you and your baby be safe.’”
- **NOTE**: This is still used as a blessing for pregnant women in Theravada. | “Noble birth” is a unique way of referring to ordination. It echoes the <i lang='pi' translate='no'>upanayana</i> initiation, where the teacher is said to become pregnant with the student and give birth to them as a brahmin (Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa 11.5.4; also see notes to <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn26/en/sujato#15.2'>MN 26:15.2</a>ff.).
