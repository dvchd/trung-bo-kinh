# Phần 1  (50 đoạn)

## mn92:1.1  `bj11.598, cck13.531, dr13.500, ms10M_1191, msdiv396, ndp10.396, nya1, sya13.548, vri13.355`
- **PLI**: Evaṁ me sutaṁ—
- **EN**: So I have heard.
- **NOTE**: This entire sutta is repeated at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/snp3.7/en/sujato'>Snp 3.7</a>.

## mn92:1.2
- **PLI**: ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā aṅguttarāpesu cārikaṁ caramāno mahatā bhikkhusaṅghena saddhiṁ aḍḍhateḷasehi bhikkhusatehi yena āpaṇaṁ nāma aṅguttarāpānaṁ nigamo tadavasari.
- **EN**: At one time the Buddha was wandering together with a large Saṅgha of 1,250 mendicants in the land of the Aṅguttarāpans when he arrived at a town of theirs named Āpaṇa.

## mn92:2.1  `nya2`
- **PLI**: Assosi kho keṇiyo jaṭilo:
- **EN**: The matted-hair ascetic Keṇiya heard:

## mn92:2.2
- **PLI**: “samaṇo khalu, bho, gotamo sakyaputto sakyakulā pabbajito aṅguttarāpesu cārikaṁ caramāno mahatā bhikkhusaṅghena saddhiṁ aḍḍhateḷasehi bhikkhusatehi āpaṇaṁ anuppatto.
- **EN**: “It seems the ascetic Gotama—a Sakyan, gone forth from a Sakyan family—has arrived at Āpaṇa, together with a large Saṅgha of 1,250 mendicants.

## mn92:2.3
- **PLI**: Taṁ kho pana bhavantaṁ gotamaṁ evaṁ kalyāṇo kittisaddo abbhuggato:
- **EN**: He has this good reputation:

## mn92:2.4
- **PLI**: ‘itipi so bhagavā arahaṁ sammāsambuddho vijjācaraṇasampanno sugato lokavidū anuttaro purisadammasārathi satthā devamanussānaṁ buddho bhagavā’ti.
- **EN**: ‘That Blessed One is perfected, a fully awakened Buddha, accomplished in knowledge and conduct, holy, knower of the world, supreme guide for those fit for training, teacher of gods and humans, awakened, blessed.’

## mn92:2.5
- **PLI**: So imaṁ lokaṁ sadevakaṁ samārakaṁ sabrahmakaṁ sassamaṇabrāhmaṇiṁ pajaṁ sadevamanussaṁ sayaṁ abhiññā sacchikatvā pavedeti.
- **EN**: He has realized with his own insight this world—with its gods, Māras, and divinities, this population with its ascetics and brahmins, gods and humans—and he makes it known to others.

## mn92:2.6
- **PLI**: So dhammaṁ deseti ādikalyāṇaṁ majjhekalyāṇaṁ pariyosānakalyāṇaṁ sātthaṁ sabyañjanaṁ, kevalaparipuṇṇaṁ parisuddhaṁ brahmacariyaṁ pakāseti.
- **EN**: He proclaims a teaching that’s good in the beginning, good in the middle, and good in the end, meaningful and well-phrased. And he reveals a spiritual practice that’s entirely full and pure.

## mn92:2.7
- **PLI**: Sādhu kho pana tathārūpānaṁ arahataṁ dassanaṁ hotī”ti.
- **EN**: It’s good to see such perfected ones.”

## mn92:3.1  `cck13.532, csp1ed10.348, csp2ed10.348, ms10M_1192, nya3, sya13.549`
- **PLI**: Atha kho keṇiyo jaṭilo yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavatā saddhiṁ sammodi.
- **EN**: So Keṇiya approached the Buddha and exchanged greetings with him.
- **NOTE**: Keṇiya features also in the Vinaya, where at this point he offered the Buddha and the Saṅgha a drink in the evening, reasoning that the Brahmanical sages of the past did likewise (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-kd6/en/sujato#35.1.2'>Kd 6:35.1.2</a>). As a result, the Buddha allowed fruit juice and other drinks in the evening.

## mn92:3.2
- **PLI**: Sammodanīyaṁ kathaṁ sāraṇīyaṁ vītisāretvā ekamantaṁ nisīdi.
- **EN**: When the greetings and polite conversation were over, he sat down to one side.

## mn92:3.3
- **PLI**: Ekamantaṁ nisinnaṁ kho keṇiyaṁ jaṭilaṁ bhagavā dhammiyā kathāya sandassesi samādapesi samuttejesi sampahaṁsesi.
- **EN**: The Buddha educated, encouraged, fired up, and inspired him with a Dhamma talk.

## mn92:3.4
- **PLI**: Atha kho keṇiyo jaṭilo bhagavatā dhammiyā kathāya sandassito samādapito samuttejito sampahaṁsito bhagavantaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: Then he said to the Buddha,

## mn92:3.5
- **PLI**: “adhivāsetu me bhavaṁ gotamo svātanāya bhattaṁ saddhiṁ bhikkhusaṅghenā”ti.
- **EN**: “Would Worthy Gotama together with the mendicant Saṅgha please accept tomorrow’s meal from me?”

## mn92:3.6
- **PLI**: Evaṁ vutte, bhagavā keṇiyaṁ jaṭilaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: When he said this, the Buddha said to him,

## mn92:3.7
- **PLI**: “mahā kho, keṇiya, bhikkhusaṅgho aḍḍhateḷasāni bhikkhusatāni, tvañca brāhmaṇesu abhippasanno”ti.
- **EN**: “The Saṅgha is large, Keṇiya; there are 1,250 mendicants. And you are devoted to the brahmins.”

## mn92:3.8  `dr13.501, ms10M_1193, ndp10.397`

## mn92:3.9

## mn92:3.10

- **EN**: Would Worthy Gotama together with the mendicant Saṅgha please accept tomorrow’s meal from me?”

## mn92:3.11
- **PLI**: Dutiyampi kho bhagavā keṇiyaṁ jaṭilaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: And for a second time, the Buddha gave the same reply.

## mn92:3.12
- **PLI**: “mahā kho, keṇiya, bhikkhusaṅgho aḍḍhateḷasāni bhikkhusatāni, tvañca brāhmaṇesu abhippasanno”ti.
- **EN**: “The Saṅgha is large, Keṇiya; there are 1,250 mendicants. And you are devoted to the brahmins.”  (= mn92:3.7)

## mn92:3.13  `ms10M_1194, vri13.356`

## mn92:3.14

## mn92:4.1  `bj11.600, cck13.533, ms10M_1195, nya4, sya13.550`
- **PLI**: Atha kho keṇiyo jaṭilo bhagavato adhivāsanaṁ viditvā uṭṭhāyāsanā yena sako assamo tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā mittāmacce ñātisālohite āmantesi:
- **EN**: Then, knowing that the Buddha had consented, Keṇiya rose from his seat and went to his own hermitage. There he addressed his friends and colleagues, relatives and kin,

## mn92:4.2
- **PLI**: “suṇantu me bhonto, mittāmaccā ñātisālohitā;
- **EN**: “My friends and colleagues, relatives and kin: please listen!

## mn92:4.3
- **PLI**: samaṇo me gotamo nimantito svātanāya bhattaṁ saddhiṁ bhikkhusaṅghena.
- **EN**: The ascetic Gotama together with the mendicant Saṅgha has been invited by me for tomorrow’s meal.

## mn92:4.4
- **PLI**: Yena me kāyaveyyāvaṭikaṁ kareyyāthā”ti.
- **EN**: Please help me out with the manual preparations.”
- **NOTE**: “Manual preparations” is <i lang='pi' translate='no'>kāyaveyyāvaṭikaṁ</i>. Ten kinds of service are enumerated in the Jain Tattvārthasūtra 9.24.

## mn92:4.5
- **PLI**: “Evaṁ, bho”ti kho keṇiyassa jaṭilassa mittāmaccā ñātisālohitā keṇiyassa jaṭilassa paṭissutvā appekacce uddhanāni khaṇanti, appekacce kaṭṭhāni phālenti, appekacce bhājanāni dhovanti, appekacce udakamaṇikaṁ patiṭṭhāpenti, appekacce āsanāni paññapenti.
- **EN**: “Yes, worthy sir,” they replied. Some dug ovens, some chopped wood, some washed dishes, some set up a water jar, and some spread out seats.

## mn92:4.6
- **PLI**: Keṇiyo pana jaṭilo sāmaṁyeva maṇḍalamālaṁ paṭiyādeti.
- **EN**: Meanwhile, Keṇiya set up the pavilion himself.

## mn92:5.1  `csp1ed10.349, csp2ed10.349, ms10M_1196, msdiv397, nya5`
- **PLI**: Tena kho pana samayena selo brāhmaṇo āpaṇe paṭivasati tiṇṇaṁ vedānaṁ pāragū sanighaṇḍukeṭubhānaṁ sākkharappabhedānaṁ itihāsapañcamānaṁ, padako, veyyākaraṇo, lokāyatamahāpurisalakkhaṇesu anavayo, tīṇi ca māṇavakasatāni mante vāceti.
- **EN**: Now at that time the brahmin Sela was residing in Āpaṇa. He had mastered the three Vedas, together with their vocabularies and ritual performance, their phonology and word classification, and the testaments as fifth. He knew them word-by-word, and their grammar. He was well versed in cosmology and the marks of a great man. And he was teaching three hundred young students to recite the hymns.
