## mn134:6.3
- **PLI**: “Candano nāma so, bhikkhu, devaputto.
- **EN**: “That godling was named Candana.

## mn134:6.4
- **PLI**: Candano, bhikkhu, devaputto aṭṭhiṁ katvā manasikatvā sabbacetasā samannāharitvā ohitasoto dhammaṁ suṇāti.
- **EN**: Candana pays attention, applies the mind, concentrates wholeheartedly, and actively listens to the teaching.
- **NOTE**: Candana is here praised for his skill in learning, yet earlier he says that although he was present when the Buddha taught the “recitation passage and analysis”, he only remembered the verses (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn134/en/sujato#2.12'>MN 134:2.12</a>). As at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn133/en/sujato#6.2'>MN 133:6.2</a>, the parallels are more coherent, as they only mention him hearing the verses.

- **EN**: Well then, mendicant, listen and apply your mind well, I will speak.”
