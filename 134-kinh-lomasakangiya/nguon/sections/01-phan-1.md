- **EN**: At one time the Buddha was staying near Sāvatthī in Jeta’s Grove, Anāthapiṇḍika’s monastery.

## mn134:1.3
- **PLI**: Tena kho pana samayena āyasmā lomasakaṅgiyo sakkesu viharati kapilavatthusmiṁ nigrodhārāme.
- **EN**: Now at that time Venerable Lomasakaṅgiya was staying in the Sakyan country at Kapilavatthu in the Banyan Tree Monastery.
- **NOTE**: Lomasakaṅgiya’s verse speaks of his determination to forge his way through the dense jungle in search of seclusion (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/thag1.27/en/sujato'>Thag 1.27</a>). He is probably also the same monk who appears in <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn54.12/en/sujato'>SN 54.12</a>. His name means “hairy-limbs”.

## mn134:2.1  `nya2`
- **PLI**: Atha kho candano devaputto abhikkantāya rattiyā abhikkantavaṇṇo kevalakappaṁ nigrodhārāmaṁ obhāsetvā yenāyasmā lomasakaṅgiyo tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā ekamantaṁ aṭṭhāsi. Ekamantaṁ ṭhito kho candano devaputto āyasmantaṁ lomasakaṅgiyaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: Then, late at night, the glorious godling Candana, lighting up the entire Banyan Tree Monastery, went up to the Venerable Lomasakaṅgiya, and stood to one side. Standing to one side, he said to Lomasakaṅgiya:
