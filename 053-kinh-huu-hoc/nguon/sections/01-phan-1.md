- **EN**: Now at that time a new town hall had recently been constructed for the Sakyans of Kapilavatthu. It had not yet been occupied by an ascetic or brahmin or any human at all.
- **NOTE**: The completion of a town hall was celebrated by a talk for the Sakyans at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn35.243/en/sujato#1.2'>SN 35.243:1.2</a> and the Mallas at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn33/en/sujato#1.2.1'>DN 33:1.2.1</a>. Such halls were community meeting places that played a central role in civic society and communal decision-making in democratic republics such as the Mallas and the Sakyans. The Buddha’s participation is a sign of his support for their civic and democratic process.
