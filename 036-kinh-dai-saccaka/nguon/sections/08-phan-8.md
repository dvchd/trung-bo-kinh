# Phần 8  (8 đoạn)

## mn36:48.11
- **PLI**: nigaṇṭhaṁ nāṭaputtaṁ vādena vādaṁ samārabhitā.
- **EN**: and the Jain ascetic of the Ñātika clan in debate.
- **NOTE**: This shows that despite his Jain parentage, and despite his citation of Ājīvakas above, Saccaka was no adherent of these philosophies. It seems that he was a contrarian at heart.

## mn36:48.12
- **PLI**: Sopi mayā vādena vādaṁ samāraddho aññenaññaṁ paṭicari, bahiddhā kathaṁ apanāmesi, kopañca dosañca appaccayañca pātvākāsi.
- **EN**: They all dodged the issue, distracting the discussion with irrelevant points, and displaying annoyance, hate, and bitterness.

## mn36:48.13
- **PLI**: Bhoto pana gotamassa evaṁ āsajja āsajja vuccamānassa, upanītehi vacanappathehi samudācariyamānassa, chavivaṇṇo ceva pariyodāyati, mukhavaṇṇo ca vippasīdati, yathā taṁ arahato sammāsambuddhassa.
- **EN**: But when the worthy Gotama is repeatedly attacked with inappropriate and intrusive criticism, the complexion of his skin brightens and the color of his face becomes clear, just like a perfected one, a fully awakened Buddha.

## mn36:48.14
- **PLI**: Handa ca dāni mayaṁ, bho gotama, gacchāma.
- **EN**: Well, now, worthy Gotama, I must go.

## mn36:48.15
- **PLI**: Bahukiccā mayaṁ, bahukaraṇīyā”ti.
- **EN**: I have many duties, and much to do.”

## mn36:48.16
- **PLI**: “Yassadāni tvaṁ, aggivessana, kālaṁ maññasī”ti.
- **EN**: “Please, Aggivessana, go at your convenience.”

## mn36:48.17  `ms9M_1267`
- **PLI**: Atha kho saccako nigaṇṭhaputto bhagavato bhāsitaṁ abhinanditvā anumoditvā uṭṭhāyāsanā pakkāmīti.
- **EN**: Then Saccaka, the son of Jain parents, having approved and agreed with what the Buddha said, rose from his seat and left.

## mn36:48.18
- **PLI**: Mahāsaccakasuttaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ chaṭṭhaṁ.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)
