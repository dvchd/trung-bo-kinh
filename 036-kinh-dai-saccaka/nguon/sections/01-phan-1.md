- **NOTE**: “Development of physical endurance” is <i lang='pi' translate='no'>kāyabhāvanā</i>, which is not a normal Buddhist term. The literal meaning is “development of the body”, but I translate in accord with the sense.

- **NOTE**: Described as prominent sages of the Ājīvakas at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn76/en/sujato#53.6'>MN 76:53.6</a> and <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an6.57/en/sujato#7.1'>AN 6.57:7.1</a>.
