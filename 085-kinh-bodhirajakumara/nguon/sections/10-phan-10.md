- **PLI**: “Evameva kho, rājakumāra, pañcimāni padhāniyaṅgāni.
- **EN**: “In the same way, prince, there are these five factors that support meditation.
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Padhāna</i> is literally to “fix” or “place” and hence to “try” or “strive”. Its core application is to apply oneself to spiritual practices, and hence in Buddhism it overlaps in meaning with “meditation”; a <i lang='pi' translate='no'>padhānasāla</i> is a “meditation hall”. Here the five factors are qualities that are essential foundations for meditation.

## mn85:58.2
- **PLI**: Katamāni pañca?
- **EN**: What five?

## mn85:58.3
- **PLI**: Idha, rājakumāra, bhikkhu saddho hoti; saddahati tathāgatassa bodhiṁ:
- **EN**: It’s when a noble disciple has faith in the Realized One’s awakening:

## mn85:58.4
- **PLI**: ‘itipi so bhagavā arahaṁ sammāsambuddho vijjācaraṇasampanno sugato lokavidū anuttaro purisadammasārathi satthā devamanussānaṁ buddho bhagavā’ti;
- **EN**: ‘That Blessed One is perfected, a fully awakened Buddha, accomplished in knowledge and conduct, holy, knower of the world, supreme guide for those fit for training, teacher of gods and humans, awakened, blessed.’

## mn85:58.5
- **PLI**: appābādho hoti appātaṅko samavepākiniyā gahaṇiyā samannāgato nātisītāya nāccuṇhāya majjhimāya padhānakkhamāya;
- **EN**: They are rarely ill or unwell. Their stomach digests well, being neither too hot nor too cold, but just right, and fit for meditation.

## mn85:58.6

- **EN**: They’re not devious or deceitful. They reveal themselves honestly to the Teacher or sensible spiritual companions.

## mn85:58.7

- **EN**: They live with energy roused up for giving up unskillful qualities and embracing skillful qualities. They’re strong, staunchly vigorous, not slacking off when it comes to developing skillful qualities.

## mn85:58.8

- **EN**: They’re wise. They have the wisdom of arising and passing away which is noble, penetrative, and leads to the complete ending of suffering.

- **EN**: These are the five factors that support meditation.
