# Phần 1  (50 đoạn)

## mn27:1.1  `bj10.426, cck12.320, dr12.300, ms9M_1008, msdiv288, ndp9.226, nya1, sya12.336, vri12.236`
- **PLI**: Evaṁ me sutaṁ—
- **EN**: So I have heard.
- **NOTE**: This sutta urges against drawing rash conclusions, insisting on a cautious and pragmatic approach to the truth. The theme of epistemological caution is further developed in such suttas as <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn47/en/sujato'>MN 47</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn60/en/sujato'>MN 60</a>, and <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn99/en/sujato'>MN 99</a>. | According to the Sinhalese chronicle Mahāvaṁsa 14.22, this was the first discourse taught by the arahant Mahinda to King Devanampiyatissa, leading to his conversion and the spread of Buddhism in Sri Lanka. This would have been around 250 BCE.

## mn27:1.2
- **PLI**: ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme.
- **EN**: At one time the Buddha was staying near Sāvatthī in Jeta’s Grove, Anāthapiṇḍika’s monastery.

## mn27:2.1  `nya2`
- **PLI**: Tena kho pana samayena jāṇussoṇi brāhmaṇo sabbasetena vaḷavābhirathena sāvatthiyā niyyāti divādivassa.
- **EN**: Now at that time the brahmin Jānussoṇi drove out from Sāvatthī in the middle of the day in an all-white chariot drawn by mares.
- **NOTE**: A chariot drawn by mares was the preferred vehicle of Jānussoṇi (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn99/en/sujato#30.1'>MN 99:30.1</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn45.4/en/sujato#1.3'>SN 45.4:1.3</a>), as well as the brahmin student Ambaṭṭha <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn3/en/sujato#1.6.1'>DN 3:1.6.1</a>. Chāndogya Upaniṣad 4.2.1 depicts Jānaśruti’s wealth as including cows, gold, and a chariot drawn by mules.

## mn27:2.2
- **PLI**: Addasā kho jāṇussoṇi brāhmaṇo pilotikaṁ paribbājakaṁ dūratova āgacchantaṁ.
- **EN**: He saw the wanderer Pilotika coming off in the distance,
- **NOTE**: Pilotika Vacchāyana is not mentioned elsewhere. His first name means “patch” and may be a reference to his patchwork robe (cp. “Ajita of the hair-blanket”). Vacchāyana is a patronymic, possibly indicating he was of the lineage of Vātsya, a student of Yājñavalkya (Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa 9.5.1.62, Brahmāṇḍa Purāṇa 2.35.29).

## mn27:2.3
- **PLI**: Disvāna pilotikaṁ paribbājakaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: and said to him,

## mn27:2.4  `ms9M_1009`
- **PLI**: “Handa kuto nu bhavaṁ vacchāyano āgacchati divādivassā”ti?
- **EN**: “So, worthy Vacchāyana, where are you coming from in the middle of the day?”

## mn27:2.5  `csp1ed9.233, csp2ed9.233, ms9M_1010`
- **PLI**: “Ito hi kho ahaṁ, bho, āgacchāmi samaṇassa gotamassa santikā”ti.
- **EN**: “Just now, worthy sir, I’ve come from the presence of the ascetic Gotama.”

## mn27:2.6  `ms9M_1011`
- **PLI**: “Taṁ kiṁ maññati bhavaṁ vacchāyano samaṇassa gotamassa paññāveyyattiyaṁ paṇḍito maññe”ti.
- **EN**: “What do you think of the ascetic Gotama’s lucidity of wisdom? Do you think he’s astute?”

## mn27:2.7  `ms9M_1012`
- **PLI**: “Ko cāhaṁ, bho, ko ca samaṇassa gotamassa paññāveyyattiyaṁ jānissāmi.
- **EN**: “Worthy sir, who am I to judge the ascetic Gotama’s lucidity of wisdom?
- **NOTE**: As at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn99/en/sujato#30.7'>MN 99:30.7</a> and <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an5.194/en/sujato#2.4'>AN 5.194:2.4</a>. The Buddha speaks of his own lucidity of wisdom at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn12/en/sujato#62.6'>MN 12:62.6</a>.

## mn27:2.8
- **PLI**: Sopi nūnassa tādisova yo samaṇassa gotamassa paññāveyyattiyaṁ jāneyyā”ti.
- **EN**: You’d really have to be on the same level to judge his lucidity of wisdom.”

## mn27:2.9  `ms9M_1013`
- **PLI**: “Uḷārāya khalu bhavaṁ vacchāyano samaṇaṁ gotamaṁ pasaṁsāya pasaṁsatī”ti.
- **EN**: “Worthy Vacchāyana praises the ascetic Gotama with high praise indeed.”

## mn27:2.10  `ms9M_1014`
- **PLI**: “Ko cāhaṁ, bho, ko ca samaṇaṁ gotamaṁ pasaṁsissāmi,
- **EN**: “Who am I to praise the ascetic Gotama?

## mn27:2.11
- **PLI**: pasatthapasatthova so bhavaṁ gotamo seṭṭho devamanussānan”ti.
- **EN**: He is praised by the praised as the first among gods and humans.”

## mn27:2.12  `ms9M_1015`
- **PLI**: “Kaṁ pana bhavaṁ vacchāyano atthavasaṁ sampassamāno samaṇe gotame evaṁ abhippasanno”ti?
- **EN**: “But for what reason are you so devoted to the ascetic Gotama?”

## mn27:3.1  `cck12.321, ms9M_1016, ndp9.227, nya3, pts-vp-pli1.176, sya12.337, vri12.237`
- **PLI**: “Seyyathāpi, bho, kusalo nāgavaniko nāgavanaṁ paviseyya.
- **EN**: “Suppose that a skilled bull elephant tracker were to enter a bull elephant wood.
- **NOTE**: The text differentiates “bull elephant” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>nāga</i>), “elephant” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>hatthi</i>), and “cow elephant” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>hatthinī</i>). This is essential to the parable, as the tracker is specifically seeking a bull.

## mn27:3.2
- **PLI**: So passeyya nāgavane mahantaṁ hatthipadaṁ, dīghato ca āyataṁ, tiriyañca vitthataṁ.
- **EN**: There he’d see a large elephant’s footprint, long and broad.

## mn27:3.3
- **PLI**: So niṭṭhaṁ gaccheyya:
- **EN**: He would come to the conclusion,
- **NOTE**: The Pali phrase <i lang='pi' translate='no'>niṭṭhaṁ gaccheyya</i> is an exact match for the English idiom “come to a conclusion”.

## mn27:3.4
- **PLI**: ‘mahā vata bho nāgo’ti.
- **EN**: ‘This must be a big bull elephant.’

## mn27:3.5
- **PLI**: Evameva kho ahaṁ, bho, yato addasaṁ samaṇe gotame cattāri padāni athāhaṁ niṭṭhamagamaṁ:
- **EN**: In the same way, because I saw four footprints of the ascetic Gotama I came to the conclusion,

## mn27:3.6
- **PLI**: ‘sammāsambuddho bhagavā, svākkhāto bhagavatā dhammo, suppaṭipanno bhagavato sāvakasaṅgho’ti.
- **EN**: ‘The Blessed One is a fully awakened Buddha. The teaching is well explained. The Saṅgha is practicing well.’

## mn27:4.1  `bj10.428, cck12.322, csp1ed9.234, csp2ed9.234, dr12.301, ms9M_1017, msdiv289, nya4, sya12.338`

## mn27:4.2

## mn27:4.12
- **PLI**: Te samaṇo gotamo dhammiyā kathāya sandasseti samādapeti samuttejeti sampahaṁseti.
- **EN**: The ascetic Gotama educates, encourages, fires up, and inspires them with a Dhamma talk.

## mn27:4.13
- **PLI**: Te samaṇena gotamena dhammiyā kathāya sandassitā samādapitā samuttejitā sampahaṁsitā na ceva samaṇaṁ gotamaṁ pañhaṁ pucchanti, kutossa vādaṁ āropessanti?

## mn27:4.15
