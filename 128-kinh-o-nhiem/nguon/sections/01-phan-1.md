# Phần 1  (50 đoạn)

## mn128:1.1  `bj12.344, cck14.275, ms11M_635, msdiv236, ndp11.221, nya1, pts-vp-pli3.153, sya14.295, vri14.192`
- **PLI**: Evaṁ me sutaṁ—
- **EN**: So I have heard.
- **NOTE**: This discourse offers a unique insight into the Buddha’s own meditative development. The “corruptions” of the title are the subtle mental flaws that undermine meditation when it is on the brink of absorption. | This discourse and <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn31/en/sujato'>MN 31</a> appear to be influenced by Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad 4.3, a famous dialogue where an unusually reluctant Yājñavalkya is repeatedly pressed by King Janaka to reveal the true nature of a person’s light.

## mn128:1.2
- **PLI**: ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā kosambiyaṁ viharati ghositārāme.
- **EN**: At one time the Buddha was staying near Kosambī in Ghosita’s monastery.

## mn128:2.1  `nya2`
- **PLI**: Tena kho pana samayena kosambiyaṁ bhikkhū bhaṇḍanajātā kalahajātā vivādāpannā aññamaññaṁ mukhasattīhi vitudantā viharanti.
- **EN**: Now at that time the mendicants of Kosambī were fighting, quarreling, and disputing, continually wounding each other with barbed words.
- **NOTE**: This is in reference to the notorious quarrel at Kosambī, also referred to at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn48/en/sujato#2.1'>MN 48:2.1</a>. The events are detailed in the Vinaya at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-kd10/en/sujato'>Kd 10</a>.

## mn128:3.1  `nya3`

- **EN**: Then a mendicant went up to the Buddha, bowed, stood to one side, and told him what was happening, adding:

## mn128:3.2

## mn128:3.4
- **PLI**: Adhivāsesi bhagavā tuṇhībhāvena.
