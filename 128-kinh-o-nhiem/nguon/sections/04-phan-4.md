## mn128:16.7
- **PLI**: ‘vicikicchā kho me udapādi, vicikicchādhikaraṇañca pana me samādhi cavi.
- **EN**: ‘Doubt arose in me, and because of that my immersion fell away.
- **NOTE**: When immersion is not consolidated, even the slightest uncertainty as to how to proceed can disturb the meditation. Doubt can also arise regarding whether the visions seen in meditation are real. This can be counteracted by determining one’s meditation at the beginning. | These three sentences use grammatical tense very precisely: an event is recalled (in past tense); a timeless principle is inferred (in present tense); and a resolution is made (in future tense).

## mn128:16.8
- **PLI**: Samādhimhi cute obhāso antaradhāyati dassanañca rūpānaṁ.
- **EN**: When immersion falls away, the light and vision of forms vanish.

## mn128:16.9
- **PLI**: Sohaṁ tathā karissāmi yathā me puna na vicikicchā uppajjissatī’ti.
- **EN**: I’ll make sure that doubt will not arise in me again.’
