# Phần 3  (15 đoạn)

## mn109:16.9
- **PLI**: “Yaṁ panāniccaṁ dukkhaṁ vipariṇāmadhammaṁ, kallaṁ nu taṁ samanupassituṁ:
- **EN**: “But if it’s impermanent, suffering, and perishable, is it fit to be regarded thus:

## mn109:16.10
- **PLI**: ‘etaṁ mama, esohamasmi, eso me attā’”ti?
- **EN**: ‘This is mine, I am this, this is my self’?”

## mn109:16.11
- **PLI**: “No hetaṁ, bhante”.
- **EN**: “No, sir.”

## mn109:16.12
- **PLI**: “Tasmātiha, bhikkhave, yaṁ kiñci rūpaṁ atītānāgatapaccuppannaṁ ajjhattaṁ vā bahiddhā vā oḷārikaṁ vā sukhumaṁ vā hīnaṁ vā paṇītaṁ vā yaṁ dūre santike vā sabbaṁ rūpaṁ: ‘netaṁ mama, nesohamasmi, na meso attā’ti evametaṁ yathābhūtaṁ sammappaññāya daṭṭhabbaṁ.
- **EN**: “So you should truly see any kind of form at all—past, future, or present; internal or external; solid or subtle; inferior or superior; far or near: <em>all</em> form—with right understanding: ‘This is not mine, I am not this, this is not my self.’

## mn109:17.1  `nya17`
- **PLI**: Yā kāci vedanā …
- **EN**: You should truly see any kind of feeling …

## mn109:17.2
- **PLI**: yā kāci saññā …
- **EN**: perception …

## mn109:17.3
- **PLI**: ye keci saṅkhārā …
- **EN**: choices …

## mn109:17.4
- **PLI**: yaṁ kiñci viññāṇaṁ atītānāgatapaccuppannaṁ ajjhattaṁ vā bahiddhā vā oḷārikaṁ vā sukhumaṁ vā hīnaṁ vā paṇītaṁ vā yaṁ dūre santike vā sabbaṁ viññāṇaṁ: ‘netaṁ mama, nesohamasmi, na meso attā’ti evametaṁ yathābhūtaṁ sammappaññāya daṭṭhabbaṁ.
- **EN**: consciousness at all—past, future, or present; internal or external; solid or subtle; inferior or superior; far or near: <em>all</em> consciousness—with right understanding: ‘This is not mine, I am not this, this is not my self.’

## mn109:17.5
- **PLI**: Evaṁ passaṁ, bhikkhave, sutavā ariyasāvako rūpasmimpi nibbindati, vedanāyapi nibbindati, saññāyapi nibbindati, saṅkhāresupi nibbindati, viññāṇasmimpi nibbindati;
- **EN**: Seeing this, a learned noble disciple grows disillusioned with form, feeling, perception, choices, and consciousness.

## mn109:18.1  `nya18`
- **PLI**: nibbindaṁ virajjati, virāgā vimuccati. Vimuttasmiṁ vimuttamiti ñāṇaṁ hoti.
- **EN**: Being disillusioned, desire fades away. When desire fades away they’re freed. When they’re freed, they know they’re freed.

## mn109:18.2
- **PLI**: ‘Khīṇā jāti, vusitaṁ brahmacariyaṁ, kataṁ karaṇīyaṁ, nāparaṁ itthattāyā’ti pajānātī”ti.
- **EN**: They understand: ‘Rebirth is ended, the spiritual journey has been completed, what had to be done has been done, there is nothing further for this place.’”

## mn109:18.3  `ms11M_212`
- **PLI**: Idamavoca bhagavā.
- **EN**: That is what the Buddha said.

## mn109:18.4
- **PLI**: Attamanā te bhikkhū bhagavato bhāsitaṁ abhinandunti.
- **EN**: Satisfied, the monks approved what the Buddha said.
- **NOTE**: The parallel at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn22.82/en/sujato#15.1'>SN 22.82:15.1</a> has a completely different closing passage. Generally speaking, such passages were added at a later date to wrap up the teaching portion, and there is considerable variation within the Pali texts, let alone Chinese and other parallels.

## mn109:18.5
- **PLI**: Imasmiñca pana veyyākaraṇasmiṁ bhaññamāne saṭṭhimattānaṁ bhikkhūnaṁ anupādāya āsavehi cittāni vimucciṁsūti.
- **EN**: And while this discourse was being spoken, the minds of sixty monks were freed from defilements by not grasping.

## mn109:18.6
- **PLI**: Mahāpuṇṇamasuttaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ navamaṁ.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)
