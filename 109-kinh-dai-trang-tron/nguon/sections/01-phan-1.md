## mn109:5.1  `nya5`

## mn109:8.1  `ms11M_203, nya8, pts-vp-pli3.17`

## mn109:9.6  `ms11M_205, ndp11.79`
- **PLI**: “Cattāro kho, bhikkhu, mahābhūtā hetu, cattāro mahābhūtā paccayo rūpakkhandhassa paññāpanāya.
- **EN**: “The four principal states are the reason why the aggregate of form is found.

## mn109:9.7
- **PLI**: Phasso hetu, phasso paccayo vedanākkhandhassa paññāpanāya.
- **EN**: Contact is the reason why the aggregates of feeling,
- **NOTE**: In dependent origination, “contact” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>phassa</i>) is the immediate condition for “feeling” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>vedanā</i>). But contact can also be regarded as the immediate condition for all three: feeling, perception, and choices (eg. <a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn35.93/en/sujato#1.14'>SN 35.93:1.14</a>). These things are always active in consciousness, so they can be treated as simultaneous, as arising in sequence, or as mutually conditioning.

## mn109:9.8
- **PLI**: Phasso hetu, phasso paccayo saññākkhandhassa paññāpanāya.
- **EN**: perception,
- **NOTE**: Feeling and “perception” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>saññā</i>) are closely connected, since how we feel conditions how we recognize and interpret. While contact directly underlies feeling, perception works so quickly that is can be included as a direct consequence as well.

## mn109:9.9
- **PLI**: Phasso hetu, phasso paccayo saṅkhārakkhandhassa paññāpanāya.
- **EN**: and choices are found.
- **NOTE**: Likewise with “choices” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>saṅkhārā</i>), which are also part of the complex of reactivity that normally take effect immediately. For example, suppose we try eating a berry in the forest. “Contact” with its bitter taste “feels” unpleasant, so we “perceive” it as poisonous, and “choose” to spit it out.

## mn109:9.10
- **PLI**: Nāmarūpaṁ kho, bhikkhu, hetu, nāmarūpaṁ paccayo viññāṇakkhandhassa paññāpanāyā”ti.
- **EN**: Name and form are the reasons why the aggregate of consciousness is found.”
- **NOTE**: Normally in dependent origination, it is consciousness that is a condition for name and form. The presentation here assumes their mutual conditioning, as spelled out at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn15/en/sujato#22.1'>DN 15:22.1</a>. Name and form are the embryo that supports consciousness in the process of rebirth, while the physical body with its senses, and the psychological functions included under “name” continue to evolve and grow with consciousness through life.
