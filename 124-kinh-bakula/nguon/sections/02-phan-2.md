## mn124:39.1  `cck14.242, ms11M_570, msdiv212, ndp11.193, nya39`
- **PLI**: Labheyyāhaṁ, āvuso bākula, imasmiṁ dhammavinaye pabbajjaṁ, labheyyaṁ upasampadan”ti.
- **EN**: “Reverend Bakkula, may I receive the going forth, the ordination in this teaching and training?”

## mn124:39.2
- **PLI**: Alattha kho acelakassapo imasmiṁ dhammavinaye pabbajjaṁ, alattha upasampadaṁ.
- **EN**: And the naked ascetic Kassapa received the going forth, the ordination in this teaching and training.

## mn124:39.3
- **PLI**: Acirūpasampanno panāyasmā kassapo eko vūpakaṭṭho appamatto ātāpī pahitatto viharanto nacirasseva—yassatthāya kulaputtā sammadeva agārasmā anagāriyaṁ pabbajanti, tadanuttaraṁ—brahmacariyapariyosānaṁ diṭṭheva dhamme sayaṁ abhiññā sacchikatvā upasampajja vihāsi.
- **EN**: Not long after his ordination, Venerable Kassapa, living alone, withdrawn, diligent, keen, and resolute, soon realized the supreme culmination of the spiritual path in this very life. He lived having achieved with his own insight the goal for which gentlemen rightly go forth from the lay life to homelessness.

## mn124:39.4
- **PLI**: “Khīṇā jāti, vusitaṁ brahmacariyaṁ, kataṁ karaṇīyaṁ, nāparaṁ itthattāyā”ti abbhaññāsi.
- **EN**: He understood: “Rebirth is ended; the spiritual journey has been completed; what had to be done has been done; there is nothing further for this place.”

## mn124:39.5
- **PLI**: Aññataro kho panāyasmā kassapo arahataṁ ahosi.
- **EN**: And Venerable Kassapa became one of the perfected.

## mn124:40.1  `bj12.302, dr13.237, ms11M_571, nya40`
- **PLI**: Atha kho āyasmā bākulo aparena samayena avāpuraṇaṁ ādāya vihārena vihāraṁ upasaṅkamitvā evamāha:
- **EN**: Then some time later Venerable Bakkula, taking a latchkey, went from dwelling to dwelling, saying,
- **NOTE**: The “latchkey” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>avāpuraṇa</i>) was a universal key inserted into outside doors to lift the bolt within (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn22.90/en/sujato#1.2'>SN 22.90:1.2</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an9.11/en/sujato#2.1'>AN 9.11:2.1</a>). He took it to access the inner dwellings in the monastery to make his announcement.
