## mn137:26.5
- **PLI**: Tathāgatena hi, bhikkhave, arahatā sammāsambuddhena purisadammo sārito aṭṭha disā vidhāvati.
- **EN**: But driven by the Realized One, the perfected one, the fully awakened Buddha, a person in training proceeds in eight directions:
