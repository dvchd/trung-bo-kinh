## mn137:14.1  `cck14.372, dr13.368, ms11M_931, msdiv308, nya14, sya14.405, vri14.267`
- **PLI**: Tattha katamā cha gehasitā upekkhā?
- **EN**: And in this context what are the six kinds of domestic equanimity?

## mn137:14.2
- **PLI**: Cakkhunā rūpaṁ disvā uppajjati upekkhā bālassa mūḷhassa puthujjanassa anodhijinassa avipākajinassa anādīnavadassāvino assutavato puthujjanassa.
- **EN**: When seeing a sight with their eyes, equanimity arises for the unlearned ordinary person—a foolish ordinary person who has not overcome their limitations and the results of deeds, and is blind to the drawbacks.

## mn137:14.3
- **PLI**: Yā evarūpā upekkhā, rūpaṁ sā nātivattati.
- **EN**: Such equanimity does not transcend the sight.

## mn137:14.4
- **PLI**: Tasmā sā upekkhā ‘gehasitā’ti vuccati.
- **EN**: That’s why it’s called domestic equanimity.

## mn137:14.5

- **EN**: When hearing a sound with their ears …

## mn137:14.6
- **PLI**: ghānena gandhaṁ ghāyitvā …
- **EN**: When smelling an odor with their nose …

## mn137:14.7
- **PLI**: jivhāya rasaṁ sāyitvā …
- **EN**: When tasting a flavor with their tongue …

## mn137:14.8
- **PLI**: kāyena phoṭṭhabbaṁ phusitvā …
- **EN**: When encountering a touch with their body …

## mn137:14.9
- **PLI**: manasā dhammaṁ viññāya uppajjati upekkhā bālassa mūḷhassa puthujjanassa anodhijinassa avipākajinassa anādīnavadassāvino assutavato puthujjanassa.
- **EN**: When knowing an idea with their mind, equanimity arises for the unlearned ordinary person—a foolish ordinary person who has not overcome their limitations and the results of deeds, and is blind to the drawbacks.

## mn137:14.10
- **PLI**: Yā evarūpā upekkhā, dhammaṁ sā nātivattati.
- **EN**: Such equanimity does not transcend the idea.

## mn137:14.11

- **EN**: That’s why it’s called domestic equanimity.

- **EN**: These are the six kinds of domestic equanimity.
