## mn43:19.1  `ms9M_1535, nya19`
- **PLI**: “Paṭhamaṁ panāvuso, jhānaṁ katiaṅgikan”ti?
- **EN**: “But how many factors does the first absorption have?”

## mn43:19.2  `ms9M_1536`
- **PLI**: “Paṭhamaṁ kho, āvuso, jhānaṁ pañcaṅgikaṁ.
- **EN**: “The first absorption has five factors.

## mn43:19.3
- **PLI**: Idhāvuso, paṭhamaṁ jhānaṁ samāpannassa bhikkhuno vitakko ca vattati, vicāro ca pīti ca sukhañca cittekaggatā ca.
- **EN**: When a mendicant has entered the first absorption, placing the mind, keeping it connected, rapture, bliss, and unification of mind are present.
- **NOTE**: These five absorption factors are found in the suttas here and, with other factors appended, at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn111/en/sujato#4.1'>MN 111:4.1</a>. The list summarizes the normal depiction of the first absorption, as the first four factors are all part of the standard first absorption formula, and all absorption or <i lang='pi' translate='no'>samādhi</i> is characterized by unification of mind (eg. <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn20/en/sujato#3.3'>MN 20:3.3</a>). They became a standard analysis of the first absorption in late canonical texts (eg. <a href='https://suttacentral.net/ps1.5/en/sujato#10.2'>Ps 1.5:10.2</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/ds2.1.1/en/sujato#3.1'>Ds 2.1.1:3.1</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/vb12/en/sujato#99.1'>Vb 12:99.1</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/pe6/en/sujato#66.2'>Pe 6:66.2</a>).

## mn43:19.4
- **PLI**: Paṭhamaṁ kho, āvuso, jhānaṁ evaṁ pañcaṅgikan”ti.
- **EN**: That’s how the first absorption has five factors.”
