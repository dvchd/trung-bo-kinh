## mn119:30.1  `nya30`

- **EN**: Suppose a chariot stood harnessed to thoroughbreds at a level crossroads, with a goad ready.
- **NOTE**: Similar imagery is found at Kaṭha Upaniṣad 3.3–9.

## mn119:31.2

- **EN**: A deft horse trainer, a master charioteer, might mount the chariot, taking the reins in his right hand and goad in the left. He’d drive out and back wherever he wishes, whenever he wishes.
