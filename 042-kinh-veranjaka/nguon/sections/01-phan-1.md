## mn42:1.2
- **PLI**: ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme.
- **EN**: At one time the Buddha was staying near Sāvatthī in Jeta’s Grove, Anāthapiṇḍika’s monastery.

## mn42:2.1  `nya2`
- **PLI**: Tena kho pana samayena verañjakā brāhmaṇagahapatikā sāvatthiyaṁ paṭivasanti kenacideva karaṇīyena.
- **EN**: Now at that time the brahmins and householders of Verañjā were residing in Sāvatthī on some business.
- **NOTE**: Verañjā has not been conclusively identified, but it lay between Madhurā (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/an4.53/en/sujato#1.1'>AN 4.53:1.1</a>) and Soreyya (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-bu-vb-pj1/en/sujato#4.18'>Bu Pj 1:4.18</a>). If modern Soron is Soreyya, it is tempting to accept Dhammika’s identification of Verañjā with Atranjikhera (note to <a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-bu-vb-pj1/en/sujato#4.18'>Bu Pj 1:4.18</a>) just north of Etah in Uttar Pradesh. Atranjikhera is a huge mound whose excavation revealed four layers of occupation, the latest of which was the Northern Black Polished Ware culture of the Buddha’s day. It was a staging ground for horse traders from Kasmir along the northern route from Kashmir, which more or less followed the course of the Ganges.
