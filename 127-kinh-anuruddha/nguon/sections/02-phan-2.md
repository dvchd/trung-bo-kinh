## mn127:9.1  `ms11M_619, msdiv232, ndp11.215, nya9, sya14.288`
- **PLI**: Catasso kho imā gahapati, bhavūpapattiyo.
- **EN**: Householder, there are these four kinds of rebirth in a future life.
- **NOTE**: “Rebirth in a future life” renders <i lang='pi' translate='no'>bhavūpapatti</i>. This section explains how the meditation one develops can shape one’s rebirth.

## mn127:9.2
- **PLI**: Katamā catasso?
- **EN**: What four?

## mn127:9.3
- **PLI**: Idha, gahapati, ekacco ‘parittābhā’ti pharitvā adhimuccitvā viharati.
- **EN**: Take someone who meditates focused on pervading ‘limited luminosity’.

## mn127:9.4
- **PLI**: So kāyassa bhedā paraṁ maraṇā parittābhānaṁ devānaṁ sahabyataṁ upapajjati.
- **EN**: When their body breaks up, after death, they’re reborn in the company of the gods of limited luminosity.
- **NOTE**: The gods of limited radiance are mentioned at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn41/en/sujato#18-42.22'>MN 41:18–42.22</a>, where they are reborn due to a limited practice of the second absorption.

## mn127:9.5
- **PLI**: Idha pana, gahapati, ekacco ‘appamāṇābhā’ti pharitvā adhimuccitvā viharati.
- **EN**: Next, take someone who meditates focused on pervading ‘limitless luminosity’.

## mn127:9.6
- **PLI**: So kāyassa bhedā paraṁ maraṇā appamāṇābhānaṁ devānaṁ sahabyataṁ upapajjati.
- **EN**: When their body breaks up, after death, they’re reborn in the company of the gods of limitless luminosity.

## mn127:9.7
- **PLI**: Idha pana, gahapati, ekacco ‘saṅkiliṭṭhābhā’ti pharitvā adhimuccitvā viharati.
- **EN**: Next, take someone who meditates focused on pervading ‘corrupted radiance’.
- **NOTE**: Normally the suttas maintain a clear distinction between the “corrupted” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>saṅkiliṭṭha</i>) mind that is purified before attaining the radiance of absorption. This passage, however, suggests that things might not always be so clear-cut.

## mn127:9.8
- **PLI**: So kāyassa bhedā paraṁ maraṇā saṅkiliṭṭhābhānaṁ devānaṁ sahabyataṁ upapajjati.
- **EN**: When their body breaks up, after death, they’re reborn in the company of the gods of corrupted luminosity.

## mn127:9.9
- **PLI**: Idha pana, gahapati, ekacco ‘parisuddhābhā’ti pharitvā adhimuccitvā viharati.
- **EN**: Next, take someone who meditates focused on pervading ‘pure radiance’.

## mn127:9.10
- **PLI**: So kāyassa bhedā paraṁ maraṇā parisuddhābhānaṁ devānaṁ sahabyataṁ upapajjati.
- **EN**: When their body breaks up, after death, they’re reborn in the company of the gods of pure luminosity.

## mn127:9.11
- **PLI**: Imā kho, gahapati, catasso bhavūpapattiyo.
- **EN**: These are the four kinds of rebirth in a future life.
