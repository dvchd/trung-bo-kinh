# Phần 3  (30 đoạn)

## mn11:14.1  `dr12.122, ms9M_470, msdiv144, nya14`
- **PLI**: Tathāgato ca kho, bhikkhave, arahaṁ sammāsambuddho sabbupādānapariññāvādo paṭijānamāno sammā sabbupādānapariññaṁ paññapeti—
- **EN**: The Realized One, the perfected one, the fully awakened Buddha claims to propound the complete understanding of all kinds of grasping.

## mn11:14.2
- **PLI**: kāmupādānassa pariññaṁ paññapeti, diṭṭhupādānassa pariññaṁ paññapeti, sīlabbatupādānassa pariññaṁ paññapeti, attavādupādānassa pariññaṁ paññapeti.
- **EN**: He describes the complete understanding of grasping at sensual pleasures, views, precepts and observances, and theories of a self.

## mn11:15.1  `nya15`
- **PLI**: Evarūpe kho, bhikkhave, dhammavinaye yo satthari pasādo so sammaggato akkhāyati;
- **EN**: In such a teaching and training, confidence in the Teacher is said to be rightly placed.

## mn11:15.2

## mn11:15.3

- **EN**: fulfillment of the precepts,

## mn11:15.4
- **PLI**: yā sahadhammikesu piyamanāpatā sā sammaggatā akkhāyati.
- **EN**: and love and affection for those sharing the same path are said to be rightly placed.

## mn11:15.5
- **PLI**: Taṁ kissa hetu?
- **EN**: Why is that?

## mn11:15.6
- **PLI**: Evañhetaṁ, bhikkhave, hoti yathā taṁ svākkhāte dhammavinaye suppavedite niyyānike upasamasaṁvattanike sammāsambuddhappavedite.
- **EN**: It’s because that teaching and training is well explained and well propounded, emancipating, leading to peace, proclaimed by a fully awakened Buddha.

## mn11:16.1  `bj10.164, cck12.125, csp1ed9.97, csp2ed9.97, ms9M_471, msdiv145, ndp9.95, nya16, sya12.135, vri12.98`
- **PLI**: Ime ca, bhikkhave, cattāro upādānā. Kiṁnidānā kiṁsamudayā kiṁjātikā kiṁpabhavā?
- **EN**: What is the source, origin, birthplace, and inception of these four kinds of grasping?
- **NOTE**: Grasping is fully understood only when its source is known. Here the Buddha begins a partial treatment of dependent origination, implicitly connecting this to his special doctrine of not-self.

## mn11:17.1  `cck12.126, dr12.123, ms9M_472, nya17, sya12.136`
- **PLI**: Yato ca kho, bhikkhave, bhikkhuno avijjā pahīnā hoti vijjā uppannā, so avijjāvirāgā vijjuppādā neva kāmupādānaṁ upādiyati, na diṭṭhupādānaṁ upādiyati, na sīlabbatupādānaṁ upādiyati, na attavādupādānaṁ upādiyati.
- **EN**: When that mendicant has given up ignorance and given rise to knowledge, with the fading away of ignorance and the arising of knowledge they don’t grasp at sensual pleasures, views, precepts and observances, or theories of a self.

## mn11:17.2
- **PLI**: Anupādiyaṁ na paritassati, aparitassaṁ paccattaññeva parinibbāyati.
- **EN**: Not grasping, they’re not anxious. Not being anxious, they personally become extinguished.

## mn11:17.3
- **PLI**: ‘Khīṇā jāti, vusitaṁ brahmacariyaṁ, kataṁ karaṇīyaṁ, nāparaṁ itthattāyā’ti pajānātī”ti.
- **EN**: They understand: ‘Rebirth is ended, the spiritual journey has been completed, what had to be done has been done, there is nothing further for this place.’”
