# Phần 1  (50 đoạn)

## mn11:1.1  `csp1ed9.92, csp2ed9.92, dr12.116, ms9M_452, msdiv139, ndp9.90, nya1, sya12.128, vri12.93`
- **PLI**: Evaṁ me sutaṁ—
- **EN**: So I have heard.
- **NOTE**: Multiple suttas (eg. <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn8/en/sujato'>DN 8</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn25/en/sujato'>DN 25</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn12/en/sujato'>MN 12</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an6.64/en/sujato'>AN 6.64</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an9.11/en/sujato'>AN 9.11</a>, and <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an10.21/en/sujato'>AN 10.21</a>) invoke the indomitable force of the lion’s roar, the most mighty sound of the jungle (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/sn22.78/en/sujato#1.2'>SN 22.78:1.2</a>).

## mn11:1.2
- **PLI**: ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme.
- **EN**: At one time the Buddha was staying near Sāvatthī in Jeta’s Grove, Anāthapiṇḍika’s monastery.

## mn11:1.3
- **PLI**: Tatra kho bhagavā bhikkhū āmantesi:
- **EN**: There the Buddha addressed the mendicants,

## mn11:1.4
- **PLI**: “bhikkhavo”ti.
- **EN**: “Mendicants!”

## mn11:1.5
- **PLI**: “Bhadante”ti te bhikkhū bhagavato paccassosuṁ.
- **EN**: “Venerable sir,” they replied.

## mn11:1.6
- **PLI**: Bhagavā etadavoca:
- **EN**: The Buddha said this:

## mn11:2.1  `ms9M_453, nya2, pts-vp-pli1.64`
- **PLI**: “Idheva, bhikkhave, samaṇo, idha dutiyo samaṇo, idha tatiyo samaṇo, idha catuttho samaṇo;
- **EN**: “‘Only here is there a true ascetic, here a second ascetic, here a third ascetic, and here a fourth ascetic.
- **NOTE**: The same teaching is given more briefly at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an4.241/en/sujato#1.1'>AN 4.241:1.1</a>, where the four ascetics are defined as the stream-enterer, once-returner, non-returner, and perfected one, and at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn16/en/sujato#5.27.3'>DN 16:5.27.3</a>, where the presence of the four ascetics is attributed to the practice of the eightfold path.

## mn11:2.2
- **PLI**: suññā parappavādā samaṇebhi aññehīti.
- **EN**: Other sects are empty of ascetics.’

## mn11:2.3
- **PLI**: Evametaṁ, bhikkhave, sammā sīhanādaṁ nadatha.
- **EN**: This, mendicants, is how you should rightly roar your lion’s roar.

## mn11:3.1  `bj10.156, cck12.119, dr12.117, ms9M_454, msdiv140, nya3, sya12.129`
- **PLI**: Ṭhānaṁ kho panetaṁ, bhikkhave, vijjati yaṁ aññatitthiyā paribbājakā evaṁ vadeyyuṁ:

## mn11:3.2
- **PLI**: ‘ko panāyasmantānaṁ assāso, kiṁ balaṁ, yena tumhe āyasmanto evaṁ vadetha—
- **EN**: ‘But what is the source of the venerables’ certainty and forcefulness that they say this?’
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Ko assāso kiṁ balaṁ</i> is found only here and at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn93/en/sujato#6.7'>MN 93:6.7</a>, where it also deals with an unequivocal claim to superiority.

- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)

## mn11:3.4
- **PLI**: suññā parappavādā samaṇebhi aññehī’ti?

## mn11:3.5
- **PLI**: Evaṁvādino, bhikkhave, aññatitthiyā paribbājakā evamassu vacanīyā:
- **EN**: You should say to them:

## mn11:3.6
- **PLI**: ‘atthi kho no, āvuso, tena bhagavatā jānatā passatā arahatā sammāsambuddhena cattāro dhammā akkhātā ye mayaṁ attani sampassamānā evaṁ vadema—
- **EN**: ‘There are four things explained by the Blessed One, who knows and sees, the perfected one, the fully awakened Buddha. Seeing these things in ourselves we say that:
- **NOTE**: Self-confidence is reflective, not dogmatic.

## mn11:3.7
- **PLI**: idheva samaṇo, idha dutiyo samaṇo, idha tatiyo samaṇo, idha catuttho samaṇo;
- **EN**: “Only here is there a true ascetic, here a second ascetic, here a third ascetic, and here a fourth ascetic.

## mn11:3.8
- **PLI**: suññā parappavādā samaṇebhi aññehīti.
- **EN**: Other sects are empty of ascetics.”

## mn11:3.9
- **PLI**: Katame cattāro?
- **EN**: What four?

## mn11:3.10
- **PLI**: Atthi kho no, āvuso, satthari pasādo, atthi dhamme pasādo, atthi sīlesu paripūrakāritā;
- **EN**: We have confidence in the Teacher, we have confidence in the teaching, and we have fulfilled the precepts.

## mn11:3.11
- **PLI**: sahadhammikā kho pana piyā manāpā—
- **EN**: And we have love and affection for those who share our path,
- **NOTE**: This appears to be the only occurrence of <i lang='pi' translate='no'>sahadhammika</i> in this sense in early Pali. Normally it has the sense “legitimate” (as eg. <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn90/en/sujato#5.4'>MN 90:5.4</a>). The parallel at MA 103 has <span lang='lzh'>同道</span>.

## mn11:3.12
- **PLI**: gahaṭṭhā ceva pabbajitā ca.
- **EN**: both laypeople and renunciates.

## mn11:3.13
- **PLI**: Ime kho no, āvuso, tena bhagavatā jānatā passatā arahatā sammāsambuddhena cattāro dhammā akkhātā ye mayaṁ attani sampassamānā evaṁ vadema—
- **EN**: These are the four things.’

## mn11:3.14
- **PLI**: idheva samaṇo, idha dutiyo samaṇo, idha tatiyo samaṇo, idha catuttho samaṇo;
