# Phần 2  (50 đoạn)

## mn41:13.6
- **PLI**: Evaṁ kho, gahapatayo, catubbidhaṁ vācāya dhammacariyāsamacariyā hoti.
- **EN**: This is how principled and moral conduct is fourfold by way of speech.

## mn41:14.1  `ms9M_1466, nya14`
- **PLI**: Kathañca, gahapatayo, tividhaṁ manasā dhammacariyāsamacariyā hoti?
- **EN**: And how is principled and moral conduct threefold by way of mind?

## mn41:14.2
- **PLI**: Idha, gahapatayo, ekacco anabhijjhālu hoti, yaṁ taṁ parassa paravittūpakaraṇaṁ taṁ nābhijjhātā hoti: ‘aho vata yaṁ parassa taṁ mamassā’ti.
- **EN**: It’s when someone is not covetous. They don’t covet the wealth and belongings of others: ‘Oh, if only their belongings were mine!’

## mn41:14.3  `cck12.501, dr12.462, ms9M_1467`
- **PLI**: Abyāpannacitto kho pana hoti appaduṭṭhamanasaṅkappo: ‘ime sattā averā abyābajjhā anīghā sukhī attānaṁ pariharantū’ti.
- **EN**: They have a kind heart and loving intentions: ‘May these sentient beings live free of enmity and ill will, untroubled and happy!’

## mn41:14.4  `ms9M_1468, sya12.525`
- **PLI**: Sammādiṭṭhiko kho pana hoti aviparītadassano:
- **EN**: They have right view, an undistorted perspective:

## mn41:14.5
- **PLI**: ‘atthi dinnaṁ atthi yiṭṭhaṁ atthi hutaṁ, atthi sukatadukkaṭānaṁ kammānaṁ phalaṁ vipāko, atthi ayaṁ loko atthi paro loko, atthi mātā atthi pitā, atthi sattā opapātikā, atthi loke samaṇabrāhmaṇā sammaggatā sammāpaṭipannā ye imañca lokaṁ parañca lokaṁ sayaṁ abhiññā sacchikatvā pavedentī’ti.
- **EN**: ‘There is meaning in giving, sacrifice, and offerings. There are fruits and results of good and bad deeds. There is an afterlife. There are such things as mother and father, and beings that are reborn spontaneously. And there are ascetics and brahmins who are rightly comported and rightly practiced, and who describe the afterlife after realizing it with their own insight.’

## mn41:14.6
- **PLI**: Evaṁ kho, gahapatayo, tividhaṁ manasā dhammacariyāsamacariyā hoti.
- **EN**: This is how principled and moral conduct is threefold by way of mind.

## mn41:14.7  `ms9M_1469`
- **PLI**: Evaṁ dhammacariyāsamacariyāhetu kho, gahapatayo, evam’idhekacce sattā kāyassa bhedā paraṁ maraṇā sugatiṁ saggaṁ lokaṁ upapajjanti.
- **EN**: This is how principled and moral conduct is the reason why some sentient beings, when their body breaks up, after death, are reborn in a good place, a heavenly realm.

## mn41:15.1  `bj10.670, csp1ed9.359, csp2ed9.359, ms9M_1470, msdiv442, nya15, pts-vp-pli1.289, vri12.363`
- **PLI**: Ākaṅkheyya ce, gahapatayo, dhammacārī samacārī:
- **EN**: Someone of principled and moral conduct might wish:

## mn41:15.2
- **PLI**: ‘aho vatāhaṁ kāyassa bhedā paraṁ maraṇā khattiyamahāsālānaṁ sahabyataṁ upapajjeyyan’ti;
- **EN**: ‘If only, when my body breaks up, after death, I would be reborn in the company of well-to-do aristocrats!’
- **NOTE**: The Buddha is, of course, not recommending this, merely accommodating it. At this point, the ten ways of skillful deeds are both necessary and sufficient for the desired outcome. This changes with the higher realms, where absorption is also required, and the realization of arahantship, which requires the whole noble eightfold path. See note at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn120/en/sujato#12.12'>MN 120:12.12</a>.

## mn41:15.3
- **PLI**: ṭhānaṁ kho panetaṁ vijjati, yaṁ so kāyassa bhedā paraṁ maraṇā khattiyamahāsālānaṁ sahabyataṁ upapajjeyya.
- **EN**: It’s possible that this might happen.

## mn41:15.4
- **PLI**: Taṁ kissa hetu?
- **EN**: Why is that?

## mn41:15.5
- **PLI**: Tathā hi so dhammacārī samacārī.
- **EN**: Because they have principled and moral conduct.
