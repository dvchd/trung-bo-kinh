# Phần 3  (26 đoạn)

## mn103:14.2
- **PLI**: ‘mayhañca kho vihesā bhavissati parassa ca puggalassa upaghāto. Paro hi puggalo kodhano upanāhī daḷhadiṭṭhī duppaṭinissaggī, na cāhaṁ sakkomi etaṁ puggalaṁ akusalā vuṭṭhāpetvā kusale patiṭṭhāpetun’ti.
- **EN**: ‘I will be troubled and the other individual will be hurt, for they’re irritable and acrimonious. And they hold fast to their views, refusing to let go. I cannot draw them away from the unskillful and establish them in the skillful.’

## mn103:14.3
- **PLI**: Evarūpe, bhikkhave, puggale upekkhā nātimaññitabbā.
- **EN**: Don’t underestimate the value of equanimity regarding such an individual.
- **NOTE**: Wisdom knows that sometimes there is nothing to be done. That doesn’t mean one gives up, but rather, that sometimes patience is the best way. Note that this is the last option, however, and gentle intervention is always preferred.

## mn103:15.1  `cck14.45, csp1ed11.31, csp2ed11.31, dr13.42, ms11M_83, msdiv40, nya15, sya14.47`
- **PLI**: Tesañca vo, bhikkhave, samaggānaṁ sammodamānānaṁ avivadamānānaṁ sikkhataṁ aññamaññassa vacīsaṁhāro uppajjeyya diṭṭhipaḷāso cetaso āghāto appaccayo anabhiraddhi.
- **EN**: As you train in harmony, appreciating each other, without quarreling, the sides might continue to bring up settled issues with each other, with contempt for each other’s views, resentful, bitter, and exasperated.
- **NOTE**: Readings include <i lang='pi' translate='no'>vacīsaṁsāro</i>, <i lang='pi' translate='no'>vacīsaṁhāro</i>, and <i lang='pi' translate='no'>vacīsaṅkhāro</i>. <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Vacīsaṁsāro</i> is found, without recorded variants, in the parallel passage at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an2.63/en/sujato#1.1'>AN 2.63:1.1</a>. The commentary here glosses with <i lang='pi' translate='no'>vacanasañcāro</i>, while the subcommentary to AN 2.63 has <i lang='pi' translate='no'>pavattamānā</i>, both of which support the dominant reading <i lang='pi' translate='no'>vacīsaṁsāro</i>. Their explanations differ, however as the commentary here explains as spreading rumors from one place to another, while at AN 2.63 it is said to be ongoing arguing. Since the normal meaning of <i lang='pi' translate='no'>saṁsāra</i> is “endless ongoing”, and since, in both MN and AN, this follows a passage where disputes were supposed to have been settled, perhaps the sense is “continuing to bring up matters that have been settled”.

## mn103:15.2
- **PLI**: Tattha ekatopakkhikānaṁ bhikkhūnaṁ yaṁ bhikkhuṁ suvacataraṁ maññeyyātha so upasaṅkamitvā evamassa vacanīyo:
- **EN**: In this case you should approach whichever mendicant you think is most amenable among those who side with one party and say to them:

## mn103:15.3
- **PLI**: ‘yaṁ no, āvuso, amhākaṁ samaggānaṁ sammodamānānaṁ avivadamānānaṁ sikkhataṁ aññamaññassa vacīsaṁhāro uppanno diṭṭhipaḷāso cetaso āghāto appaccayo anabhiraddhi, taṁ jānamāno samaṇo garaheyyā’ti.
- **EN**: ‘Reverend, as we were training, the sides continued to bring up settled issues with each other. If the Ascetic knew about this, would he criticize it?’

## mn103:15.4
- **PLI**: Sammā byākaramāno, bhikkhave, bhikkhu evaṁ byākareyya:
- **EN**: Answering rightly, the mendicant should say:

- **EN**: ‘Yes, reverend, he would.’

## mn103:15.7

- **EN**: ‘No, reverend, one cannot.’

## mn103:17.1  `cck14.46, dr13.43, ms11M_85, ndp11.36, nya17, sya14.48`
- **PLI**: Tañce, bhikkhave, bhikkhuṁ pare evaṁ puccheyyuṁ:
- **EN**: If others should ask that mendicant:

## mn103:17.2
- **PLI**: ‘āyasmatā no ete bhikkhū akusalā vuṭṭhāpetvā kusale patiṭṭhāpitā’ti?
- **EN**: ‘Were you the venerable who drew those mendicants away from the unskillful and established them in the skillful?’

## mn103:17.3
- **PLI**: Sammā byākaramāno, bhikkhave, bhikkhu evaṁ byākareyya:
- **EN**: Answering rightly, the mendicant should say:

## mn103:17.4
- **PLI**: ‘idhāhaṁ, āvuso, yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkamiṁ, tassa me bhagavā dhammaṁ desesi, tāhaṁ dhammaṁ sutvā tesaṁ bhikkhūnaṁ abhāsiṁ.
- **EN**: ‘Well, reverends, I approached the Buddha. He taught me the Dhamma. After hearing that teaching I explained it to those mendicants.

## mn103:17.5
- **PLI**: Taṁ te bhikkhū dhammaṁ sutvā akusalā vuṭṭhahiṁsu, kusale patiṭṭhahiṁsū’ti.
- **EN**: When those mendicants heard that teaching they were drawn away from the unskillful and established in the skillful.’

## mn103:17.6
- **PLI**: Evaṁ byākaramāno kho, bhikkhave, bhikkhu na ceva attānaṁ ukkaṁseti, na paraṁ vambheti, dhammassa cānudhammaṁ byākaroti, na ca koci sahadhammiko vādānuvādo gārayhaṁ ṭhānaṁ āgacchatī”ti.
- **EN**: Answering in this way, that mendicant doesn’t glorify themselves or put others down. They answer in line with the teaching, with no legitimate grounds for rebuttal and criticism.”
