- **EN**: I see no reason for anyone—whether ascetic, brahmin, god, Māra, or the Divinity, or anyone else in the world—to legitimately scold me, saying: ‘Regardless of your goal in teaching the Dhamma, it does not deliver one who applies it to the complete ending of suffering.’

## mn12:26.2
- **PLI**: Etamahaṁ, sāriputta, nimittaṁ asamanupassanto khemappatto abhayappatto vesārajjappatto viharāmi.
- **EN**: Since I see no such reason, I live secure, fearless, and self-assured.

- **EN**: A Realized One possesses these four kinds of self-assurance. With these he claims the bull’s place, roars his lion’s roar in the assemblies, and turns the divine wheel.

## mn12:29.1  `dr12.132, ms9M_503, msdiv151, ndp9.103, nya29`
- **PLI**: Aṭṭha kho imā, sāriputta, parisā.
- **EN**: Sāriputta, there are these eight assemblies.
- **NOTE**: Mentioned also at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an8.69/en/sujato'>AN 8.69</a>, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn16/en/sujato#3.21.3'>DN 16:3.21.3</a>, and <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn33/en/sujato#3.1.139'>DN 33:3.1.139</a>.

## mn12:29.2
- **PLI**: Katamā aṭṭha?
- **EN**: What eight?

## mn12:29.3
- **PLI**: Khattiyaparisā, brāhmaṇaparisā, gahapatiparisā, samaṇaparisā, cātumahārājikaparisā, tāvatiṁsaparisā, māraparisā, brahmaparisā—
- **EN**: The assemblies of aristocrats, brahmins, householders, and ascetics. An assembly of the gods of the four great kings. An assembly of the gods of the thirty-three. An assembly of Māras. An assembly of divinities.

## mn12:29.4
- **PLI**: imā kho, sāriputta, aṭṭha parisā.
- **EN**: These are the eight assemblies.

## mn12:29.5
- **PLI**: Imehi kho, sāriputta, catūhi vesārajjehi samannāgato tathāgato imā aṭṭha parisā upasaṅkamati ajjhogāhati.
- **EN**: Possessing these four kinds of self-assurance, the Realized One approaches and enters right into these eight assemblies.
- **NOTE**: <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an8.69/en/sujato'>AN 8.69</a> and <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn16/en/sujato#3.21.3'>DN 16:3.21.3</a> both relate the Buddha entering these assemblies, but neither they, nor the Chinese parallel to this passage, attribute his confidence to the four kinds of self-assurance.

## mn12:29.6
- **PLI**: Abhijānāmi kho panāhaṁ, sāriputta, anekasataṁ khattiyaparisaṁ upasaṅkamitā.
- **EN**: I recall having approached an assembly of hundreds of aristocrats.
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Anekasataṁ khattiyaparisaṁ</i> is singular, “an assembly of hundreds of aristocrats”, rather than “hundreds of assemblies of aristocrats”.

## mn12:29.7
- **PLI**: Tatrapi mayā sannisinnapubbañceva, sallapitapubbañca, sākacchā ca samāpajjitapubbā.
- **EN**: There I used to sit with them, converse, and engage in discussion.

## mn12:29.8
- **PLI**: Tatra vata maṁ bhayaṁ vā sārajjaṁ vā okkamissatīti nimittametaṁ, sāriputta, na samanupassāmi.
- **EN**: But I don’t see any reason to feel afraid or insecure.
