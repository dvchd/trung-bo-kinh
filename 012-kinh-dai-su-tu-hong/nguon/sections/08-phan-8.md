# Phần 8  (20 đoạn)

## mn12:62.5
- **PLI**: Ahaṁ kho pana, sāriputta, etarahi jiṇṇo vuddho mahallako addhagato vayoanuppatto, āsītiko me vayo vattati.
- **EN**: For now I am old, elderly, and senior, I’m advanced in years, and have reached the final stage of life. I am eighty years old.
- **NOTE**: This situates this discourse, and hence the Sunakkhatta cycle as a whole, near the end of the Buddha’s life.

## mn12:62.6
- **PLI**: Idha me assu, sāriputta, cattāro sāvakā vassasatāyukā vassasatajīvino, paramāya satiyā ca gatiyā ca dhitiyā ca samannāgatā paramena ca paññāveyyattiyena.
- **EN**: Suppose I had four disciples with a lifespan of a hundred years. And they each were perfect in memory, rememberance, retention, and perfect lucidity of wisdom.
- **NOTE**: Memory (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>sati</i>), rememberance (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>gati</i>), retention (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>dhiti</i>), and perfect lucidity of wisdom (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>paññāveyyattiya</i>). The first three of these are exemplified by Ānanda (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/an1.220/en/sujato'>AN 1.220</a>).

## mn12:62.7
- **PLI**: Seyyathāpi, sāriputta, daḷhadhammā dhanuggaho sikkhito katahattho katūpāsano lahukena asanena appakasireneva tiriyaṁ tālacchāyaṁ atipāteyya,
- **EN**: Imagine how easily a well-trained expert archer with a strong bow would shoot a light arrow across the shadow of a palm tree.

## mn12:62.8
- **PLI**: evaṁ adhimattasatimanto evaṁ adhimattagatimanto evaṁ adhimattadhitimanto evaṁ paramena paññāveyyattiyena samannāgatā.
- **EN**: That’s how extraordinary they were in memory, rememberance, retention, and perfect lucidity of wisdom.

## mn12:62.9
- **PLI**: Te maṁ catunnaṁ satipaṭṭhānānaṁ upādāyupādāya pañhaṁ puccheyyuṁ, puṭṭho puṭṭho cāhaṁ tesaṁ byākareyyaṁ, byākatañca me byākatato dhāreyyuṁ, na ca maṁ dutiyakaṁ uttari paṭipuccheyyuṁ.
- **EN**: They’d bring up questions about the four kinds of mindfulness meditation again and again, and I would answer each question. They’d remember the answers and not ask the same question twice.

## mn12:62.10
- **PLI**: Aññatra asitapītakhāyitasāyitā aññatra uccārapassāvakammā, aññatra niddākilamathapaṭivinodanā apariyādinnāyevassa, sāriputta, tathāgatassa dhammadesanā, apariyādinnaṁyevassa tathāgatassa dhammapadabyañjanaṁ, apariyādinnaṁyevassa tathāgatassa pañhapaṭibhānaṁ.
- **EN**: And they’d pause only to eat and drink, go to the toilet, and sleep to dispel weariness. But the Realized One would not run out of Dhamma teachings, words and phrases of the teachings, or spontaneous answers.

## mn12:62.11
- **PLI**: Atha me te cattāro sāvakā vassasatāyukā vassasatajīvino vassasatassa accayena kālaṁ kareyyuṁ.
- **EN**: And at the end of a hundred years my four disciples would pass away.

## mn12:62.12
- **PLI**: Mañcakena cepi maṁ, sāriputta, pariharissatha, nevatthi tathāgatassa paññāveyyattiyassa aññathattaṁ.
- **EN**: Even if you have to carry me around on a stretcher, there will never be any deterioration in the Realized One’s lucidity of wisdom.

## mn12:64.1  `dr12.149, ms9M_535, msdiv162, ndp9.116, nya64`
- **PLI**: Tena kho pana samayena āyasmā nāgasamālo bhagavato piṭṭhito ṭhito hoti bhagavantaṁ bījayamāno.
- **EN**: Now at that time Venerable Nāgasamāla was standing behind the Buddha fanning him.
- **NOTE**: We meet Nāgasamāla in less auspicious circumstances at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/ud8.7/en/sujato'>Ud 8.7</a>, where he stubbornly disagrees with the Buddha on which path to take.

## mn12:64.2
- **PLI**: Atha kho āyasmā nāgasamālo bhagavantaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: Then he said to the Buddha:

## mn12:64.3
- **PLI**: “acchariyaṁ, bhante, abbhutaṁ, bhante.
- **EN**: “It’s incredible, sir, it’s amazing!

## mn12:64.4
- **PLI**: Api hi me, bhante, imaṁ dhammapariyāyaṁ sutvā lomāni haṭṭhāni.
- **EN**: While I was listening to this exposition of the teaching my hair stood up!

## mn12:64.5
- **PLI**: Konāmo ayaṁ, bhante, dhammapariyāyo”ti?
- **EN**: What is the name of this exposition of the teaching?”

## mn12:64.6
- **PLI**: “Tasmātiha tvaṁ, nāgasamāla, imaṁ dhammapariyāyaṁ lomahaṁsanapariyāyotveva naṁ dhārehī”ti.
- **EN**: “Well then, Nāgasamāla, you may remember this exposition of the teaching as ‘The Hair-raising Discourse’.”
- **NOTE**: This original title is widely attested in the tradition, being found in some <i lang='pi' translate='no'>uddāna</i> summary verses in Burmese and Sinhalese manuscripts, <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mil7.5.7/en/sujato#2.1'>Mil 7.5.7:2.1</a>, a Jātaka of the same name (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/ja94/en/sujato'>Ja 94</a>), several commentaries, and the Sanskrit and Chinese parallels. The subcommentary says that the name Mahāsīhanāda was given by the redactors.
