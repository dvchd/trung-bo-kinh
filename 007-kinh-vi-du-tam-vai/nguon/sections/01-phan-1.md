## mn7:2.1  `bj10.84, ms9M_176, nya2`
- **PLI**: “Seyyathāpi, bhikkhave, vatthaṁ saṅkiliṭṭhaṁ malaggahitaṁ;
- **EN**: “Suppose, mendicants, there was a cloth that was dirty and soiled.

## mn7:2.2
- **PLI**: tamenaṁ rajako yasmiṁ yasmiṁ raṅgajāte upasaṁhareyya—yadi nīlakāya yadi pītakāya yadi lohitakāya yadi mañjiṭṭhakāya durattavaṇṇamevassa aparisuddhavaṇṇamevassa.
- **EN**: No matter what dye the dyer applied—whether blue or yellow or red or magenta—it would look poorly dyed and impure in color.

## mn7:2.3
- **PLI**: Taṁ kissa hetu?
- **EN**: Why is that?

## mn7:2.4
- **PLI**: Aparisuddhattā, bhikkhave, vatthassa.
- **EN**: Because of the impurity of the cloth.

## mn7:2.5
- **PLI**: Evameva kho, bhikkhave, citte saṅkiliṭṭhe, duggati pāṭikaṅkhā.
- **EN**: In the same way, when the mind is corrupt, a bad destiny is to be expected.
- **NOTE**: A “bad destiny” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>duggati</i>) is any realm below the human, namely the animal, ghost, and hell realms.

## mn7:2.6
- **PLI**: Seyyathāpi, bhikkhave, vatthaṁ parisuddhaṁ pariyodātaṁ;
- **EN**: Suppose there was a cloth that was pure and clean.

## mn7:2.7
- **PLI**: tamenaṁ rajako yasmiṁ yasmiṁ raṅgajāte upasaṁhareyya—yadi nīlakāya yadi pītakāya yadi lohitakāya yadi mañjiṭṭhakāya—surattavaṇṇamevassa parisuddhavaṇṇamevassa.
- **EN**: No matter what dye the dyer applied—whether blue or yellow or red or magenta—it would look well dyed and pure in color.

## mn7:2.8
- **PLI**: Taṁ kissa hetu?
- **EN**: Why is that?

## mn7:2.9
- **PLI**: Parisuddhattā, bhikkhave, vatthassa.
- **EN**: Because of the purity of the cloth.

## mn7:2.10
- **PLI**: Evameva kho, bhikkhave, citte asaṅkiliṭṭhe, sugati pāṭikaṅkhā.
- **EN**: In the same way, when the mind isn’t corrupt, a good destiny is to be expected.

## mn7:3.1  `cck12.60, csp1ed9.44, csp2ed9.44, dr12.57, ms9M_177, msdiv71, nya3, pts-vp-pli1.37, sya12.65`
- **PLI**: Katame ca, bhikkhave, cittassa upakkilesā?
- **EN**: And what are the corruptions of the mind?
- **NOTE**: Here the text shifts from <i lang='pi' translate='no'>saṅkiliṭṭha</i> (“corrupt”) to <i lang='pi' translate='no'>upakkilesa</i> (“corruption”). These are general terms for unskillful qualities of mind and the change in prefix has no particular significence.

## mn7:3.2
- **PLI**: Abhijjhāvisamalobho cittassa upakkileso, byāpādo cittassa upakkileso, kodho cittassa upakkileso, upanāho cittassa upakkileso, makkho cittassa upakkileso, paḷāso cittassa upakkileso, issā cittassa upakkileso, macchariyaṁ cittassa upakkileso, māyā cittassa upakkileso, sāṭheyyaṁ cittassa upakkileso, thambho cittassa upakkileso, sārambho cittassa upakkileso, māno cittassa upakkileso, atimāno cittassa upakkileso, mado cittassa upakkileso, pamādo cittassa upakkileso.
- **EN**: Covetousness and immoral greed, ill will, anger, acrimony, disdain, contempt, jealousy, stinginess, deceit, deviousness, obstinacy, aggression, conceit, arrogance, vanity, and negligence are corruptions of the mind.
