## mn69:17.1  `csp1ed10.137, csp2ed10.137, dr13.200, mc2.149, ms10M_482, nya17`
- **PLI**: Āraññikenāvuso, bhikkhunā abhidhamme abhivinaye yogo karaṇīyo.
- **EN**: A wilderness monk should make an effort in regards to the teaching and training.
- **NOTE**: In the suttas, <i lang='pi' translate='no'>abhivinaya</i> means “about the training” just as <i lang='pi' translate='no'>abhidhamma</i> means “about the teaching” (eg. <a href='https://suttacentral.net/an3.140/en/sujato#4.4'>AN 3.140:4.4</a>). Over time, such discussions “about” the fundamentals came to be thought of as a separate body of analytical texts called <i lang='pi' translate='no'>abhidhamma</i> and <i lang='pi' translate='no'>abhivinaya</i>. Thus in the late canonical Parivāra, compiled in Sri Lanka, the analysis (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>vibhatti</i>) of a Vinaya rule is called <i lang='pi' translate='no'>abhivinaya</i> (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-pvr1.1/en/sujato#3.33'>Pvr 1.1:3.33</a>).

## mn69:17.2
- **PLI**: Santāvuso, āraññikaṁ bhikkhuṁ abhidhamme abhivinaye pañhaṁ pucchitāro.
- **EN**: There are those who will question a wilderness monk about the teaching and training.

## mn69:17.4
- **PLI**: ‘Kiṁ panimassāyasmato āraññikassa ekassāraññe serivihārena yo ayamāyasmā abhidhamme abhivinaye pañhaṁ puṭṭho na sampāyatī’ti—
- **EN**: ‘What’s the point of this wilderness venerable’s staying alone and autonomous in the wilderness, since he is stumped by a question about the teaching and training?’

## mn69:17.5
- **PLI**: tassa bhavanti vattāro.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)

## mn69:17.6
- **PLI**: Tasmā āraññikena bhikkhunā abhidhamme abhivinaye yogo karaṇīyo.

## mn69:19.1  `dr13.201, ms10M_484, nya19`
- **PLI**: Āraññikenāvuso, bhikkhunā uttari manussadhamme yogo karaṇīyo.
- **EN**: A wilderness monk should practice meditation to realize the superhuman state.

## mn69:19.2
- **PLI**: Santāvuso, āraññikaṁ bhikkhuṁ uttari manussadhamme pañhaṁ pucchitāro.
- **EN**: There are those who will question a wilderness monk about the superhuman state.

## mn69:19.3
- **PLI**: Sace, āvuso, āraññiko bhikkhu uttari manussadhamme pañhaṁ puṭṭho na sampāyati, tassa bhavanti vattāro.
- **EN**: If he is stumped, there’ll be some who say:

## mn69:19.4
- **PLI**: ‘Kiṁ panimassāyasmato āraññikassa ekassāraññe serivihārena yo ayamāyasmā yassatthāya pabbajito tamatthaṁ na jānātī’ti—
- **EN**: ‘What’s the point of this wilderness venerable’s staying alone and autonomous in the wilderness, since he doesn’t know the goal for which he went forth?’
- **NOTE**: Normally the “superhuman states” are in plural as they include absorptions, various advanced meditative abilities, and the stages of realization. Unusually, the form is singular here, denoting the “goal for which one goes forth”, namely arahantship. This point is confirmed in the Chinese parallel, which speaks of the ending of defilements (MA 26 at T i 456a3).

## mn69:19.5
- **PLI**: tassa bhavanti vattāro.
- **EN**: (Sujato gộp đoạn này vào câu trước — dịch trực tiếp từ PLI)

## mn69:19.6
- **PLI**: Tasmā āraññikena bhikkhunā uttari manussadhamme yogo karaṇīyo”ti.
- **EN**: That’s why a wilderness monk should practice meditation to realize the superhuman state.”
