# Phần 3  (2 đoạn)

## mn69:19.9
- **PLI**: “Āraññikenāpi kho, āvuso moggallāna, bhikkhunā ime dhammā samādāya vattitabbā pageva gāmantavihārinā”ti.
- **EN**: “Reverend Moggallāna, these things should be undertaken and followed by wilderness monks, and still more by those who live within a village.”
- **NOTE**: Those who live in a village are closer to temptations and need to be even more on their guard.
