## mn65:2.1  `csp1ed10.101, csp2ed10.101, ms10M_361, nya2`
- **PLI**: “Ahaṁ kho, bhikkhave, ekāsanabhojanaṁ bhuñjāmi;
- **EN**: “Mendicants, I eat my food in one sitting per day.
- **NOTE**: The practice of eating one meal a day is encouraged but not required by Vinaya. At <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn21/en/sujato#7.4'>MN 21:7.4</a> the Buddha reminisced about the days when he merely needed to mention it and monks would follow suit. | In the related story at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn70/en/sujato#1.3'>MN 70:1.3</a>, the Buddha encouraged not eating at night, to which monks also objected, occasioning the laying down of a Vinaya rule (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/pli-tv-bu-vb-pc37/en/sujato'>Bu Pc 37</a>), although at least some of the monks later came to regret their objections, recognizing that the Buddha had acted for their welfare (<a href='https://suttacentral.net/mn66/en/sujato#6.4'>MN 66:6.4</a>).

## mn65:2.2
- **PLI**: ekāsanabhojanaṁ kho, ahaṁ, bhikkhave, bhuñjamāno appābādhatañca sañjānāmi appātaṅkatañca lahuṭṭhānañca balañca phāsuvihārañca.
- **EN**: Doing so, I find that I’m healthy and well, nimble, strong, and living comfortably.

## mn65:2.3
- **PLI**: Etha, tumhepi, bhikkhave, ekāsanabhojanaṁ bhuñjatha;
- **EN**: You too should eat your food in one sitting per day.

## mn65:2.4
- **PLI**: ekāsanabhojanaṁ kho, bhikkhave, tumhepi bhuñjamānā appābādhatañca sañjānissatha appātaṅkatañca lahuṭṭhānañca balañca phāsuvihārañcā”ti.
- **EN**: Doing so, you’ll find that you’re healthy and well, nimble, strong, and living comfortably.”

## mn65:3.1  `cck13.169, ms10M_362, nya3, pts-vp-pli1.438, sya13.164`
- **PLI**: Evaṁ vutte, āyasmā bhaddāli bhagavantaṁ etadavoca:
- **EN**: When he said this, Venerable Bhaddāli said to the Buddha,
- **NOTE**: Bhaddāli does not appear elsewhere in early texts.

## mn65:3.2
- **PLI**: “ahaṁ kho, bhante, na ussahāmi ekāsanabhojanaṁ bhuñjituṁ;
- **EN**: “Sir, I’m not going to attempt to eat my food in one sitting per day.

## mn65:3.3
- **PLI**: ekāsanabhojanañhi me, bhante, bhuñjato siyā kukkuccaṁ, siyā vippaṭisāro”ti.
- **EN**: For when eating once a day I might feel remorse and regret.”
- **NOTE**: The commentary says that he feared he would not be able to sustain his monkhood. Chinese parallels at this point indicate that he feared discomfort (EA 49.7 at T ii 800b29, T 1425 at T xxii 359b14).

## mn65:4.1  `nya4`
- **PLI**: “Tena hi tvaṁ, bhaddāli, yattha nimantito assasi tattha ekadesaṁ bhuñjitvā ekadesaṁ nīharitvāpi bhuñjeyyāsi.
- **EN**: “Well then, Bhaddāli, eat one part of the meal in the place where you’re invited, and bring the rest back to eat.

## mn65:4.2
- **PLI**: Evampi kho tvaṁ, bhaddāli, bhuñjamāno ekāsano yāpessasī”ti.
- **EN**: Eating this way, too, you will sustain yourself.”
- **NOTE**: <i lang='pi' translate='no'>Ekāsano</i> here in Mahāsaṅgīti edition appears superfluous, as it is missing in most editions.

## mn65:4.3
- **PLI**: “Evampi kho ahaṁ, bhante, na ussahāmi bhuñjituṁ;
- **EN**: “Sir, I’m not going to attempt to eat that way, either.

## mn65:4.4
- **PLI**: evampi hi me, bhante, bhuñjato siyā kukkuccaṁ, siyā vippaṭisāro”ti.
- **EN**: For when eating that way I might also feel remorse and regret.”

## mn65:4.5
- **PLI**: Atha kho āyasmā bhaddāli bhagavatā sikkhāpade paññāpiyamāne bhikkhusaṅghe sikkhaṁ samādiyamāne anussāhaṁ pavedesi.
- **EN**: Then, as this rule was being laid down by the Buddha and the Saṅgha was undertaking it, Bhaddāli announced he would not attempt to keep it.

## mn65:4.6
- **PLI**: Atha kho āyasmā bhaddāli sabbaṁ taṁ temāsaṁ na bhagavato sammukhībhāvaṁ adāsi, yathā taṁ satthusāsane sikkhāya aparipūrakārī.
- **EN**: Then for the whole of that three months Bhaddāli would not see the Buddha face to face, as happens when someone doesn’t fulfill the training according to the Teacher’s instructions.
- **NOTE**: “Would not see the Buddha face to face” is a loose rendering of <i lang='pi' translate='no'>na bhagavato sammukhībhāvaṁ adāsi</i>. The idiom can be captured more literally at <a href='https://suttacentral.net/dn3/en/sujato#2.6.2'>DN 3:2.6.2</a>; when King Pasenadi meets Pokkharasāti, he does not “grant him an audience face to face” (<i lang='pi' translate='no'>tassa … sammukhībhāvampi na dadāti</i>) but only behind a curtain.

## mn65:7.1  `nya7`
- **PLI**: “Evamāvuso”ti kho āyasmā bhaddāli tesaṁ bhikkhūnaṁ paṭissutvā yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavantaṁ abhivādetvā ekamantaṁ nisīdi. Ekamantaṁ nisinno kho āyasmā bhaddāli bhagavantaṁ etadavoca:

## mn65:7.2
- **PLI**: “accayo maṁ, bhante, accagamā yathābālaṁ yathāmūḷhaṁ yathāakusalaṁ, yohaṁ bhagavatā sikkhāpade paññāpiyamāne bhikkhusaṅghe sikkhaṁ samādiyamāne anussāhaṁ pavedesiṁ.
- **EN**: “I made a mistake, sir. It was foolish, stupid, and unskillful of me that, as this rule was being laid down by the Buddha and the Saṅgha was undertaking it, I announced I would not attempt to keep it.

## mn65:7.3
- **PLI**: Tassa me, bhante, bhagavā accayaṁ accayato paṭiggaṇhātu āyatiṁ saṁvarāyā”ti.
- **EN**: Please, sir, accept my mistake for what it is, so I will restrain myself in future.”

## mn65:8.1  `cck13.171, csp1ed10.102, csp2ed10.102, dr13.151, mc2.112, ms10M_364, ndp10.122, nya8, pts-vp-pli1.439, sya13.165, sya13.166`
